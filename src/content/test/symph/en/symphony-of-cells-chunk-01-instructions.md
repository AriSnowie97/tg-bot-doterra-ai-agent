## 2. Basic Rules for Essential Oils

- If the oil is labeled **S** (sensitive) or **D** (dilute) — always apply a base oil to the skin before use.
- Allow the oil to drop from the bottle without letting the dispenser touch the skin.
- Avoid getting oil in the eyes or ears.
- Do not add water to essential oils unless you want to intensify their effect — to soothe unwanted effects, use a fatty base oil.
- Store bottles away from direct sunlight and heat.
- Use oils internally only if the bottle is marked "Supplement Facts".

## 3. Preparing for the Protocol

- Lay out all the necessary oils in an easily accessible place.
- Prepare the application area (massage table, couch, yoga mat, etc.).
- Unless otherwise specified, place the person face down with their back and feet exposed.
- Allow the person to reach a calm and relaxed state.

## 4. Performing Each Protocol (Step-by-Step)

1. Follow the steps guided by the symbols — they show where to apply the oil. Some oils in the Symphony of the Cells™ system come in rollers and are applied differently than drops from a bottle. If there is a hand icon — apply the oil to your palm first.
2. For intense oils (clove, oregano, thyme), the "Dilute" icon is a precautionary measure for sensitive people.
3. Apply the oils step-by-step in clockwise circular motions from the lower back to the base of the skull.
4. Follow the individual instructions and symbols for each step — this may include holding the oil under the person's nose for a deep breath or applying it to the heart area, etc.
5. When the corresponding icon is present, apply any remaining oil from your hands to the feet.
6. Complete all the steps listed for the specific protocol in order.

## 5. Self-Application of Oils

- Symphony of the Cells™ is suitable for children, loved ones, clients, friends, and family — but you can also perform it on yourself as needed.
- Apply to areas you can reach: start at the lower back, work your way up, across the shoulders, down the spine, and then to the base of the skull.
- If you cannot reach the lower back — apply to the upper back, and vice versa.
- Finish each step by applying the remaining oil from your hands to the soles of the feet.
- Optionally, finish with a hot compress.
- If you cannot reach your back at all — apply only to the feet.
- The goal of Symphony of the Cells is an easy and simple application of oils to the body.

## 6. Frequency and Amount

- **Prevention** (for relatively healthy people): 2–3 times a month.
- **For health improvement**: 2–3 times a week for 1–2 weeks or as needed, then return to the maintenance routine (2–3 times a month).
- **Children and people over 50**: reduce the number of drops from 4–6 to 2–3 drops per oil.
- Remember to apply the base oil at the beginning.
