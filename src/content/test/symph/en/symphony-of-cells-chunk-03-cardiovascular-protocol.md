## Protocol Steps

### Step 1 — Frankincense
Inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Alternates:** Balance / Immortelle (Salubelle) / Pink Pepper / Fennel

### Step 2 — Carrier Oil (Fractionated Coconut Oil)
Apply a layer of oil to the entire back.
**Alternates:** olive oil, coconut oil, sesame oil, almond oil, etc.

### Step 3 — Thyme
Apply to back, feet.
**Alternates:** Oregano / Copaiba / Clove

### Step 4 — Massage Blend: AromaTouch
Apply to back, feet.
**Alternates:** Basil / Cypress / Marjoram

### Step 5 — Turmeric and Lemongrass
Apply to back, feet.
**Alternates for Turmeric:** Ginger / Cinnamon / Cardamom
**Alternates for Lemongrass:** Helichrysum / Black Pepper / Dill

### Step 6 — Protective Blend / On Guard
Apply to back, feet.
**Alternates:** Cinnamon / Clove / Copaiba

### Step 7 — Marjoram
Apply to back, feet.
**Alternates:** Basil / Cypress / AromaTouch

### Step 8 — Basil
Apply to back, feet.
**Alternates:** AromaTouch / Marjoram / Coriander

### Step 9 — Lavender
Apply to back, feet.
**Alternates:** Petitgrain / Roman Chamomile / Coriander

### Step 10 — Peppermint
Apply to back, feet.
**Alternates:** Deep Blue / Spearmint / DigestZen (ZenGest)

## Warming (Optional)

Warming can be added to the protocol: in the shower, bath, sauna, or using a hot towel. Warming enhances the effect of the protocol.

> Even if you are missing an oil, still perform the protocol. It is better than nothing.
