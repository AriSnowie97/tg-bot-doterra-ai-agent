# How to Support the Gut Microbiome (chunk 29)

## Three Directions of Gut Recovery

1. Eliminate foods that trigger inflammatory processes in the gut
2. Establish a healthy eating pattern
3. Minimize stress

## What to Remove from the Diet

- Unbalanced diet (excess of refined products)
- Allergens and foods that can cause intolerances (gluten, sugar, dairy products, etc.)
- Preservatives, synthetic flavor enhancers, additives (monosodium glutamate, sulfites)
- Toxins (benzoates, tartrazine)
- Medications that disrupt the gut microbiome (antibiotics, antacids, NSAIDs, hormonal drugs) — *do not stop taking prescribed medications on your own*
- Stress

## General Principles of Nutrition

- Variety in the diet, more fiber.
- **The "80/20 plate" principle:** 80% of the plate — greens and vegetables (raw, steamed, baked, stewed), 20% — proteins and healthy fats (meat, fish, avocado, etc.).
- Adequate fluid intake throughout the day.
- Thorough chewing, do not wash down food with liquids.
- Mindful eating, food diary.

## Beverages to Stimulate the Gallbladder

**Moderate:**
- Chicory
- Mint tea
- Rosehip decoction

**Strong choleretics:**
- Beetroot juice
- Black radish juice
- Fresh cabbage juice

ℹ️ **Note:** the material is of a general educational wellness nature. This is not medical advice — the elimination of entire food groups and especially any change in taking prescribed medications (antibiotics, hormonal drugs, etc.) must be agreed upon with a doctor, rather than carried out independently based on this chunk. Strong choleretic juices are contraindicated in gallstone disease — a doctor's consultation is required here as well.
