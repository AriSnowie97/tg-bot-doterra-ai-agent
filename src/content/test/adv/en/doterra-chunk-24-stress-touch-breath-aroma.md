# The Body Remembers Everything: Stress Relief Through Touch, Breathing, and Aroma (Chunk 24)

## Why Stress Lives in the Body (Physiology)

Chronic (background, accumulated) stress differs from acute stress. The body records every suppressed stress and every unexperienced emotion in the muscles, fascia, and breathing patterns.

Under stress, the brain activates the sympathetic nervous system ("fight or flight"): cortisol and adrenaline are released into the bloodstream, and muscles contract. The problem for modern humans is that the response often does not complete: the muscle tensed up and did not relax. This is how **muscle blocks** are formed — areas of chronic contraction with impaired blood circulation, toxin accumulation, and pain.

**Most common block areas:**
- Neck and trapezius muscle — "the burden of responsibility"
- Lower back — fear, insecurity
- Chest and diaphragm — suppressed emotions, grief
- Pelvis and hips — accumulated stress, traumatic experience
- Jaw (TMJ) — control, suppressed aggression, or anxiety

To "dissolve" blocks, a comprehensive approach is needed: from the outside (massage, touch) and from the inside (breathing, meditation, awareness).

## Massage with Essential Oils

Touch activates the vagus nerve, lowers cortisol, and increases oxytocin and serotonin. Just 20 minutes of massage switch the nervous system from sympathetic to parasympathetic mode.

**Dilution:** 2–3 drops of essential oil per 5 ml of carrier oil (e.g., doTERRA Fractionated Coconut Oil).

**Oils for massage:**
- **Lavender** — lowers cortisol, relaxes muscles; for massage of the neck, shoulders, temples.
- **Vetiver** — the oil of "grounding"; for massage of feet and lower legs; during anxious states and "racing thoughts".
- **Frankincense** — calms the limbic system; solar plexus massage to work with emotional blocks.
- **Serenity (Restful Blend)** — a blend for deep relaxation (lavender, chamomile, sandalwood, ylang-ylang); for massaging the spine, lower back, and feet in the evening.
- **Myrrh** — for releasing emotional blocks, paired with frankincense.
- **Roman Chamomile** — relaxes the nervous system, relieves internal irritation; massage of the abdomen and solar plexus.
- **Balance (Grounding Blend)** — a woody-herbal blend for grounding; massage along the spine.

**Practice — Morning Foot Self-Massage (5 min):**
2 drops of Vetiver + 1 drop of Lavender in coconut oil. Massage each foot for 2.5 minutes, paying attention to the heel (safety zone) and the pads of the toes. Intention: "I am returning to my body. I am safe."

**Practice — Evening Neck and Shoulder Massage:**
2 drops of Serenity + 1 drop of Frankincense in carrier oil. Slow circular movements from the base of the skull to the shoulders, breathing deeply.

## Breathing: The Fastest Entry to Relaxation

- **4-7-8 Technique** (Dr. Andrew Weil): inhale 4 sec → hold 7 sec → exhale 8 sec. Three cycles before sleep or during acute stress.
- **Diaphragmatic Breathing:** one hand on the chest, the other on the abdomen; breathe so that only the abdomen moves — activates the vagus nerve.
- **Box Breathing:** inhale 4 → hold 4 → exhale 4 → hold 4.

**Oils for breathing practices** (1 drop on palms, rub together, inhale from cupped hands):
- **Peppermint** — opens airways, tones attention.
- **Eucalyptus** — expands the bronchi, facilitates breathing.
- **Breathe (Respiratory Blend)** — peppermint, eucalyptus, tea tree, cedarwood; for breathing meditations.
- **Bergamot** — relieves anxiety, helps to "exhale" tension.

## Meditation: Meeting Yourself

Regular practice reduces the activity of the amygdala (anxiety center) and thickens the prefrontal cortex (calmness center).

- **Body Scan, 10–15 min:** lie down, close your eyes, slowly move your attention from the feet to the crown of the head, noticing sensations without judgment.
- **Meditation with a Mantra/Intention:** repeat a simple statement ("I am relaxed and safe", "letting go", "thank you") on the exhale.
- **Candle Flame Meditation:** a candle made of natural wax + oil in a diffuser, look at the flame for 5–7 min.

**Oils for meditation:**
- **Frankincense** — slows down breathing, expands consciousness.
- **Vetiver** — grounds when thoughts scatter.
- **Sandalwood** — supports focus and inner calm.
- **Balance** — harmonizes at the start of practice.
- **AromaTouch (Massage Blend)** — on the wrists before meditation, for a sense of safety.
- **Console (Comforting Blend)** — for meditations aimed at processing grief and loss.

## Relaxation at Home

**Bath with oils:** 5–7 drops of oils + sea salt (Epsom salt) or honey/milk/cream (emulsifier), into a bath at 37–38°C for 20–30 min.

**Bath recipes:**
- Anti-stress: 3 drops Lavender + 2 drops Frankincense + 2 drops Vetiver
- Restorative: 3 drops Serenity + 2 drops Roman Chamomile + 2 drops Ylang Ylang
- Grounding: 3 drops Balance + 2 drops Sandalwood + 2 drops Bergamot

**Diffusion for evening relaxation** (30–40 min before sleep):
- Serenity + Frankincense
- Adaptive + Copaiba
- Vetiver + Bergamot + Ylang Ylang
- Balance + Sandalwood
- Patchouli + Frankincense

## Body Therapy and Releasing Blocks

- **Shaking (Neurogenic Tremoring / TRE),** Dr. David Berceli's method: lie on a mat, bend knees, feet closer to the pelvis, allow legs to shake gently without control, 15–20 min.
- **Working with the diaphragm:** lying on your back, hands on lower ribs; on inhale the ribs expand; on exhale — sound "haaa"/"aaa".
- **Acupressure:** point between the eyebrows (Yintang) — anxiety and brain fatigue, press for 30–60 sec; point on the wrist (Pericardium 6, "Neiguan") — stress-induced nausea and anxiety.
- **Yoga Nidra:** 30–45 min of a state between sleep and wakefulness, with audio guidance.

**Oils for working with blocks:**
- **Vetiver** — on the soles of the feet and along the spine.
- **Frankincense** — on the sternum and solar plexus.
- **Console (Comforting Blend)** — for working with grief and loss.
- **Forgive (Renewing Blend)** — for working with resentment and self-blame; on the heart center and solar plexus.
- **Geranium** — restores balance, hormonal and emotional state.

## Step-by-Step Evening Recovery (45–60 min)

1. **19:00–20:00 — End of the day.** Diffuser: Adaptive or Serenity (3 drops).
2. **Bath or shower (20 min).** 2 drops of Lavender + 2 drops of Frankincense + sea salt; or Frankincense/Rose on the heart area and wrists after the shower.
3. **Self-massage (5–7 min).** 1 drop of Vetiver + 1 drop of Balance in coconut oil; massage of feet and calves from bottom to top.
4. **Breathing practice (5 min).** 4-7-8 technique, 5 cycles, with your favorite aroma on the palms.
5. **Meditation / Body Scan (10–15 min).** Body scan from feet to head without judgment.
6. **Intention before sleep.** Hand on heart: "I did everything I could today. I am safe. I let go of this day."

> Important: not isolated practices, but a system. Consistency is more important than intensity — start small (10 min every evening, one diffuser, one oil).
