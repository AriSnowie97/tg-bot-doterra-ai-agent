# Body Care — Fractionated Coconut Oil

**Category:** Body Care

**Type:** base (carrier) body oil

**Link:** https://www.doterra.com/US/en/p/usage-fractionated-coconut-oil-carrier-oil

---

## Short Description

👑 Fractionated Coconut Oil is a base oil for diluting essential oils and body care
👑 Lightweight, colorless, and odorless; completely miscible with all doTERRA essential oils
👑 Leaves no greasy residue and does not clog pores

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride — the main component obtained by fractionating coconut oil
▫️ 100% plant-based origin (Cocos nucifera)

## Indications

🔹 When and what to use for: carrier oil for dilution (reduces skin sensitivity when applying concentrated essential oils), moisturizing (softens dry skin on hands, feet, elbows), massage (ideal texture for massage blends with essential oils), long shelf life (does not solidify and keeps almost indefinitely)

## Directions for Use

🔹 For dilution: 1 part essential oil to 5 or more parts Fractionated Coconut Oil — depending on skin sensitivity
🔹 Topically: apply to the skin for moisturization without interacting with the aroma of other blends
🔹 For massage: mix with favorite essential oils and apply to the neck and shoulders

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 A universal base component for almost all topical doTERRA essential oil recipes
🔹 Frequently used together with Deep Blue, AromaTouch, and other massage blends

## Interesting Facts

👑 Fractionation removes the long-chain triglycerides that cause regular coconut oil to solidify at room temperature
👑 The fractionation process also increases the proportion of caprylic and capric acids, which are beneficial for skin health
👑 Main chemical component — Caprylic/capric triglyceride

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
