# Blue Lotus Touch

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Single Oil, Touch (10 ml roll-on)

**Link:** https://www.doterra.com/US/en/blue-lotus-touch

---

## Brief Description

👑 A rare floral lotus extract, known in Egyptian culture and art
👑 A soothing, slightly sweet aroma for meditation and massage
👑 A natural skin moisturizer thanks to squalane

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Fractionated Coconut Oil
▫️ Blue Lotus Flower Extract (Nymphaea caerulea)

## Indications

🔹 For meditation, massage, and regular skin moisturization

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply to wrists and neck for a soothing aroma
🔹 Use during meditation or yoga
🔹 Apply as part of your morning or evening skin care routine

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Blends well with Jasmine Touch, Magnolia Touch, Lavender Touch to create a personal fragrance

## Fun Facts

👑 Blue lotus flowers were placed on the body of Pharaoh Tutankhamun before burial
👑 Obtained by solvent extraction method (absolute) rather than steam distillation

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
