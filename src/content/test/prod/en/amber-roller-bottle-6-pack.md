## Brief Description

👑 Set of six 10 mL amber glass roller bottles for custom essential oil blends
👑 The same glass and metal roller used in doTERRA Touch bottles
👑 Ideal for blending, storing, and personal application of essential oils

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Amber glass — protects essential oils from sunlight
▫️ Stainless steel roller — even application without wasting oil
▫️ 10 mL volume — convenient compact size for a bag or pocket

## Indications

🔹 Personal blends: allow you to create your own combinations of essential oils with a carrier oil
🔹 Convenience of use: the roller provides targeted application without direct hand contact with the oil
🔹 Portability: compact size is suitable for travel and everyday carry
🔹 Economy: a diluted blend in a roller bottle is consumed more slowly than oil from a dropper

## Directions for Use

🔹 Fill the bottle approximately three-quarters full with a carrier oil (e.g., Fractionated Coconut Oil)
🔹 Add selected essential oils (typically 10-15 drops per 10 mL of carrier oil)
🔹 Secure the roller and cap, flip several times to mix
🔹 Apply to desired areas of the skin — wrists, temples, neck

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Frequently used with Fractionated Coconut Oil as a carrier
🔹 Suitable for any doTERRA essential oils and blends — Deep Blue, AromaTouch, custom recipes

## Interesting Facts

👑 Amber glass color is traditionally used for storing essential oils as it blocks ultraviolet rays
👑 The exact same type of bottle and roller is used in pre-made products of the doTERRA Touch line
👑 Roller bottles are one of the most popular accessories among users who create their own recipes

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.

## Additional Information

🔹 Thoroughly dilute concentrated essential oils with a carrier oil before applying to the skin
🔹 Label bottles for convenience and safety
🔹 Wash and dry bottles before reusing them with a new blend

> ⚠️ This information is for informational purposes only. Essential oil dilution depends on skin type and the specific oil — consult a specialist if necessary.
