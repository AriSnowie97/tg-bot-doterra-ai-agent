## Brief Description

👑 Foaming hand wash concentrate with Citrus Bloom scent
👑 Gently cleanses without drying the skin
👑 Includes a reusable glass dispenser with a pump

## Ingredients and Key Components

▫️ Natural surfactants
▫️ Banana fruit extract — for skin hydration and softening
▫️ Citrus Bloom® Springtime Blend (essential oils)
▫️ Free of parabens, phthalates, phosphates, artificial dyes, and synthetic fragrances

## Indications

🔹 Daily hand washing at home
🔹 Need for gentle cleansing without drying the skin

## Directions for Use

🔹 Pour the concentrate into the abōde Hand Wash Dispenser up to the fill line
🔹 Add purified water up to the fill line (recommended dilution is 1 part concentrate to 3 parts water)
🔹 Attach the pump and gently shake
🔹 Use as needed for hand washing

👑 One bottle of concentrate refills the 16 oz dispenser approximately twice

## Cautions

> ⚠️ For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes and broken skin. Store in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight and excessive heat.
