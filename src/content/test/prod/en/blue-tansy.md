# Blue Tansy

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Essential Oil (Single, 5 mL)

**Link:** https://www.doterra.com/US/en/blog/product-spotlight-using-blue-tansy

---

## Brief Description

👑 Rich blue oil for a restorative massage
👑 Soothes skin irritations and redness
👑 Soft, sweet, herbaceous aroma

## Ingredients & Key Components

▫️ Tanacetum annuum (Blue Tansy) essential oil

## Indications

🔹 Recovery after physical exertion
🔹 Care for sensitive or irritated skin

## Directions for Use

🔹 Topically: dilute with a carrier oil for massage (may stain skin/fabrics — dilute with care)

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Peace® Touch — contains Blue Tansy in its blend

## Fun Facts

👑 The rich blue color comes from chamazulene, which is formed during distillation
👑 Sold in a 5 mL bottle due to the high concentration of active compounds

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
