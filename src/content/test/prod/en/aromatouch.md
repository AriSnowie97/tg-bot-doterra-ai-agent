# AromaTouch — Massage Blend

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Massage Blend

**Link:** https://www.doterra.com/US/en/p/aromatouch-massage-blend-oil

---

## Brief Description

👑 Proprietary massage blend developed specifically to complement the AromaTouch Technique
👑 Fresh, clean, minty, and powdery aroma combining Cypress, Peppermint, Marjoram, Basil, Grapefruit, and Lavender
👑 Calms and softens the skin, making any massage more comfortable

## Ingredients and Key Components

▫️ Cypress (Cupressus sempervirens) — a tall evergreen tree native to southern Europe and western Asia, rich in skin-beneficial monoterpenes
▫️ Peppermint (Mentha piperita) — a hybrid of watermint and spearmint, first described by Carl Linnaeus in 1753
▫️ Basil (Ocimum basilicum) — a member of the mint family, traditionally used in both cooking and aromatherapy
▫️ Marjoram (Origanum majorana)
▫️ Grapefruit (Citrus paradisi)
▫️ Lavender (Lavandula angustifolia)

## Indications

🔹 Muscle tension: helps reduce feelings of tension in muscles during and after a massage
🔹 Stressful situations: application before an important event helps calm the nerves
🔹 At-home spa ritual: creates a professional spa atmosphere without visiting a salon
🔹 Skin care during massage: calms and softens the skin thanks to the combination of Cypress and Lavender
🔹 Emotional connection: the AromaTouch Hand Technique strengthens emotional bonds between people through touch

## Directions for Use

🔹 Add 3–4 drops to a diffuser to create a spa atmosphere at home
🔹 Inhale directly before a massage to set a relaxing mood
🔹 Apply to the neck and shoulders to release tension
🔹 Dilute with a carrier oil (5 drops per 10 ml) for a full-body massage
🔹 Use in the AromaTouch Hand Technique for hand massage of a loved one
🔹 Add to Epsom salt and dissolve in a hot bath for relaxation

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Lavender (for a spa atmosphere)
🔹 Peppermint and Eucalyptus (post-workout)
🔹 Vetiver (for evening relaxation)
🔹 Bergamot (before an important event)
🔹 Used in the AromaTouch Training Kit and Refill Kit for technique training

## Fun Facts

👑 AromaTouch is the central oil in the signature technique by Dr. Hill, which combines touch and aromatherapy
👑 α-Pinene, one of the main components of Cypress, helps reduce the appearance of skin imperfections
👑 The high menthol content in Peppermint creates an invigorating, refreshing effect during physical activity
👑 The fresh minty-powdery aroma is a recognizable feature that distinguishes AromaTouch from other doTERRA massage blends

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas. Dilute with a carrier oil before massage to minimize skin sensitivity. Do not apply to broken or irritated skin.
