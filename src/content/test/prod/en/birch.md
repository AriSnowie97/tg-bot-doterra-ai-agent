# Birch

**Category:** Essential oils

**Type:** Essential oil (single, 15 mL)

**Link:** https://www.doterra.com/US/en/p/birch-oil

---

## Brief description

👑 Rare wood oil with a cooling effect for massage
👑 Supports a healthy skin appearance when applied topically
👑 Powerful, minty-spicy aroma similar to Wintergreen

## Composition and key ingredients

▫️ Betula lenta (black/sweet birch) essential oil

## Indications

🔹 Cooling massage for back and legs after a workout
🔹 Masking of undesirable odors
🔹 Skin care

## Directions for use

🔹 Diffusion: 3-4 drops (powerful aroma, 1-2 drops is sufficient)
🔹 Topically: dilute with a carrier oil

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products

🔹 Wintergreen — similar aromatic profile, but different plants

## Fun facts

👑 doTERRA is the first company to establish a sustainable commercial release of 100% pure Birch oil
👑 To produce 1 quart of oil, about 3 tons of bark are required

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
