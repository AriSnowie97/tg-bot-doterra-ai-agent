## How to Use the Diagram

1. Identify the condition you are experiencing.
2. Determine which body system your condition is associated with (if unsure, refer to the Disease Map, i.e., the lists of conditions in protocol chunks 1–21).
3. Select an SOC (Symphony of the Cells) protocol to use based on the body system (see the "Body Systems" section in each protocol chunk).
4. Use essential oils, oil blends, and dietary supplements for internal consumption to support the affected body system.
5. It is recommended to use daily (according to package instructions): Health and Longevity Complex, Digestive Enzyme Complex, Detoxification Capsules, as well as drink water with a few drops of any citrus oil added.

ℹ️ **Note:** items 4–5 refer to the internal use of oils and supplements — reminding you of the safety rule from chunk 1: oils for internal use are permissible only if labeled with "Supplement Facts" on the bottle.
