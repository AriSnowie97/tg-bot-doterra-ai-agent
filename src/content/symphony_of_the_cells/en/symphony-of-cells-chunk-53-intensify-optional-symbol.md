# Symphony of the Cells™ — "Enhancement (optional)" Symbol 

![Symphony of the Cells](/images/strongest.jpg)

## Symbol Description

A drop icon with a thermometer and an "Opt" label (gray hexagon) — "Enhancement (optional)".

**Essence:** the effect of essential oils can be enhanced by adding water. This helps flush oils from the body, creates heat, and, accordingly, causes more movement (reactions) in the body.

**Ways to add water/heat:**
- take a shower
- take a bath
- use a hot compress
- go to a sauna

**Important:** oils are not washed away by water — their effect is only enhanced.

**Recommendation at this step:** add an additional carrier oil to reduce the intensity of heat and moisture if the reaction seems too strong.

---
ℹ️ **Note:** aligns with the safety rule from chunk 1 ("do not add water to essential oils unless you want to enhance the effect; use a fatty carrier oil to soothe undesirable effects") — this symbol is specifically about the conscious, voluntary use of this effect, not an accidental enhancement. The step is marked as optional ("Opt") — not a mandatory element of the protocol.
