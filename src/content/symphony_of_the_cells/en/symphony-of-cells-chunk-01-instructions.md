## 2. Basic Essential Oil Rules

- If an oil is labeled **S** (sensitive) or **D** (dilute), be sure to apply carrier oil to the skin before use.
- Allow the oil to drop from the bottle without touching the dispenser to the skin.
- Avoid getting oils into eyes or ears.
- Do not add water to essential oils unless you want to intensify their action — to soothe undesirable effects, use a fatty carrier oil.
- Store bottles away from direct sunlight and heat.
- Use oils internally only if the bottle has a "Supplement Facts" label.

## 3. Preparation for the Protocol

- Set out all necessary oils in an easily accessible place.
- Prepare a location for application (massage table, sofa, yoga mat, etc.).
- Unless specified otherwise, have the person lie face down, exposing their back and feet.
- Allow the person to reach a calm, relaxed state.

## 4. Performing Each Protocol (Step-by-Step)

1. Follow the steps guided by the symbols — they indicate where to apply the oil. Some oils in the Symphony of the Cells™ system come in roll-ons and are applied differently than drops from a bottle. If there is a hand icon, apply the oil to your palm first.
2. For intense oils (clove, oregano, thyme), the "Dilute" icon is a safety precaution for sensitive individuals.
3. Apply oils step by step in circular clockwise motions from the lower back up to the base of the skull.
4. Follow individual instructions and symbols for each step — this may include holding the oil to the person's nose for a deep breath or applying it to the heart area, etc.
5. When the corresponding icon is present, apply any remaining oil on your hands to the feet.
6. Complete all steps indicated for the specific protocol in order.

## 5. Self-Application of Oils

- Symphony of the Cells™ is suitable for children, loved ones, clients, friends, and family — but can also be self-applied as needed.
- Apply wherever you can reach: start from the lower back, work upward, across the shoulders, down the spine, then to the base of the skull.
- If you cannot reach your lower back, apply to the upper back, and vice versa.
- Finish each step by applying any remaining oil on your hands to the soles of your feet.
- Finish with a hot compress if desired.
- If you cannot reach your back at all, apply only to the feet.
- The goal of Symphony of the Cells is easy and simple oil application onto the body.

## 6. Frequency and Quantity

- **Prevention** (for relatively healthy individuals): 2–3 times a month.
- **For health improvement**: 2–3 times a week for 1–2 weeks or as needed, then return to the preventive regimen (2–3 times a month).
- **Children and people over 50**: reduce the number of drops from 4–6 to 2–3 drops per oil.
- Remember to apply carrier oil at the beginning.
