## Protocol Steps

### Step 1 — Frankincense
Apply to the heart/chest area, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutions:** Balance / Immortelle (Salubelle) / Pink Pepper / Fennel

### Step 2 — Carrier Oil (Fractionated Coconut)
Apply a layer of oil over the entire back.
**Substitutions:** olive oil, coconut oil, sesame oil, almond oil, etc.

### Step 3 — Melissa
Apply to back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutions:** Black Pepper / Lemongrass / Copaiba

### Step 4 — Yarrow | Pom
Apply to back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutions:** Blue Tansy / Black Pepper / Melissa

### Step 5 — Black Spruce
Apply to back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutions:** Siberian Fir / Helichrysum / Arborvitae

### Step 6 — Bergamot
Apply to back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutions:** Grapefruit / Whisper / Lime

### Step 7 — Magnolia
Apply oil first to hands, then to the back of the person receiving the application. Apply oil to the back with hands, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutions:** Lavender / Petitgrain / Neroli

### Step 8 — Calming Blend: Adaptive
Apply to back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutions:** Neroli / Copaiba / Magnolia

### Step 9 — Cedarwood
Apply to back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutions:** Arborvitae / Patchouli / Vetiver

### Step 10 — Spearmint
Apply to back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutions:** Peppermint / Pink Pepper / Peace

## Heat Application (Optional)

Heat application can be added to the protocol: in the shower, bath, sauna, or using a hot towel. Heat enhances the effect of the protocol.

> Even if an item is missing, perform the protocol anyway. It is better than doing nothing.
