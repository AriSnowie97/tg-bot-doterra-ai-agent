# Symphony of the Cells™ — "Apply to Hands" Symbol (chunk 52)

![Symphony of the Cells](/images/hand.jpg)

## Symbol Description

Palm/hand icon (burgundy hexagon) — "Apply to hands".

**Purpose:** this step is about convenience of use.

**Explanation:** some oils required for certain applications come in roller bottles, but sometimes it is easier to apply the oil to the hand/hands first, and then to the back of the person receiving the application.

---
ℹ️ **Note:** clarification to the symbol legend from chunk 1, which already states "if you see the hand icon, apply the oil to the palm first"; this chunk explains why this is done — it is a matter of convenience, not a separate action on another part of the body.
