## Protocol Steps

### Step 1 — Frankincense
Apply to heart/chest area, inhale from hands, apply to feet.
**Substitutions:** Balance / Immortelle (Salubelle) / Pink Pepper / Fennel

### Step 2 — Carrier Oil (Fractionated Coconut Oil)
Apply a layer of oil to the entire back.
**Substitutions:** olive oil, coconut oil, sesame oil, almond oil, etc.

### Step 3 — Tea Tree
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutions:** Green Mandarin / Rosemary / Eucalyptus

### Step 4 — Wintergreen and Birch
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutions for Wintergreen:** Birch / DeepBlue / Siberian Fir
**Substitutions for Birch:** Wintergreen / DeepBlue / Cypress

### Step 5 — Massage Blend: AromaTouch
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutions:** Basil / Cypress / Marjoram

### Step 6 — Lemongrass
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutions:** Helichrysum / Black Pepper / Dill

### Step 7 — Siberian Fir
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutions:** Douglas Fir / White Fir / Cedarwood

### Step 8 — Helichrysum
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutions:** DeepBlue / Immortelle / Myrrh

### Step 9 — Soothing Blend: Deep Blue
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutions:** Helichrysum / Peppermint / Wintergreen

### Step 10 — Peppermint
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutions:** DeepBlue / Spearmint / DigestZen (ZenGest)

## Heat Application (Optional)

Heat application can be added to the protocol: in the shower, bath, sauna, or using a hot towel. Heat application enhances the effect of the protocol.

> Even if an item is missing, perform the protocol anyway. It is better than nothing.
