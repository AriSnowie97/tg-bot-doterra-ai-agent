## Protocol Steps

### Step 1 — Frankincense
Apply to the heart/chest area, back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutions:** Balance / Immortelle (Salubelle) / Pink Pepper / Fennel

### Step 2 — Carrier Oil (Fractionated Coconut)
Apply a layer of oil over the entire back.
**Substitutions:** olive oil, coconut oil, sesame oil, almond oil, etc.

### Step 3 — Rosemary and Thyme
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutions for Rosemary:** Tea Tree / Neroli / Eucalyptus
**Substitutions for Thyme:** Oregano / Copaiba / Clove

### Step 4 — Clove
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutions:** On Guard / Cassia / Cinnamon

### Step 5 — Lemongrass
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutions:** Helichrysum / Black Pepper / Dill

### Step 6 — Lemon
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutions:** Wild Orange / Lime / Grapefruit

### Step 7 — Geranium
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutions:** Copaiba / Zendocrine / Rose

### Step 8 — Sandalwood
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutions:** Cedarwood / Spikenard / Myrrh

### Step 9 — Turmeric
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutions:** Ginger / Cinnamon / Cardamom

### Step 10 — Peppermint
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutions:** Deep Blue / Spearmint / DigestZen (ZenGest)

## Heating (Optional)

Heating can be added to the protocol: in the shower, bath, sauna, or using a hot towel. Heating enhances the effect of the protocol.

> Even if you are missing an item, perform the protocol anyway. It is better than nothing.
