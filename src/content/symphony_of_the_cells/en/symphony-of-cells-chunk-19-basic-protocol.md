## Protocol Steps

### Step 1 — Frankincense
Apply to the heart/chest area, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutes:** Balance / Immortelle (Salubelle) / Pink Pepper / Fennel

### Step 2 — Tea Tree
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutes:** Green Mandarin / Rosemary / Eucalyptus

### Step 3 — Massage Blend: AromaTouch
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutes:** Basil / Cypress / Marjoram

### Step 4 — Peppermint
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutes:** Deep Blue / Spearmint / DigestZen (ZenGest)

> Note: in this protocol (unlike others), there is no carrier oil and no warming step — it is shorter, only 4 steps.
