# Symphony of the Cells™ — "Application to the Heart/Chest Area" Symbol 

![Symphony of the Cells](/images/heart.jpg)

## Symbol Description

Heart icon (terracotta hexagon) — "Application to the heart/chest area".

**Action:** ask the person receiving the procedure to apply the oil to the heart area themselves.

**Method of application:**
- if the oil is in a roller bottle — it can be applied directly to the heart/chest area;
- or first apply to the hands, and then transfer to the heart/chest area using massage movements.
