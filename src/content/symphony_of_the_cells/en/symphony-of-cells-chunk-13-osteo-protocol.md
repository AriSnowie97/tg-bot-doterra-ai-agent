## Protocol Steps

### Step 1 — Frankincense
Apply to the heart/chest area, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutions:** Balance / Immortelle (Salubelle) / Pink Pepper / Fennel

### Step 2 — Carrier Oil (Fractionated Coconut)
Apply a layer of oil over the entire back.
**Substitutions:** olive oil, coconut oil, sesame oil, almond oil, etc.

### Step 3 — Rosemary
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutions:** Tea Tree / Neroli / Eucalyptus

### Step 4 — Massage Blend: AromaTouch
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutions:** Basil / Cypress / Marjoram

### Step 5 — Lemongrass
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutions:** Helichrysum / Black Pepper / Dill

### Step 6 — Grapefruit
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutions:** Bergamot / Tangerine / Wild Orange

### Step 7 — Lemon
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutions:** Wild Orange / Lime / Grapefruit

### Step 8 — Helichrysum
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutions:** Deep Blue / Immortelle / Myrrh

### Step 9 — Siberian Fir
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutions:** Douglas Fir / White Fir / Cedarwood

### Step 10 — Peppermint
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutions:** Deep Blue / Spearmint / DigestZen (ZenGest)

## Heating (Optional)

You can add heating to the protocol: in the shower, bath, sauna, or with a warm towel. Heating enhances the effect of the protocol.

> Even if you are missing an item, perform the protocol anyway. It is better than nothing.
