## Protocol Steps

### Step 1 — Frankincense
Apply to the heart/chest area, inhale from hands, apply to feet.
**Substitutes:** Balance / Immortelle (Salubelle) / Pink Pepper / Fennel

### Step 2 — Carrier Oil (Fractionated Coconut)
Apply a layer of oil to the entire back.
**Substitutes:** olive oil, coconut oil, sesame oil, almond oil, etc.

### Step 3 — Oregano
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutes:** Thyme / Basil / Caraway

### Step 4 — Thyme
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutes:** Oregano / Copaiba / Clove

### Step 5 — Protective Blend / On Guard
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutes:** Cinnamon / Clove / Copaiba

### Step 6 — Massage Blend: AromaTouch
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutes:** Basil / Cypress / Marjoram

### Step 7 — Lemongrass
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutes:** Helichrysum / Black Pepper / Dill

### Step 8 — Tea Tree
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutes:** Green Mandarin / Rosemary / Eucalyptus

### Step 9 — Melissa and Arborvitae
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutes for Melissa:** Black Pepper / Lemongrass / Copaiba
**Substitutes for Arborvitae:** Melissa / Siberian Fir / Birch

### Step 10 — Peppermint
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutes:** Deep Blue / Spearmint / DigestZen (ZenGest)

## Heat Application (Optional)

You can add heat application to the protocol: in the shower, bath, sauna, or using a hot towel. Heat enhances the effect of the protocol.

> Even if an item is missing, perform the protocol anyway. It is better than nothing.
