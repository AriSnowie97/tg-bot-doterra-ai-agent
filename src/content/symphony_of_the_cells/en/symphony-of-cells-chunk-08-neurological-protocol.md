## Protocol Steps

### Step 1 — Frankincense
Apply to heart/chest area, back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutions:** Balance / Immortelle (Salubelle) / Pink Pepper / Fennel

### Step 2 — Carrier Oil (fractionated coconut)
Apply a layer of oil over the entire back.
**Substitutions:** olive oil, coconut oil, sesame oil, almond oil, etc.

### Step 3 — Thyme
Apply to back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutions:** Oregano / Copaiba / Clove

### Step 4 — Clove
Apply to back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutions:** On Guard / Cassia / Cinnamon

### Step 5 — Massage Blend: AromaTouch
Apply to back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutions:** Basil / Cypress / Marjoram

### Step 6 — Lemongrass
Apply to back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutions:** Helichrysum / Black Pepper / Dill

### Step 7 — Melissa and Arborvitae
Apply to back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutions for Melissa:** Black Pepper / Lemongrass / Copaiba
**Substitutions for Arborvitae:** Melissa / Siberian Fir / Birch

### Step 8 — Turmeric
Apply to back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutions:** Ginger / Cinnamon / Cardamom

### Step 9 — Patchouli
Apply to back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutions:** Vetiver / In Tune / Cedarwood

### Step 10 — Bergamot
Apply to back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutions:** Grapefruit / Whisper / Lime

### Step 11 — Peppermint
Apply to back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutions:** Deep Blue / Spearmint / DigestZen (ZenGest)

> Even if you are missing an item, perform the protocol anyway. It is better than nothing.
