## Protocol Steps

### Step 1 — Frankincense
Apply to the heart/chest area, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutes:** Balance / Immortelle (Salubelle) / Pink Pepper / Fennel

### Step 2 — Neroli
Apply to the back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutes:** Petitgrain / Tea Tree / Lavender

### Step 3 — Rose
Apply the oil to hands first, bring them to the nose (inhale), then apply the oil with hands to the back, feet.
**Substitutes:** Geranium / Lemongrass / Ylang-Ylang

### Step 4 — Jasmine
Apply the oil to hands first, bring them to the nose (inhale), then apply the oil with hands to the back, feet.
**Substitutes:** Roman Chamomile / Rose / Ylang-Ylang

### Step 5 — Carrier Oil (Fractionated Coconut)
Apply a layer of oil over the entire back.
**Substitutes:** olive oil, coconut oil, sesame oil, almond oil, etc.

### Step 6 — Spikenard
Apply to the back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutes:** Myrrh / Vetiver / Patchouli

### Step 7 — Melissa
Apply to the back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutes:** Black Pepper

### Step 8 — Centering Blend: Align
Apply to the back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutes:** Rose / Jasmine / Geranium

### Step 9 — Douglas Fir
Apply to the back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutes:** Siberian Fir / Cypress / Blue Tansy

### Step 10 — Spearmint
Apply to the back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutes:** Peppermint / Pink Pepper / Peace

> Even if you are missing an item, perform the protocol anyway. It is better than nothing.
