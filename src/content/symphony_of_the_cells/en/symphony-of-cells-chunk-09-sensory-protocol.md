## Protocol Steps

### Step 1 — Grounding Blend: Balance and Frankincense
Apply to heart/chest area, back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutes for Balance:** Black Spruce / Frankincense / Blue Tansy
**Substitutes for Frankincense:** Balance / Immortelle (Salubelle) / Pink Pepper / Fennel

### Step 2 — Carrier Oil (Fractionated Coconut)
Apply a layer of oil to the entire back.
**Substitutes:** olive oil, coconut oil, sesame oil, almond oil, etc.

### Step 3 — Petitgrain
