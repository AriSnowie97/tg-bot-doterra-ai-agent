# Symphony of the Cells™ — "Carrier Oil Application" Symbol

![Symphony of the Cells](/images/droplet.jpg)

## Symbol Description

Droplet icon (brown hexagon) — "Carrier Oil Application".

**Action:** Apply a layer of carrier oil over the entire back. Fractionated coconut oil or another oil is suitable: olive, coconut, sesame, almond, etc.

**Purpose:** Carrier oil acts as a protective barrier before applying potentially stronger essential oils that may irritate sensitive skin.
