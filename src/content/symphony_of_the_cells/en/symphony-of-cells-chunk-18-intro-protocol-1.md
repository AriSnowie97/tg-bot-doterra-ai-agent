## Protocol Steps

### Step 1 — Frankincense
Apply to back and feet.
**Substitutes:** Balance / Immortelle (Salubelle) / Pink Pepper / Fennel

### Step 2 — Carrier Oil (fractionated coconut)
Apply a layer of oil over the entire back.
**Substitutes:** olive oil, coconut oil, sesame oil, almond oil, etc.

### Step 3 — Oregano
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutes:** Thyme / Basil / Caraway

### Step 4 — Digestive Blend: DigestZen (ZenGest)
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutes:** Ginger / Peppermint / Fennel

### Step 5 — Tea Tree
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutes:** Green Mandarin / Rosemary / Eucalyptus

### Step 6 — Lemon
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutes:** Wild Orange / Lime / Grapefruit

### Step 7 — Protective Blend: On Guard
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutes:** Cinnamon / Clove / Copaiba

### Step 8 — Peppermint
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutes:** Deep Blue / Spearmint / DigestZen (ZenGest)

> Even if you are missing an item, perform the protocol anyway. It is better than nothing.
