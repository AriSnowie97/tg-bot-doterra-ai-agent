## Protocol Steps

### Step 1 — Frankincense
Inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutes:** Balance / Immortelle (Salubelle) / Pink Pepper / Fennel

### Step 2 — Carrier Oil (fractionated coconut)
Apply a layer of oil over the entire back.
**Substitutes:** olive oil, coconut oil, sesame oil, almond oil, etc.

### Step 3 — Thyme
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutes:** Oregano / Copaiba / Clove

### Step 4 — Massage Blend: AromaTouch
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutes:** Basil / Cypress / Marjoram

### Step 5 — Turmeric and Lemongrass
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutes for Turmeric:** Ginger / Cinnamon / Cardamom
**Substitutes for Lemongrass:** Helichrysum / Black Pepper / Dill

### Step 6 — Protective Blend / On Guard
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutes:** Cinnamon / Clove / Copaiba

### Step 7 — Marjoram
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutes:** Basil / Cypress / AromaTouch

### Step 8 — Basil
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutes:** AromaTouch / Marjoram / Coriander

### Step 9 — Lavender
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutes:** Petitgrain / Roman Chamomile / Coriander

### Step 10 — Peppermint
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutes:** Deep Blue / Spearmint / DigestZen (ZenGest)

## Heating (Optional)

You can add heat application to the protocol: in a shower, bath, sauna, or using a hot towel. Heating enhances the effect of the protocol.

> Even if an item is missing, perform the protocol anyway. It is better than nothing.
