# Symphony of the Cells™ — "Application to the Liver Area" Symbol

![Symphony of the Cells](/images/liver.jpg)

## Symbol Description

Liver icon (salmon hexagon) — "Application to the liver area".

**Action:** ask the person receiving the application to apply the oil to their liver area themselves (on the right side, below the rib cage).

**Method of application:**
- if the oil is in a roller bottle — it can be applied directly to the liver area;
- or first apply to the hands, and then rub into the liver area using massaging motions.
