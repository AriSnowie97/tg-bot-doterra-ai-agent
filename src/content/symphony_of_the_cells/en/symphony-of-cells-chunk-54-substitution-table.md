# Symphony of the Cells™ — Oil Substitutability Chart 

## General Substitution Rule

You can select a replacement for an essential oil or blend if you do not have a specific oil from the protocol.

1. If none of the oils suggested as replacements are available, find this oil as an ingredient in a blend. For example, if Frankincense is needed but unavailable, take the Balance blend or any other blend that contains it.
2. As recommended by Boyd Truman: if neither the required oil, nor a replacement, nor a blend containing it is available, substitute with Frankincense, Copaiba, or Turmeric.
