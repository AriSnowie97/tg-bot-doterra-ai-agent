## Protocol Steps

### Step 1 — Frankincense
Apply to heart/chest area, inhale from hands, apply to feet.
**Substitutes:** Balance / Immortelle (Salubelle) / Pink Pepper / Fennel

### Step 2 — Neroli
Apply to back, feet.
**Substitutes:** Petitgrain / Tea Tree / Lavender

### Step 3 — Carrier Oil (Fractionated Coconut)

**Substitutes:** olive oil, coconut oil, sesame oil, almond oil, etc.

Heat application can be added to the protocol: in the shower, bath, sauna, or using a hot towel. Heat enhances the effect of the protocol.
