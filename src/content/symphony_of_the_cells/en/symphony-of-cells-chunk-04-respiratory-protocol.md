## Protocol Steps

### Step 1 — Frankincense
Apply to the heart/chest area, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutes:** Balance / Immortelle (Salubelle) / Pink Pepper / Fennel

### Step 2 — Carrier Oil (Fractionated Coconut)
Apply a layer of oil to the entire back.
**Substitutes:** olive oil, coconut oil, sesame oil, almond oil, etc.

### Step 3 — Tea Tree
Apply to back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutes:** Green Mandarin / Rosemary / Eucalyptus

### Step 4 — Protective Blend / On Guard
Apply to back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutes:** Cinnamon / Clove / Copaiba

### Step 5 — Massage Blend: AromaTouch
Apply to back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutes:** Basil / Cypress / Marjoram

### Step 6 — Respiratory Blend: Breathe/Air
Apply to back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutes:** Eucalyptus / Rosemary / Tea Tree

### Step 7 — Rosemary
Apply to back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutes:** Tea Tree / Neroli / Eucalyptus

### Step 8 — Eucalyptus
Apply to back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutes:** Breathe (Air) / Tea Tree / Basil

### Step 9 — Melissa and Arborvitae
Apply to back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutes for Melissa:** Black Pepper / Lemongrass / Copaiba
**Substitutes for Arborvitae:** Melissa / Siberian Fir / Birch

### Step 10 — Peppermint
Apply to back, inhale from palms, apply to feet.
**Substitutes:** DeepBlue / Spearmint / DigestZen (ZenGest)

## Warming Up (Optional)

Warming up can be added to the protocol: in the shower, bath, sauna, or using a hot towel. Warming up enhances the effect of the protocol.

> Even if you are missing a specific item, perform the protocol anyway. It is better than nothing.
