## Protocol Steps

### Standing

#### Step 1 — Rose
Inhale from hands.
**Substitutes:** Geranium / Lemongrass / Ylang-Ylang

#### Step 2 — Geranium
Inhale from hands.
**Substitutes:** Copaiba / Zendocrine / Rose

### Lying Down

#### Step 3 — Frankincense
Apply to heart/chest area, inhale from hands, apply to feet.
**Substitutes:** Balance / Immortelle (Salubelle) / Pink Pepper / Fennel

#### Step 4 — Neroli
Apply to back, inhale from hands, apply to feet.
**Substitutes:** Petitgrain / Tea Tree / Lavender

#### Step 5 — Carrier Oil (fractionated coconut)
Apply a layer of oil over the entire back.
**Substitutes:** olive oil, coconut oil, sesame oil, almond oil, etc.

#### Step 6 — Clove
Apply to back, inhale from hands, apply to feet.
**Substitutes:** On Guard / Cassia / Cinnamon

#### Step 7 — Melissa
Apply to back, inhale from hands, apply to feet.
**Substitutes:** Black Pepper / Lemongrass / Copaiba

#### Step 8 — Juniper Berry
Apply to back, inhale from hands, apply to feet.
**Substitutes:** Zendocrine / Coriander / Celery Seed

#### Step 9 — Bergamot
Apply to back, inhale from hands, apply to feet.
**Substitutes:** Grapefruit / Whisper / Lime

#### Step 10 — Renewing Blend: Forgive
Apply to back, inhale from hands, apply to feet.
**Substitutes:** Bergamot / Juniper Berry / Myrrh

> Even if an oil is missing, perform the protocol anyway. It is better than nothing.
