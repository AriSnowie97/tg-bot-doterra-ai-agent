# Veráge Skin Care — Immortelle Hydrating Serum

**Category:** Personal Care

**Type:** hydrating serum


---

## Brief Description

👑 Hydrating serum formulated with a lipid complex that mirrors the lipids found in young, healthy skin
👑 Combines six essential oils: Frankincense, Hawaiian Sandalwood, Lavender, Myrrh, Helichrysum, and Rose
👑 Deeply hydrates and reduces the appearance of fine lines

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Jojoba and Macadamia Oil Esters
▫️ Frankincense essential oil (Boswellia carterii/sacra/papyrifera/frereana)
▫️ Hawaiian Sandalwood essential oil (Santalum paniculatum)
▫️ Lavender, Myrrh, Helichrysum, and Rose essential oils
▫️ Squalene, Phytosterols, Tocopherol

## Indications

🔹 Deep hydration: the lipid complex supports the skin's natural barrier
🔹 Anti-aging care: reduces the appearance of fine lines and promotes smoothness
🔹 Skin balance: maintains optimal lipid balance for a radiant look
🔹 Suitable for all skin types and ages

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply to clean face and neck in a light upward motion
🔹 Use morning and night
🔹 Follow with Veráge Moisturizer to complete your care ritual

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Veráge Cleanser and Veráge Toner — previous steps in the Veráge line
🔹 Veráge Moisturizer — final step to seal in hydration

## Interesting Facts

👑 The name Immortelle refers to the flower that retains its shape even after drying — a symbol of lasting beauty
👑 The lipid complex is formulated to mimic the structure of skin lipids at peak health and youthfulness
👑 The formula features six essential oils simultaneously — a rarity for a cosmetic product

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
