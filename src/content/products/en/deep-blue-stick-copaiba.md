# doTERRA Deep Blue® Stick + Copaiba

**Category:** Personal Care

**Type:** Topical stick (48 g)


---

## Short Description

👑 A solid stick with the Deep Blue blend (CPTG) and copaiba for topical application to areas of tension
👑 On the US label it is a topical analgesic with 10% menthol for temporary relief of minor aches and pains in muscles and joints
👑 Dries quickly and contains moisturizing emollients — skin feels soft and not greasy, with a cooling, soothing sensation

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Active ingredient: menthol 10% (topical analgesic)
▫️ Botanical oils: wintergreen (Gaultheria procumbens), copaiba (Copaifera), peppermint (Mentha piperita), camphor, eucalyptus (Eucalyptus globulus), blue tansy (Tanacetum annuum), ylang ylang (Cananga odorata), chamomile (Chamomilla recutita), helichrysum (Helichrysum italicum), sweet almond, coconut oil
▫️ Base: tapioca starch, sunflower seed wax, glycerin, glyceryl laurate, glyceryl caprylate, glyceryl undecylenate, lauric acid, citric acid, water
▫️ Extracts: elderberry (Sambucus nigra), capsicum (Capsicum frutescens)

## Indications

🔹 Temporary relief of minor aches and pains of muscles and joints associated with simple backache, arthritis, sprains, strains and bruises (per the US label)
🔹 A cooling, soothing effect on the chosen area
🔹 A convenient on-the-go form: no dilution and no spills

## Directions for Use

🔹 Adults and children over 12: apply generously to the affected area; repeat as necessary, but no more than 4 times daily
🔹 Children under 12: consult a doctor
🔹 For a more intensive experience, apply the Deep Blue essential oil blend to the skin first, then the stick
🔹 For external use only

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products

🔹 Deep Blue® Soothing Blend — the essential oil blend (separate KB file)
🔹 Deep Blue® Rub — the 120 ml cream (separate KB file)
🔹 Deep Blue Polyphenol Complex — capsules for nutritional support (separate KB file)
🔹 Copaiba — the single essential oil (separate KB file)

## Fun Facts

👑 The stick is part of the Deep Blue line alongside the blend, the Rub cream and the Polyphenol Complex
👑 The labeled size is 48 g (1.69 oz); store below 38 °C (100 °F)
👑 Registered in the US as an over-the-counter (OTC) product: active ingredient menthol 10%

## Cautions

> ⚠️ For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes and mucous membranes (if contact occurs, rinse thoroughly with water). Do not apply to wounds or damaged skin, bandage tightly or use with a heating pad. Do not use if allergic to salicylates (including aspirin) without consulting a physician. Stop use and ask a doctor if irritation or redness occurs, if nausea, vomiting, abdominal discomfort, diarrhea or skin rash occurs, or if pain lasts more than 7 days. Pregnant or breast-feeding users should ask a health care professional before use. Keep out of reach of children; if swallowed, get medical help immediately. Do not use if the seal is broken.
