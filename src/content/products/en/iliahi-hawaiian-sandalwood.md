# 'Iliahi (Hawaiian Sandalwood)

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Essential Oil (Single, 15 mL)


---

## Brief Description

👑 Hawaiian sandalwood species for hair and skin restoration
👑 Promotes relaxation and prepares for a peaceful sleep
👑 Rich, warm, woody aroma

## Ingredients & Key Components

▫️ Santalum paniculatum (Hawaiian Sandalwood) essential oil

## Indications

🔹 Restores moisture to hair and adds shine
🔹 Relaxation and stress reduction
🔹 Preparation for a peaceful sleep

## Directions for Use

🔹 Add to shampoo or conditioner for hair restoration
🔹 Diffuse in the evening to relax before bedtime

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Sandalwood (Santalum album, Indian species) — an alternative species with similar properties

## Fun Facts

👑 'Iliahi is the Hawaiian name for sandalwood
👑 Unlike Indian Sandalwood, it originates from Hawaii (Santalum paniculatum)

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
