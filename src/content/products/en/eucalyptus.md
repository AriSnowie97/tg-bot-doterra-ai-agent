# Eucalyptus

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Essential Oil (Single, 15 ml)


---

## Short Description

👑 Camphorous oil for easy breathing and open airways
👑 Reduces feelings of tension when inhaled
👑 Cooling effect during massage

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Eucalyptus radiata essential oil (eucalyptus)

## Indications

🔹 Support for easy breathing
🔹 Reduction of feelings of tension
🔹 Cooling massage

## Directions for Use

🔹 Diffuse or apply to the chest for easy breathing
🔹 Inhale the aroma for relaxation

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 dōTERRA Breathe® — eucalyptus is part of the blend
🔹 dōTERRA On Guard® — contains a different species of eucalyptus

## Interesting Facts

👑 dōTERRA uses several species of eucalyptus in various products for different purposes

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
