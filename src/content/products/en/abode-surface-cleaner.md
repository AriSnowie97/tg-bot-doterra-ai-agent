# abōde Multi-Purpose Surface Cleaner Concentrate

**Category:** Home Care Products (abōde Line)

**Type:** Multi-Purpose Surface Cleaner Concentrate


---

## Short Description

👑 Essential oil–based multi-purpose surface cleaner concentrate — diluted with water in its own bottle
👑 Formula free of phosphates, sulfates, phthalates, dyes, and chlorine
👑 Suitable for kitchen, bathroom, countertops, tile, and finished wood

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Essential blend abōde Refreshing Blend: Lime, Litsea, Cassia, Lemon Eucalyptus, Tea Tree, Arborvitae, Kochii Eucalyptus, Coriander, Lavandin, Lemon Myrtle
▫️ Thymol — a key component of the blend, known for its cleansing properties
▫️ Concentrated formula — reduces plastic use and shipping costs

## Indications

🔹 Universal cleaning: suitable for kitchen surfaces, bathroom, countertops, fixtures
🔹 Fresh scent: leaves a pleasant citrus-herbal aroma in the home
🔹 Eco-friendliness: concentrate reduces plastic usage and transport emissions
🔹 Safer formula: free of phosphates, sulfates, phthalates, dyes, and chlorine

## Directions for Use

🔹 Pour concentrate into a glass spray bottle and top with clean water to the fill line
🔹 Spray onto surface and wipe clean with a cloth or sponge
🔹 Suitable for countertops, tile, fixtures, finished wood; avoid unsealed stone or delicate surfaces without spot testing

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Part of the abōde line — liquid dish soap, dishwasher and laundry pods, dryer ball
🔹 The aroma of the abōde Refreshing Blend can also be used separately in a diffuser to freshen the air

## Interesting Facts

👑 Thymol, a key component of the blend, is widely known for its cleansing and antiseptic properties
👑 The abōde Refreshing Blend differs from the doTERRA On Guard blend — it was developed specifically for home cleaning, whereas On Guard was originally created for internal use
👑 Sold as a concentrate specifically to reduce shipping excess water and lower the carbon footprint

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.

## Additional Information

**Precautions for use:**
🔹 For household use only, not for internal consumption
🔹 Avoid contact with eyes
🔹 Keep out of reach of children
🔹 Test on a small, inconspicuous area before using on new types of surfaces
🔹 The provided information is intended for informational purposes only. Test the product on a small area before applying to new surfaces.
