# dōTERRA baby Diaper Rash Cream

**Category:** Personal Care

**Type:** Diaper Rash Cream


---

## Brief Description

👑 Natural cream for the treatment and prevention of diaper rash
👑 Clinically tested and approved for sensitive baby skin
👑 Free of parabens, phthalates, propylene glycol, artificial colors, and talc

## Ingredients and Key Components

▫️ Lavender, Carrot Seed, and Tea Tree essential oils
▫️ Non-nano zinc oxide (creates a moisture barrier)
▫️ Muyao Shea Butter

## Indications

🔹 Diaper rash and diaper irritation
🔹 Need for a protective moisture barrier

## Directions for Use

🔹 Change wet or soiled diapers promptly
🔹 Cleanse the diaper area and allow it to dry
🔹 Apply cream generously with each diaper change, especially at bedtime

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 dōTERRA baby Lotion, Hair & Body Wash — other products in the baby line

## Interesting Facts

👑 Non-nano zinc oxide creates a barrier against excess moisture, preventing diaper rash

## Cautions

> ⚠️ For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes. If condition worsens or if symptoms last more than 7 days or clear up and occur again within a few days, consult a doctor. Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.
