### Origin
Wild Orange (Citrus sinensis) is a variety of sweet orange grown without chemical fertilizers or pesticides. doTERRA sources the oil primarily from Brazil and the Dominican Republic through cold pressing the fruit's outer peel. The name "wild" reflects the tradition of harvesting fruit from trees growing in natural conditions with minimal human intervention.

### Drug Interactions
*   Photosensitizing effects may be enhanced when used concurrently with certain antibiotics (fluoroquinolones, tetracyclines).
*   May weakly affect metabolic enzymes of certain medications (CYP3A4)—consult a doctor if taking multiple medications.

### Precautions and Contraindications
*   The oil is a photosensitizer—do not apply it to the skin 12-24 hours before exposure to the sun or a tanning bed. Cold pressing preserves a high concentration of photosensitizing components.
*   May cause contact dermatitis in people with citrus allergies.
*   Store the oil in a dark, cool place, as it is prone to oxidation. Do not use oxidized oil (which has become darker in color or harsher in smell) on the skin to avoid irritation.
*   Use exclusively diluted in children.
