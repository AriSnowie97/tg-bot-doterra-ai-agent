# Dawn® Aroma Humidifier

**Category:** Home Products

**Type:** Diffuser-Humidifier


---

## Short Description

👑 Combines the functions of an air humidifier and an essential oil diffuser
👑 Smart night mode maintains a comfortable humidity level at night
👑 Top-fill feature — no need to remove the lid

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Not applicable (a device, not a topical product)

## Indications

🔹 Maintaining a comfortable humidity level in the bedroom during sleep
🔹 Room fragrancing throughout the day

## Directions for Use

🔹 Add water and essential oils through the top opening without removing the lid
🔹 Select mist intensity mode: up to 7 hours (high), up to 12 hours (medium), up to 22 hours (low)
🔹 Use night mode to automatically maintain humidity during sleep

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products

🔹 dōTERRA Serenity® — to create a peaceful sleep atmosphere in the evening
🔹 Lavender, Sandalwood, or Vetiver — for a soothing nighttime scent
🔹 dōTERRA Breathe® or dōTERRA On Guard® — for support during sleep during seasonal threat periods

## Fun Facts

👑 Combines the functions of a classic diffuser and an air humidifier in one device
👑 Smart sleep mode automatically adjusts to maintain comfortable humidity all night long

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Use according to the manufacturer's instructions. Do not leave children unattended near an active device. Clean regularly to prevent oil residue buildup.
