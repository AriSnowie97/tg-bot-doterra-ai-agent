👑 Empty vegetable capsules for customizing your essential oil dietary supplements
👑 160 capsules per bottle — an economical option for regular DIY use
👑 Free of sodium lauryl sulfate, suitable for vegetarians

## Composition & Key Ingredients

▫️ Vegetable capsule shell (hydroxypropyl methylcellulose)
▫️ No fillers — empty capsule designed for custom filling

## Indications

🔹 DIY use: allows taking your favorite essential oils internally in encapsulated form, economical, dose control, convenient (masks taste)

## Directions for Use

🔹 Add chosen essential oil drops into the capsule, close, and swallow with water
🔹 Fill the capsule immediately prior to use
