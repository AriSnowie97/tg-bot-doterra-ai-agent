# Essential Skin Care Brightening Gel

**Category:** Personal Care

**Type:** Face Gel


---

## Short Description

👑 Gel for visible brightening of dark spots and an even skin tone
👑 Protects the stability of vitamin C in the formula
👑 Refreshing texture for daily or weekly use

## Composition & Key Ingredients

▫️ Bergamot, Juniper Berry, and Melissa essential oils
▫️ Daisy extract — a natural brightening agent
▫️ Microencapsulated vitamin C

## Indications

🔹 Dark spots and hyperpigmentation
🔹 Dull complexion

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply 1-2 times a week as an addition to your routine
🔹 Can be used in the morning or evening after toner

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products

🔹 Essential Skin Care Tightening Serum, Anti-Aging Eye Cream — for a comprehensive routine

## Interesting Facts

👑 Microencapsulation technology protects vitamin C from breakdown exposed to oxygen, ensuring stable delivery to the skin

## Cautions

> ⚠️ For external use only. Avoid direct contact with eyes. Discontinue use if skin irritation occurs. Keep out of reach of children.
