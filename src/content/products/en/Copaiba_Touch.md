# Copaiba Touch

**Category:** Roll-ons (Touch)

**Type:** Single Oil, Touch (10 ml roll-on)


---

## Short Description

👑 Favorite Copaiba oil in a convenient roll-on format
👑 Moisturizes the skin, promotes a clear and even skin tone
👑 Highest concentration of beta-caryophyllene among known essential oils

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Fractionated Coconut Oil
▫️ Copaiba tree resin (Copaifera spp.)

## Indications

🔹 Face and body skin care, massage after physical exertion

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply to face, neck, and décolleté for a clear skin tone
🔹 Use for leg massage after a workout
🔹 Apply a small amount to cuticles

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Blends well with Roman Chamomile, Cedarwood, Sandalwood, Frankincense, Ylang Ylang

## Interesting Facts

👑 Copaiba resin is harvested from the trunk of a tropical tree in South America
👑 Has been used in traditional Brazilian practices since the 16th century

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
