# doTERRA Peace — Reassuring Blend

**Category:** Essential oils

**Type:** Blend (Reassuring Blend, Emotional Aromatherapy)


---

## Overview

👑 A reassuring blend that reminds you: you don't have to be perfect to find peace
👑 Sweet, rich, minty aroma — vetiver, lavender, ylang ylang, frankincense, clary sage, marjoram, labdanum, spearmint
👑 One of the six official Emotional Aromatherapy blends — specifically designed for peace and soothing anxious feelings

## Ingredients & Key Components

▫️ Vetiver (Vetiveria zizanioides)
▫️ Lavender (Lavandula angustifolia)
▫️ Ylang Ylang (Cananga odorata)
▫️ Frankincense (Boswellia)
▫️ Clary Sage (Salvia sclarea)
▫️ Marjoram (Origanum majorana)
▫️ Labdanum (Cistus ladanifer)
▫️ Spearmint (Mentha spicata)

## Indications

🔹 Anxious thoughts: calms the mind during moments of worry or fear
🔹 Sleepless nights: application before bedtime helps stem restless thoughts
🔹 Restless child: gentle formula suitable even for the youngest family members
🔹 Meditation: applying to the forehead during practice deepens focus

## Directions for Use

🔹 Diffuse in the evening before sleep to calm anxious thoughts, or inhale directly from the bottle during moments of anxiety or panic
🔹 Apply to temples, bottoms of feet (Touch version), or during meditation to deepen practice
🔹 Internal use: not recommended for internal use — the blend is intended for aromatic and topical application

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products

🔹 Blends well with: doTERRA Motivate, Frankincense

## Interesting Facts

👑 Peace is one of the six official Emotional Aromatherapy blends, specially developed to address anxiety and fear
👑 On the Emotional Aromatherapy wheel, Peace is located next to Motivate — together they balance soothing anxiety and moving forward

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
