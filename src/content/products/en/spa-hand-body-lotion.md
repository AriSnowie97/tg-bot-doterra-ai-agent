# dōTERRA Spa Hand & Body Lotion

**Category:** Personal Care (dōTERRA Spa)

**Type:** Hand & Body Lotion (unscented)


---

## Short Description

👑 Lightweight, weightless hand and body lotion with no added fragrance — a base for personal essential oil blends
👑 Absorbs quickly without leaving a greasy residue
👑 Part of the dōTERRA SPA line for home spa care

## Composition & Key Ingredients

▫️ Sunflower seed oil, macadamia seed oil, jojoba seed oil
▫️ Murumuru seed butter and theobroma (cocoa) seed butter
▫️ Coconut oil, botanical extracts

## Indications

🔹 Moisturizing and nourishing dry skin on hands and body
🔹 Personalization: a neutral base allows adding your favorite essential oil for a custom fragrance
🔹 Hand care for frequent use without a greasy feel
🔹 Home spa ritual for relaxation and enjoyable body care

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply a generous amount to hands and body to moisturize dry skin
🔹 Add a drop of your favorite doTERRA essential oil to the palm of your hand together with the lotion for a personalized fragrance
🔹 Suitable for regular use

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Pairs well with citrus oils (Wild Orange, Bergamot) for an invigorating fragrance
🔹 Blends with Lavender, Roman Chamomile for a relaxing evening ritual
🔹 Part of the broader dōTERRA SPA line (Citrus Bliss Lotion, Rose Lotion, and others)

## Interesting Facts

👑 The formula is fragrance-free specifically so you can create your own custom aromatic composition
👑 Murumuru butter originates from the Amazon and is traditionally used to nourish skin and hair
👑 The lotion is formulated for fast absorption without leaving a greasy feel on the skin

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.

## Additional Information

🔹 For external use only.
🔹 Discontinue use if irritation occurs.
🔹 The information provided is for informational purposes only. Patch test on a small area of skin before use. If you have a history of allergic reactions, consult a dermatologist before use.
