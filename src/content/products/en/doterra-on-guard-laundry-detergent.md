# dōTERRA On Guard® Laundry Detergent

**Category:** Home Care

**Type:** Laundry Detergent (6x concentrate)


---

## Brief Description

👑 Ultra-concentrated (6x) laundry detergent based on the On Guard blend
👑 Effectively removes stains thanks to a bio-enzyme complex
👑 Safe for colored fabrics at any temperature

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Natural surfactant agent (Caprylyl/Myristyl Glucoside)
▫️ Bio-enzyme complex: protease, amylase, cellulase, mannanase, lipase
▫️ 10 mL of dōTERRA On Guard® essential oil blend (Wild Orange, Clove, Cinnamon, Eucalyptus, Rosemary) in each bottle

## Indications

🔹 Daily clothes washing
🔹 Removal of stubborn stains

## Directions for Use

🔹 Stain: apply a small amount of product directly onto the stain, leave for 3–10 minutes, then wash as usual
🔹 For stubborn stains — repeat application before the fabric dries and repeat the wash cycle
🔹 Test fabric colorfastness on a small area before treating

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 dōTERRA On Guard® Cleaner Concentrate — for home cleaning
🔹 dōTERRA On Guard® Foaming Hand Wash — for hand washing

## Interesting Facts

👑 One bottle provides up to 64 loads thanks to the ultra-concentrated (6x) formula
👑 Surfactants and enzymes are derived from sustainable, non-mineral sources

## Cautions

> ⚠️ For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes. Keep out of reach of children.
