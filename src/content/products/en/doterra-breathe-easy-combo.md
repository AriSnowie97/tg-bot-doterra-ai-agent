# dōTERRA Breathe Easy Combo

**Category:** Kits

**Type:** Respiratory Kit


---

## Short Description

👑 A trio of dōTERRA Breathe® line products for easy breathing
👑 Combines an essential oil blend, drops, and a convenient stick for on-the-go use
👑 An ideal kit for seasons of increased respiratory demand

## Contents and Key Ingredients

▫️ dōTERRA Breathe® Respiratory Blend — 15 ml essential oil blend
▫️ dōTERRA Breathe® Respiratory Drops — 2 bags of drops (lemon, peppermint, eucalyptus, thyme, melissa, cardamom)
▫️ dōTERRA Breathe® Vapor Stick

## Indications

🔹 Supports feelings of clear airways and easy breathing
🔹 Convenient application at home, at work, or on the go

## Directions for Use

🔹 Diffuse essential oil blend or apply to the chest
🔹 Dissolve drops slowly in mouth as needed
🔹 Apply Vapor Stick directly to the skin — absorbs quickly without leaving a greasy residue

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 dōTERRA Breathe® Touch — roll-on version of the classic blend

## Fun Facts

👑 Vapor Stick — a convenient format for a purse or briefcase, requires no dilution with a carrier oil

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
