# doTERRA sun Face + Body Mineral Sunscreen Lotion

**Category:** Skin & Hair Care → Sun Care

**Type:** sunscreen (mineral SPF)


---

## Short Description

👑 Mineral sunscreen lotion SPF 30 based on zinc oxide (without chemical UV filters)
👑 Broad spectrum UVA/UVB protection, water-resistant up to 80 minutes
👑 Formula free of oxybenzone, parabens, and phthalates; contains essential oils of frankincense, helichrysum, and carrot

## Ingredients and Key Components

▫️ Zinc oxide (non-nano) — mineral UV filter
▫️ Aloe vera, vitamin E (tocopherol)
▫️ Raspberry seed oil, avocado oil, jojoba esters
▫️ Essential oils: Frankincense, Helichrysum, Carrot Seed

## Indications

🔹 Sun protection: broad spectrum UVA/UVB, suitable for regular application on face and body
🔹 Skin nourishment: vitamin E and plant oils support the skin's barrier function
🔹 Suitable for sensitive skin and the whole family
🔹 Leaves no white cast upon application

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply an even layer to exposed skin areas before sun exposure
🔹 Reapply every 2 hours or immediately after swimming or heavy sweating
🔹 Suitable for face and body

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products

🔹 The sun line also includes a spray, stick (SPF 40), and After Sun restorative spray
🔹 Can be combined with After Sun Restorative Body Mist to restore skin after sun exposure

## Interesting Facts

👑 Zinc oxide is a mineral (physical) UV filter that reflects sun rays, unlike chemical filters
👑 The formula does not contain oxybenzone — an ingredient that causes concern due to its impact on marine ecosystems (reef-safe product)
👑 Contains frankincense — one of the most famous oils in the doTERRA portfolio

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.

## Additional Information

🔹 For external use only
🔹 If irritation occurs, discontinue use
🔹 Patch test on a small area of skin before use. If you have a history of allergic reactions, consult a dermatologist before use
