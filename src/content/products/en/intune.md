# InTune — Focus Blend

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Blend (Focus Blend)


---

## Brief Description

👑 Focus blend — helps maintain attention during work or study
👑 Woody, citrusy, sweet aroma — Amyris, Patchouli, Frankincense, Lime, Ylang Ylang, Hawaiian Sandalwood, Roman Chamomile
👑 Rich in limonene and α-pinene — components with soothing properties that support mental clarity

## Ingredients and Key Components

▫️ Amyris (Amyris balsamifera)
▫️ Patchouli (Pogostemon cablin)
▫️ Frankincense (Boswellia)
▫️ Lime (Citrus aurantifolia)
▫️ Ylang Ylang (Cananga odorata)
▫️ Hawaiian Sandalwood (Santalum paniculatum)
▫️ Roman Chamomile (Anthemis nobilis)

## Indications

🔹 Concentration during study: an ideal companion for studying or completing tasks that require focus
🔹 Mental fatigue: afternoon application helps restore focus
🔹 Project work: applying to the temples before a challenging task helps stay focused
🔹 Classroom or office: diffusion on a clay pendant creates a concentration zone without diffusing into the air
🔹 Calming before an exam or presentation: grounding effect helps cope with nervousness

## Directions for Use

🔹 Inhale directly from the bottle before starting work or study
🔹 Apply to a clay pendant and place on your desk or in the classroom
🔹 Apply to temples and the back of the neck during tasks that require concentration
🔹 Apply to wrists or the back of hands and inhale deeply
🔹 Apply at work when feeling afternoon fatigue (not recommended for internal use)

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 What it pairs well with (diffuser blends): Work Focus (3 drops InTune, 2 drops Rosemary), Study (3 drops InTune, 2 drops Peppermint), Calm Concentration (4 drops InTune)

## Interesting Facts

👑 Amyris earned the nickname "candlewood" due to its ability to burn brightly because of its high oil content
👑 Limonene and α-pinene, the main chemical components of the blend, are known for their soothing properties
👑 InTune is designed as a versatile helper for all ages — from students to adult professionals

**Origin and Composition:**
👑 Amyris is native to the tropical regions of Haiti and Central America and was traditionally used as a torchwood due to its high oil content
👑 Hawaiian Sandalwood ('Iliahi) is a rare oil that grows exclusively in Hawaii
👑 Patchouli and Ylang Ylang are traditionally used in perfumery for their deep, calming scent

**More About Application:**
InTune is unique because it combines stimulating and soothing components simultaneously, helping to achieve "calm concentration" rather than just alertness. The roller bottle format makes the blend convenient to use at school, the office, or the library without needing a diffuser. A clay pendant with the blend applied is a popular way to create a personal "concentration zone" without spraying the fragrance across the whole space.
