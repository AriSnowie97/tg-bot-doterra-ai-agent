# abōde Laundry Pods

**Category:** Home Care

**Type:** Laundry pods


---

## Short Description

👑 Concentrated laundry pods based on natural enzymes
👑 Effectively remove stains and preserve color brightness
👑 Refreshing citrus scent of abōde Refreshing Blend

## Ingredients and Key Components

▫️ Natural enzymes and surfactants
▫️ abōde Refreshing Blend (essential oils)
▫️ Free of phosphates, phthalates, dyes, and chlorine

## Indications

🔹 Daily clothes washing
🔹 Stain removal and fabric freshening

## Directions for Use

🔹 Toss one pod into the drum for a small or medium load
🔹 Two pods — for a large or heavily soiled load

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 abōde Dishwasher Pods — for the dishwasher
🔹 abōde Refreshing Blend — the same scent in other products of the abōde line

## Fun Facts

👑 Sold in a pack of 45 pods
👑 Packaging is made from post-consumer recycled (PCR) materials

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Use only as directed as a laundry detergent. Do not touch pods with wet hands. Store tightly closed in a dry place out of reach of children and pets. If swallowed, drink a glass of water and contact a poison control center or doctor.
