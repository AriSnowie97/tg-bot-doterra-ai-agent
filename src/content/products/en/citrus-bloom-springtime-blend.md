# Citrus Bloom® Springtime Blend

**Category:** Blends

**Type:** Essential Blend (15 ml)


---

## Brief Description

👑 A springtime blend that combines bright citrus and delicate floral notes
👑 Creates a positive, energetic mood
👑 Suitable for daily home aromatization or as a personal fragrance

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Wild Orange
▫️ Grapefruit
▫️ Lavender
▫️ Roman Chamomile
▫️ Magnolia

## Indications

🔹 Supports a positive, energetic mood
🔹 Creates a calm, harmonious atmosphere
🔹 Relieves feelings of anxiety and tension

## Directions for Use

🔹 Diffusion: 3-4 drops in a diffuser in the morning for an energizing start to the day
🔹 1-2 drops on the wrists as a personal fragrance
🔹 Add to body lotion for an aromatic massage
🔹 Topical: 1-2 drops, diluted with a carrier oil for sensitive skin

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 dōTERRA Hand & Body Lotion — for an aromatic massage
🔹 Natural Deodorant infused with Citrus Bloom — the same fragrance in a deodorant

## Interesting Facts

👑 Created specifically for the spring season, but suitable for use all year round
👑 Wild Orange and Grapefruit provide a bright, energetic note, while Lavender and Chamomile offer a soft, calming one

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas. Avoid direct sunlight and UV rays for up to 12 hours after application.
