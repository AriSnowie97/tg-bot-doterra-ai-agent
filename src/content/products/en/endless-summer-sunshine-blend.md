# Endless Summer Sunshine Blend

**Category:** Seasonal Collection

**Type:** Essential Blend (15 ml, limited summer edition)


---

## Brief Description

👑 A summer limited-edition blend embodying the nostalgia of sunny days
👑 Signature watermelon accord with floral and citrus notes
👑 Creates a bright, uplifting summer atmosphere

## Composition & Key Ingredients

▫️ Osmanthus, Grapefruit, Manuka, Green Mandarin (watermelon accord)
▫️ Green Mandarin, Tonka Bean, Grapefruit, Eucalyptus, Sandalwood, Frankincense

## Indications

🔹 Creating a summer, carefree atmosphere at home
🔹 A nostalgic, warm scent for summer evenings

## Directions for Use

🔹 Diffusion: 3-4 drops in a diffuser
🔹 Topically: dilute 1 drop in 10 drops of carrier oil

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products

🔹 Endless Summer Hair & Body Mist — the same scent in a spray format
🔹 Pilot® Diffuser — portable diffuser for the bundle set

## Fun Facts

👑 Key oils are sourced through the Co-Impact Sourcing® program: Green Mandarin, Tonka Bean, Grapefruit, Eucalyptus, Sandalwood, and Frankincense

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas. Avoid direct sunlight and UV rays for up to 12 hours after application.
