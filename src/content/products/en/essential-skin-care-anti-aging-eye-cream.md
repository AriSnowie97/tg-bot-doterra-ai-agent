# Essential Skin Care Anti-Aging Eye Cream

**Category:** Personal Care

**Type:** Eye Cream


---

## Short Description

👑 Cream for the delicate eye area with a rollerball applicator
👑 Reduces the appearance of puffiness and dark circles
👑 Cooling metal roller for an instant effect

## Ingredients and Key Ingredients

▫️ Frankincense, Ylang Ylang, and Blue Tansy essential oils

## Indications

🔹 Under-eye puffiness
🔹 Dark circles
🔹 Fine lines around the eyes

## Directions for Use

🔹 Press the pump until a small amount of product appears near the rollerball
🔹 Gently apply to the under-eye area using light motions
🔹 Use morning and evening

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products

🔹 Essential Skin Care Anti-Aging Moisturizer, Facial Cleanser, Hydrating Cream — for a comprehensive routine

## Interesting Facts

👑 Sabinene, a component of Blue Tansy, is used to reduce the appearance of blemishes
👑 The unique rollerball applicator cools and soothes the skin during application

## Cautions

> ⚠️ For external use only. Avoid direct contact with eyes. If skin irritation occurs, discontinue use. Keep out of reach of children.
