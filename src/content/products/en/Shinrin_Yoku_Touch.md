# Shinrin-Yoku Touch

**Category:** Rollers (Touch)

**Type:** Essential oil blend, Touch (10 ml roll-on)


---

## Short Description

👑 A blend inspired by the practice of forest bathing from Japan
👑 Creates a calming forest atmosphere at home
👑 Rich in terpenes and phytoncides

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Fractionated Coconut Oil
▫️ Oils rich in terpenes and phytoncides (limonene, alpha-pinene, beta-pinene)

## Indications

🔹 The need to slow down, focus, and restore inner balance

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply to the skin during meditation or journaling
🔹 Use as a personal fragrance
🔹 Avoid direct sunlight for up to 12 hours after application

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Complements the mindfulness line concept together with Frankincense Touch

## Interesting Facts

👑 The practice of shinrin-yoku (forest bathing) originated in Japan in the early 1980s
👑 Clinical studies confirm the calming effect of terpenes on the atmosphere

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
