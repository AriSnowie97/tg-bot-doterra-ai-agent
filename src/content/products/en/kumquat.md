# Kumquat

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Essential Oil (Single, 5 ml)


---

## Short Description
👑 A sweet, clean, refreshing citrus aroma
👑 Officially described as uplifting and invigorating, supporting metabolism and several body systems
👑 An oil from kumquat peel, obtained by cold pressing

## Composition and Key Ingredients
▫️ Fortunella japonica (Japanese citrus) essential oil, peel, cold pressed
▫️ The main component is limonene

## Indications
🔹 Lifting the mood and invigorating (per the official description)
🔹 Support for metabolism and the cardiovascular, digestive, immune and nervous systems (per the official description)
🔹 Adding to cleaning products to boost their effect

## Directions for Use
🔹 Aromatic: 3-4 drops in a diffuser
🔹 Topical: 1-2 drops on the desired area, dilute with a carrier oil to reduce skin sensitivity
🔹 Other: 1 drop in a facial cleanser, shampoo or toothpaste; a spray to freshen the air; 1-2 drops in a cleaning product
🔹 Internal: the official page mentions 1 drop in 120 ml of liquid; per the knowledge-base rules internal use is a higher-risk category (only if the bottle carries "Supplement Facts"), such questions are routed to a specialist

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products
🔹 Lemon, Lime, Wild Orange, Bergamot — citrus oils for diffuser blends (per the official tip)
🔹 Citrus oils have similar properties and are often interchangeable

## Fun Facts
👑 Kumquat is a small shrub native to Asia
👑 The olive-sized fruit is eaten whole: the peel is sweet and the flesh is sour
👑 Limonene is also found in lemon, lime and wild orange

## Cautions
> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas. Avoid sunlight or UV exposure for 12 hours after applying to the skin (citrus oil).
