# doTERRA Protein

**Category:** Dietary Supplements

**Type:** Dietary Supplement (protein powder, 3 options: Whey Chocolate, Whey Vanilla, Vegan)


---

## Short Description

👑 Protein powder for daily diet — 21-22 g of protein per serving, no added sugar
👑 Available in three options: Whey Chocolate, Whey Vanilla, and Vegan (plant-based)
👑 Each serving also contains 7-8 g of fiber to support digestion and satiety

## Ingredients and Key Ingredients

▫️ Whey versions: pasture-raised whey protein concentrate, calcium caseinate
▫️ Vegan version: pea, brown rice, pumpkin seed, and sacha inchi protein
▫️ Flaxseed flour, oat flour — a source of fiber in both versions
▫️ Sweeteners: monk fruit extract and stevia, no added sugar

## Indications

🔹 Muscle growth and recovery: post-workout support
🔹 Appetite control: fiber and protein support a feeling of fullness
🔹 Energy: protein is a building block for cellular energy (ATP)
🔹 Immune function: protein is essential for supporting the body's immune response

## Directions for Use

🔹 Mix one scoop with 240 ml (8 oz) of water or milk of your choice until smooth
🔹 Add to fruit and vegetable smoothies for flavor and extra benefits
🔹 For a unique flavor, add one drop of essential oil (e.g., Wild Orange)
