# Caraway

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Essential Oil (Single)


---

## Short Description
👑 An oil from caraway seed (Carum carvi), steam-distilled from crushed seeds
👑 Officially described as possibly supporting the gastrointestinal system when ingested
👑 Its distinct scent comes from D-carvone

## Composition and Key Ingredients
▫️ Carum carvi (caraway) essential oil, seed
▫️ The main scent compound is D-carvone; the official page names no other compounds

## Indications
🔹 Official support for the gastrointestinal system — per the doTERRA page description
🔹 Culinary use as a spice oil: caraway flavor is typical of German and Bavarian cooking (sauerkraut, rye bread, sausages)

## Directions for Use
🔹 The official page gives no usage instructions
🔹 Internal use: per the knowledge-base rules this is a higher-risk category (only if the bottle carries a “Supplement Facts” panel); the bot does not give dosages — such questions are routed to a specialist
🔹 No recommendations are given for aromatic or topical use — check with a specialist

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products
🔹 Oregano — Symphony of the Cells lists Caraway among its alternatives (together with Thyme and Basil)
🔹 Related Apiaceae plants: Dill, Fennel, Coriander, Cumin

## Fun Facts
👑 Caraway belongs to the carrot family (Apiaceae) and is related to dill, fennel, anise and cumin
👑 Ancient Egyptians used caraway in herbal preparations
👑 The seed is a popular spice in Europe and India
👑 On the official page: Europe's product of the month (May 2022), not for individual sale, shipped from the EU warehouse

## Cautions
> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
