# Passion Touch

**Category:** Rollers (Touch)

**Type:** Essential oil blend, Touch (10 ml roll-on)


---

## Short Description

👑 Warm spicy-floral blend for inspiration
👑 Restores interest in favorite activities and hobbies
👑 Suitable as a personal fragrance

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Fractionated Coconut Oil
▫️ Cardamom, Cinnamon, Ginger
▫️ Clove, Sandalwood, Jasmine Absolute
▫️ Vanilla Absolute, Damiana

## Indications

🔹 Feelings of boredom or indifference, need for inspiration for new endeavors

## Directions for Use

🔹 Rub over the heart area
🔹 Apply to wrists to inspire creativity
🔹 Use as a personal fragrance on the neck and wrists

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products

🔹 Complements the original Passion blend in 15 ml format

## Interesting Facts

👑 The formula is rich in phenols and esters thanks to spicy oils
👑 Created to bring a sense of joy back into everyday tasks

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
