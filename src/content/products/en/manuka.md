# Manuka

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Essential Oil (Single, 5 ml)


---

## Short Description
👑 A rich, spicy, sweet and herbaceous aroma from New Zealand
👑 Officially described as promoting a soothing environment when diffused and refreshing
👑 Can be added to skincare for the appearance of smooth, healthy-looking skin

## Composition and Key Ingredients
▫️ Leptospermum scoparium (manuka) essential oil, flowers, leaves and stems; steam distilled
▫️ Main components: leptospermone, E-calamenene, alpha-pinene, cadina-3,5-diene

## Indications
🔹 A soothing, refreshing atmosphere when diffused
🔹 Skincare — for the appearance of smooth, healthy-looking skin (per the official description)
🔹 Grounding and centering energy before meditation (tip from the doTERRA page)

## Directions for Use
🔹 Aromatic: 3-4 drops in a diffuser; a room or linen spray with water; 2-3 drops on a terracotta plate before bedtime
🔹 Topical: 1-2 drops on the desired area, diluted with a carrier oil; 1-2 drops can be added to a cleanser or toner
🔹 The official page does not mention internal use

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products
🔹 Peppermint, Bergamot — doTERRA suggests layering them with Manuka Touch (10 ml roll-on)
🔹 Manuka Touch — a 10 ml roll-on (not yet in the KB, can be added as a separate file)
🔹 Tea Tree, Eucalyptus — plants from the same myrtle family

## Fun Facts
👑 The Māori have long valued manuka for its invigorating properties; it has been used in New Zealand for centuries
👑 It belongs to the myrtle family, like tea tree and eucalyptus; it starts as a shrub and can grow into a tree of about 15 m
👑 Manuka honey comes from bees that pollinate the flowers of this tree

## Cautions
> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
