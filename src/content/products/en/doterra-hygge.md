# dōTERRA Hygge® — Cozy Blend (15 mL)

**Category:** Essential Oil Blends

**Type:** Seasonal winter essential oil blend (15 mL, US & EU)

---

## Short Description

👑 Inspired by the Danish concept of *hygge* (coziness and contentment), this warm essential oil blend turns any room into a comforting winter sanctuary.
👑 Blends bright Wild Orange with grounding Cedarwood, Frankincense, Myrrh, Vetiver, Amyris, and Cistus.

## Key Ingredients

- **Wild Orange** — bright, uplifting sweetness
- **Cedarwood & Amyris** — warm, woody tranquility
- **Frankincense & Myrrh** — rich, grounding resinous notes
- **Vetiver & Cistus** — deep emotional calm and balance

## Directions for Use

- **Aromatic:** Add 3–5 drops to your diffuser during cozy evenings or family gatherings.
- **Topical:** Dilute 1–2 drops with Fractionated Coconut Oil and apply to wrists or bottoms of feet.

## Precautions

- Possible skin sensitivity. Avoid sunlight and UV rays for at least 12 hours after applying topically.
