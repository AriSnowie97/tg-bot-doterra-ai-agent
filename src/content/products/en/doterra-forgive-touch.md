# dōTERRA Forgive® Touch

**Category:** Blends

**Type:** Essential Oil Blend Touch (roll-on, 10 ml)

## Short Description

👑 Woody and herbaceous blend for feelings of relief and forgiveness
👑 Helps release resentment and move forward
👑 Convenient roll-on for daily self-care rituals

▫️ Spruce
▫️ Bergamot
▫️ Juniper Berry
▫️ Myrrh
▫️ Arborvitae
▫️ Nootka Wood
▫️ Thyme
▫️ Citronella

## Indications

🔹 Feelings of resentment, guilt, or anger
🔹 Supporting the practice of forgiving oneself and others
🔹 Creating an atmosphere of peace during meditation

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply over the heart while repeating positive affirmations
🔹 Use during meditation
🔹 Apply to pulse points throughout the day as part of a self-care routine

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 dōTERRA Forgive® — classic (neat) blend
🔹 Other Touch blends from the emotional line — Console, Peace, Cheer

## Fun Facts

👑 Aromatic description: herbaceous, fresh, woody
👑 Thyme contains thymol — a component linked in preliminary studies to supporting skin health

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas. Avoid direct sunlight and UV rays for at least 12 hours after application.
