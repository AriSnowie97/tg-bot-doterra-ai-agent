# Jasmine (Absolute)

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Essential oil (absolute, pre-diluted, 15 mL)


---

## Short Description

👑 Royal floral absolute for mood balance
👑 Inspires feelings of joy, peace, and self-confidence
👑 Protects the skin and promotes a radiant complexion

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Jasminum grandiflorum (Jasmine) absolute, pre-diluted with a carrier oil

## Indications

🔹 Mood balancing throughout the month
🔹 Skincare for a radiant look
🔹 Personal perfume

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply directly to the skin for mood balance
🔹 Use as part of facial skincare routine

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Jasmine Touch — roll-on version in a larger format (10 mL)

## Fun Facts

👑 Jasmine is traditionally called the "King of Flowers" in the perfume industry
👑 Jasmine flowers are too delicate for classic steam distillation, so the oil is obtained through an extraction method (absolute)

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
