# Fractionated Coconut Oil

**Category:** Skin & Hair Care → Body Care

**Type:** carrier body oil


---

## Brief Description

👑 Fractionated Coconut Oil is a carrier oil for diluting essential oils and body care
👑 Light, colorless, odorless; fully soluble with all doTERRA essential oils
👑 Leaves no greasy residue and does not clog pores

## Ingredients and Key Components

▫️ Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride — main component obtained by fractionating coconut oil
▫️ 100% plant-based origin (Cocos nucifera)

## Indications

🔹 Carrier oil for dilution: reduces skin sensitivity when applying concentrated essential oils
🔹 Moisturizing: softens dry skin on hands, legs, elbows
🔹 Massage: ideal texture for massage blends with essential oils
🔹 Long shelf life: does not solidify and lasts almost indefinitely

## Directions for Use

🔹 For dilution: 1 part essential oil to 5 or more parts Fractionated Coconut Oil — depending on skin sensitivity
🔹 Topically: apply to the skin for moisturizing without interfering with the aroma of other blends
🔹 For massage: mix with favorite essential oils and apply to the neck and shoulders

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Universal base ingredient for almost all topical doTERRA essential oil recipes
🔹 Frequently used together with Deep Blue, AromaTouch, and other massage blends

## Interesting Facts

👑 Fractionation removes long-chain triglycerides, which cause regular coconut oil to solidify at room temperature
👑 The fractionation process also increases the proportion of caprylic and capric acids, which are beneficial for skin health
👑 Main chemical component — Caprylic/capric triglyceride

## Cautions

> ⚠️ For external use only. Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor’s care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.

## Additional Information

🔹 Before use, test the product on a small area of skin. If there is a history of allergic reactions, consult a dermatologist before use.
