# Lavender Touch

**Category:** Roll-ons (Touch)

**Type:** Touch Essential Oil (roll-on, 10 ml)


---

## Brief Description

👑 Pre-diluted version of the most popular Lavender oil
👑 Ideal for children and sensitive skin
👑 Soothes the body and relaxes the mind

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Fractionated Coconut Oil
▫️ Lavender Essential Oil (Lavandula angustifolia)

## Indications

🔹 Calming a restless child before bedtime
🔹 Relieving minor skin irritations
🔹 Grounding effect on pulse points

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply to temples or the back of the neck
🔹 Apply to the bottoms of a child's feet before bedtime
🔹 Apply to pulse points throughout the day

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Lavender (15 ml) — classic undiluted version

## Fun Facts

👑 Sourced mostly from Bulgaria, where lavender is distilled within 24 hours of harvesting

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
