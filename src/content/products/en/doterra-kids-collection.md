# doTERRA Kids Collection — Overview

**Category:** Essential oils

**Type:** Blend (a line of 7 pre-diluted blends for children)


---

## Brief Description

👑 Seven blends specifically formulated for the needs of a child's body, mind, and emotions
👑 All pre-diluted in Fractionated Coconut Oil — ready to use without additional dilution
👑 Gentle on sensitive children's skin while maintaining the powerful effects of the components

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Copaiba, Lavender, Spearmint, Zanthoxylum (in Rescuer)
▫️ Amyris, Balsam Fir, Coriander, Magnolia (in Steady)
▫️ Spearmint, Japanese Mint, Ginger (in Tamer)
▫️ Zdravetz and other natural components of the blends

## Indications

🔹 Rescuer — for tired legs or muscles after an active day
🔹 Steady — for grounding when feeling overwhelmed
🔹 Brave — to support confidence in new or exciting situations
🔹 Thinker — to support focus during studying or completing tasks
🔹 Stronger — to support children's immunity
🔹 Calmer — for peaceful falling asleep in the evening
🔹 Tamer — for stomach comfort and motion sickness during travel

## Directions for Use

🔹 Rescuer — apply to legs or shoulders after an active day or workout
🔹 Steady — apply to the back of the neck or wrists when feeling overwhelmed
🔹 Brave — apply to the chest or wrists before a new or scary situation
🔹 Thinker — apply to temples or the back of the neck while studying
🔹 Calmer — apply to the bottoms of feet or chest before bed
🔹 Tamer — apply to the abdomen for stomach discomfort or motion sickness during travel
🔹 Aromatic method: each blend can be diffused in a child's bedroom or classroom
🔹 Internal method: not recommended for internal consumption — the blends are intended exclusively for aromatic and topical application

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Children's bedroom in the evening: 2 drops Calmer, 1 drop Lavender
🔹 Morning before school: 2 drops Thinker, 1 drop Wild Orange
🔹 Soothing after an active day: 2 drops Rescuer

## Interesting Facts

👑 The Kids Collection line was developed specifically for the development of a child's body, mind, and emotions — the company emphasizes: these are not just "kids' versions" of adult products
👑 All seven blends come in a convenient roller bottle format — easy to apply even for young children under adult supervision
👑 Tamer is the newest blend in the line, added as a gentler alternative to adult DigestZen specifically for children
👑 Zdravetz (in Rescuer) is a rare Bulgarian plant whose name translates to "health"
👑 Each blend was formulated by a separate team of doTERRA perfumers and scientists specifically for children's needs

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Even with pre-dilution — avoid the eyes and sensitive areas of a child's skin. Use only under adult supervision. Keep out of reach of children. If irritation occurs, discontinue use. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician.
> ⚠️ The information provided is intended strictly for informational purposes and does not replace consultation with a pediatrician.
