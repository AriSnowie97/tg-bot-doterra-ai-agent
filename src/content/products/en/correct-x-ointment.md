# Correct-X® Essential Ointment

**Category:** Personal Care

**Type:** Ointment

---

## Short Description

👑 Multi-purpose ointment to soothe irritated skin
👑 Creates a moisture barrier while soothing and hydrating
👑 Petroleum-free and preservative-free, suitable for sensitive skin

## Composition & Key Ingredients

▫️ Essential oils of Frankincense, Helichrysum, Tea Tree, Cedarwood, and Lavender
▫️ Bisabolol — a soothing component
▫️ Phellodendron amurense bark extract
▫️ Jojoba oil

## Indications

🔹 Minor skin irritations and damage
🔹 Need to restore the natural, healthy state of the skin

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply a small amount of ointment to fingers
🔹 Gently rub into the skin or hair on the area needing attention

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Essential Skin Care, Veráge — for general facial skin care

## Interesting Facts

👑 Helichrysum is traditionally used in anti-aging products for its restorative properties
👑 Bisabolol is known for its soothing effect and supporting healthy-looking skin

## Cautions

> ⚠️ For external use only. Avoid direct contact with eyes. If skin irritation occurs, discontinue use. Keep out of reach of children.
