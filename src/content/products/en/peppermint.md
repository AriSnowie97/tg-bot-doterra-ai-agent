## Short Description

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Menthol (40–55%) — provides the characteristic cooling effect by interacting with cold receptors in the skin
▫️ Menthone (15–25%)
▫️ Menthyl acetate
▫️ Neomenthol
▫️ Eucalyptol (1,8-cineole)
▫️ Peppermint essential oil (Mentha × piperita), obtained by steam distillation from leaves and stems

## Indications

🔹 Headaches and migraines: Applying to temples, forehead, and back of the neck relieves tension and reduces pain intensity within 15-20 minutes
🔹 Nausea and vomiting: Inhaling or applying to wrists helps with nausea during pregnancy, motion sickness, and post-chemotherapy
🔹 Digestive issues: Relieves bloating, gas, intestinal spasms, and irritable bowel syndrome
🔹 Fatigue and drowsiness: Quickly increases energy levels and alertness — a great alternative to caffeine
🔹 Nasal congestion and colds: Expectorant and bronchodilator action eases breathing during colds, sinusitis, and allergies

🔹 Focus and memory: Research shows that inhaling peppermint improves attention, information processing speed, and memory

## Directions for Use

🔹 **Aromatic use:** Add 3-4 drops to a diffuser for alertness and improved concentration; inhale directly from the cap for nausea or dizziness; apply a drop to palms, rub together, and inhale before an important meeting or exam
🔹 **Topical use:** Apply 1-2 drops (diluted 1:1 with carrier oil) to temples and forehead for headaches; apply to neck and chest for nasal congestion and cold symptoms; dilute and apply to sore muscles and joints after workouts; apply a drop to wrists for nausea or during travel
🔹 **Internal use:** Take 1-2 drops with water or in a capsule for digestive issues, fresh breath, or an energy boost

### Research
👑 **Efficacy of Peppermint Oil in Tension Headache** (Cephalalgia (International Journal of Headache), 1996)
A double-blind study showed that applying a 10% peppermint oil solution to the forehead and temples was as effective as taking 1000 mg of paracetamol for tension headaches.
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8909356/

👑 **Menthol Reduces Postoperative Nausea** (Journal of PeriAnesthesia Nursing, 2014)
Inhaling peppermint oil significantly reduced nausea and vomiting in patients during the early postoperative period — an effective and safe alternative to pharmaceutical antiemetics.
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24373889/

👑 **Impact of Peppermint Oil on Irritable Bowel Syndrome** (Journal of Gastroenterology, 2014)
A meta-analysis of 9 randomized controlled trials confirmed that peppermint oil capsules significantly reduce abdominal pain and IBS symptoms compared to placebo.
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25490346/

🔹 For children ages 6-10, use only in high dilution (1:10 or 1:20 with a carrier oil) on the back and feet
🔹 Avoid applying near the eyes and mucous membranes
🔹 May reduce the effectiveness of certain homeopathic remedies

🔹 Always dilute with a carrier oil if you have sensitive skin
