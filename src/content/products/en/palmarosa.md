# Palmarosa

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Essential Oil (Single)


---

## Short Description
👑 A delicate rose-like aroma — the "Indian geranium"
👑 Known for its natural skin benefits: high in geraniol
👑 Can support a healthy and youthful look of the skin when added to doTERRA skincare products

## Composition and Key Ingredients
▫️ Cymbopogon martinii (palmarosa) essential oil, leaves and stems of the grass, steam distilled
▫️ High in geraniol

## Indications
🔹 Skincare — a healthy, youthful look (per the official description)
🔹 Creating a welcoming atmosphere when diffused
🔹 A relaxing massage when diluted

## Directions for Use
🔹 Aromatic: diffuse
🔹 Topical: dilute in a carrier oil for massage; 2 drops in an Epsom salt bath; combine with a doTERRA facial cleanser
🔹 The official page does not mention internal use

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products
🔹 Geranium, Rose — oils with a similar rosy profile
🔹 doTERRA skincare (Essential Skin Care, Veráge) — for adding a drop to a cleanser

## Fun Facts
👑 doTERRA sources the oil from Nepal
👑 The grass is native to Southeast Asia and grows well in humid, wetland environments
👑 The species is Cymbopogon martinii and it is also known as "Indian geranium"

## Cautions
> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas. Book "Aromalogiya": palmarosa can irritate the digestive mucosa — avoid on an empty stomach, mix with fats or food.

## Additional Information
Books in the knowledge base:
▫️ "Aromalogiya": palmarosa is among the oils that irritate the GI mucosa (with anise, bay, geranium, ginger, citronella, thyme and others)
Note: the official page does not state the bottle size and does not label the oil as limited edition.
