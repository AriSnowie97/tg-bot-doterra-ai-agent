# Cumin

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Essential Oil (Single, limited edition)


---

## Short Description
👑 A warm, peppery, toasty-sweet oil from cumin seed (Cuminum cyminum)
👑 Officially described as adding flavor to savory cooking, suitable for a diffuser and giving a warming sensation on the skin
👑 A limited-time item — offered as part of the Sunsational Flavors Kit promotion

## Composition and Key Ingredients
▫️ Cuminum cyminum (cumin) essential oil, seed
▫️ Cuminaldehyde creates the warming sensation on the skin

## Indications
🔹 Culinary use: a spicy accent for savory dishes
🔹 A scent for the diffuser (blends well with Cilantro and Coriander)
🔹 A warming sensation when applied to the skin

## Directions for Use
🔹 Aromatic: in a diffuser; blends well with Cilantro and Coriander
🔹 Topical: the official page gives no amount or dilution; Cumin is a "hot" oil, so per the KB rules only strongly diluted in a carrier oil
🔹 Internal use: per the knowledge-base rules this is a higher-risk category (only if the bottle carries a “Supplement Facts” panel); the bot does not give dosages — such questions are routed to a specialist

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products
🔹 Cilantro, Coriander — for diffuser blends
🔹 Oregano, Thyme, Basil — in the Symphony of the Cells substitution table Cumin is an alternative for Oregano

## Fun Facts
👑 Cumin seeds have been used since about 2000 B.C.
👑 Cumin is a small, slender annual herb of the Apiaceae family
👑 The oil was part of the Sunsational Flavors Kit promotion

## Cautions
> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas. Avoid sunlight or UV exposure for up to 12 hours after applying to the skin. Book "Essential ABC": cumin is a "hot" oil that must never be taken undiluted.
