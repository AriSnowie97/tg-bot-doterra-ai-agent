# Accessories — 10 mL Amber Roller Bottle 6 Pack

**Category:** Diffusers

**Type:** roller bottles for custom essential oil blends

---

## Short Description

👑 Set of six 10 mL amber glass roller bottles for custom essential oil blends
👑 The same glass and metal roller used in doTERRA Touch bottles
👑 Ideal for blending, storing, and personal application of essential oils

## Composition & Key Ingredients

▫️ Amber glass — protects essential oils from sunlight
▫️ Stainless steel roller — even application without wasting oil
▫️ 10 mL volume — convenient compact size for a bag or pocket

## Indications

🔹 Custom blends: allow you to create your own essential oil combinations with a carrier oil
🔹 Application convenience: the roller provides targeted application without direct hand contact with the oil
🔹 Portability: compact size is suitable for travel and everyday carry
🔹 Economy: diluted blend in a roller bottle is consumed slower than oil from a dropper

## Directions for Use

🔹 Fill the bottle approximately three-quarters full with a carrier oil (e.g., Fractionated Coconut Oil)
🔹 Add selected essential oils (typically 10-15 drops per 10 mL of carrier oil)
🔹 Snap on the roller and screw on the cap, invert several times to mix
🔹 Apply to desired areas of the skin — wrists, temples, neck

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Often used with Fractionated Coconut Oil as a base
🔹 Suitable for any doTERRA essential oils and blends — Deep Blue, AromaTouch, custom recipes

## Fun Facts

👑 Amber glass color is traditionally used for storing essential oils because it blocks ultraviolet rays
👑 The same type of bottle and roller is used in pre-made doTERRA Touch line products
👑 Roller bottles are one of the most popular accessories among users who create their own recipes

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor’s care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.

## Additional Information

- **Website category:** Diffusers & Accessories → Accessories
- **Additional usage instructions:** Dilute concentrated essential oils thoroughly with a carrier oil before applying to skin. Label bottles for convenience and safety. Wash and dry bottles before reusing with a new blend.
