# Douglas Fir

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Essential Oil (single, 15 mL)


---

## Short Description

👑 Woody oil for skin cleansing and easy breathing
👑 Refreshes and purifies the air at home
👑 Calming forest aroma

## Ingredients and Key Components

▫️ Pseudotsuga menziesii (Douglas Fir) essential oil

## Indications

🔹 Skin cleansing
🔹 Supporting easy breathing
🔹 Refreshing the air at home

## Directions for Use

🔹 Diffuse to purify the air
🔹 Apply topically to the chest for easy breathing

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Shinrin-Yoku® — forest woody oils for "forest bathing"

## Fun Facts

👑 One of North America's tallest coniferous trees

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
