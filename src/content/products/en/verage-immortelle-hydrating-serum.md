# Veráge Immortelle Hydrating Serum

**Category:** Skin & Hair Care → Veráge Skin Care

**Type:** hydrating serum


---

## Short Description

👑 Hydrating serum formulated with a lipid complex that mimics the lipids of young, healthy skin
👑 Combines six essential oils: Frankincense, Hawaiian Sandalwood, Lavender, Myrrh, Helichrysum, Rose
👑 Deeply hydrates and reduces the appearance of fine lines

## Ingredients & Key Components

▫️ Jojoba and Macadamia oil esters
▫️ Frankincense essential oil (Boswellia carterii/sacra/papyrifera/frereana)
▫️ Hawaiian Sandalwood essential oil (Santalum paniculatum)
▫️ Lavender, Myrrh, Helichrysum, Rose essential oils
▫️ Squalane, phytosterols, tocopherol

## Indications

🔹 Deep hydration: lipid complex supports the skin's natural barrier
🔹 Anti-aging care: reduces the appearance of fine lines and promotes smoothness
🔹 Skin balance: supports optimal lipid balance for a radiant look
🔹 Suitable for all skin types and ages

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply to clean face and neck in an upward motion
🔹 Use morning and evening
🔹 Follow with Veráge Moisturizer to complete your skincare ritual

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Veráge Cleanser and Veráge Toner — preliminary steps in the Veráge line
🔹 Veráge Moisturizer — final step to lock in hydration

## Interesting Facts

👑 The name Immortelle refers to the flower that retains its shape even after drying — a symbol of lasting beauty
👑 The lipid complex is formulated to mimic the lipid structure of skin at its peak of health and youth
👑 Contains six essential oils at once — a rarity for a cosmetic product

## Cautions

🔹 For external use only
🔹 Avoid direct contact with eyes
🔹 Discontinue use if skin irritation occurs
🔹 Keep out of reach of children

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
