# dōTERRA On Guard® Mouth Rinse

**Category:** Home Products

**Type:** Mouth Rinse


---

## Short Description

👑 Alcohol-free mouth rinse formulated with the On Guard protective blend
👑 Cleans teeth and gums, reducing plaque
👑 Freshens breath and supports a healthy-looking mouth

## Ingredients and Key Components

▫️ On Guard® Essential Oil Blend (Wild Orange, Clove, Cinnamon, Eucalyptus, Rosemary)
▫️ Myrrh — an additional essential oil for comprehensive care
▫️ Miswak Extract — for fresh breath and clean teeth
▫️ Xylitol — a natural sweetener

## Indications

🔹 Daily oral hygiene as a complement to tooth brushing
🔹 Need for an alcohol-free mouth rinse that does not dry out the mouth

## Directions for Use

🔹 Shake the bottle thoroughly before use
🔹 Measure the dose using the measuring cap
🔹 Rinse thoroughly for at least 60 seconds, then spit out; do not rinse with water
🔹 Use morning and night after brushing teeth

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 dōTERRA On Guard® Natural Whitening Toothpaste — for comprehensive oral care

## Interesting Facts

👑 Unlike many mouthwashes, the formula is alcohol-free, preventing dryness in the mouth
👑 The American Dental Association (ADA) notes that mouth rinse is a beneficial addition to brushing and flossing

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Do not swallow. Keep out of reach of children. Avoid contact with eyes.
