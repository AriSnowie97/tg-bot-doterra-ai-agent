# doTERRA Balance — Grounding Blend

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Blend (Grounding Blend)


---

## Short Description

👑 A grounding blend that helps calm anxious feelings and create an atmosphere of tranquility
👑 A warm, woody aroma that combines Spruce, Ho Wood, Frankincense, Blue Tansy, Blue Chamomile, and Osmanthus
👑 One of the few doTERRA products pre-diluted in Fractionated Coconut Oil — ready for skin application without additional dilution

## Ingredients and Key Components

▫️ Black Spruce (Picea mariana), Ho Wood (Cinnamomum camphora ct. linalool), Frankincense (Boswellia), Blue Tansy (Tanacetum annuum), Blue Chamomile (Matricaria chamomilla), Osmanthus (Osmanthus fragrans) in Fractionated Coconut Oil
▫️ Ho Wood is rich in linalool — a compound with pronounced calming properties, related to the cinnamon family

## Indications

🔹 Anxious states: helps calm anxious thoughts and create a sense of stability during stressful moments
🔹 Meditation and yoga: diffusing during practice creates a peaceful environment for focus
🔹 Children's sleep: a gentle foot massage with doTERRA Balance before bedtime helps children calm down
🔹 Work stress: applying to the wrists before a difficult meeting or on the road helps maintain calmness
🔹 Emotional balance throughout the day: morning application to the feet is a popular ritual for a stable mood

## Directions for Use

🔹 Add 3-4 drops to a diffuser to create a calm atmosphere at home or in the car, or apply a drop to palms, rub together, cup over face, and take several deep breaths
🔹 Apply 1-2 drops to wrists, neck, or bottoms of feet in the morning for grounding throughout the day (the oil is already diluted)

👑 One of doTERRA's most popular products of all time

The blend combines woody, coniferous, and floral oils from various regions of the world — from North American Black Spruce to Asian Osmanthus. During steam distillation, Blue Tansy and Blue Chamomile acquire their characteristic blue color due to chamazulene.
