# Spearmint

**Category:** Essential oils

**Type:** Essential oil (single, 15 ml)


---

## Short Description

👑 Mint with a sweet note for digestive comfort
👑 Similar to peppermint, but milder
👑 Refreshing breath

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Mentha spicata (spearmint) essential oil

## Indications

🔹 Relief of occasional stomach discomfort
🔹 Support for healthy digestion

## Directions for Use

🔹 Internally: 1 drop in water or a capsule
🔹 Add to chewing gum/toothpaste for freshness

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 dōTERRA SuperMint® Beadlets — spearmint is included in the formula

## Interesting Facts

👑 A milder alternative to Peppermint for those sensitive to menthol

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
