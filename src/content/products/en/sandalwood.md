# Sandalwood

**Category:** Essential oils

**Type:** Essential oil (single, 15 ml)


---

## Short description

👑 Woody oil for hair and skin restoration
👑 Promotes relaxation and preparation for a restful sleep
👑 Rich, warm aroma for meditation

## Composition and key ingredients

▫️ Santalum album (Indian Sandalwood) essential oil

## Indications

🔹 Restoring moisture to hair
🔹 Relaxation and sleep preparation

## Directions for use

🔹 Add to shampoo for hair restoration
🔹 Diffuse before sleep

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products

🔹 Console® Touch — contains sandalwood

## Interesting facts

👑 doTERRA also offers a Hawaiian species — 'Iliahi (Hawaiian Sandalwood)

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
