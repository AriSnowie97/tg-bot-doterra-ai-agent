# Myrrh

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Essential Oil (single, 15 ml)


---

## Short Description

👑 Resinous oil for emotional balance
👑 Excellent oil for massage
👑 Cleansing effect for the mouth and throat

## Ingredients and Key Components

▫️ Commiphora myrrha (myrrh) essential oil

## Indications

🔹 Emotional balance and mindfulness
🔹 Oral cavity and throat care

## Directions for Use

🔹 Massage application with a carrier oil
🔹 Rinsing with a diluted solution for oral hygiene

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products

🔹 Myrrh Touch — roll-on version
🔹 On Guard Mouth Rinse — contains myrrh

## Interesting Facts

👑 Resin from the Commiphora tree was traditionally used in ancient rituals

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
