# Vetiver Touch

**Category:** Roll-ons (Touch)

**Type:** Touch Essential Oil (roll-on, 10 ml)


---

## Short Description

👑 Pre-diluted version of grounding Vetiver oil
👑 Softens and hydrates the skin
👑 Ideal for an evening skincare routine or massage

## Ingredients & Key Components

▫️ Fractionated Coconut Oil
▫️ Vetiver Essential Oil (Vetiveria zizanioides)

## Indications

🔹 Grounding and calming before sleep
🔹 Skin hydration as part of an evening routine

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply to wrists, temples, neck, or feet before sleep
🔹 Use in a soothing post-workout massage

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products

🔹 Vetiver (15 ml) — classic undiluted version

## Interesting Facts

👑 Vetiver is known as the "fragrance of the soil" and the "oil of tranquility"
👑 About half of the world's vetiver production comes from Haiti

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
