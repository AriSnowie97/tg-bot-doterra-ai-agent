# Madagascar Vanilla

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Essential Oil (Single, Absolute, Pre-diluted)


---

## Brief Description

👑 Luxurious vanilla absolute for a warm, sweet aroma
👑 Ideal personal perfume and addition to cosmetics
👑 Delicious addition to beverages and desserts

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Vanilla planifolia absolute in Fractionated Coconut Oil

## Indications

🔹 Personal perfume
🔹 Beverage flavoring
🔹 Creating a cozy, relaxing atmosphere

## Directions for Use

🔹 Diffusion: 3-4 drops
🔹 Internal use: 1 drop in a beverage
🔹 Topically as a perfume or addition to lotion

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Console® — vanilla absolute is included in similar blends

## Fun Facts

👑 Obtained through a two-step CO2 extraction process from beans grown in Madagascar
👑 Pre-diluted with Fractionated Coconut Oil for convenient application

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
