# Zendocrine® — Detoxification Blend

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Blend (Detoxification Blend)


---

## Short Description
👑 A fresh, sharp, cleansing blend of five oils: Tangerine, Rosemary, Geranium, Juniper Berry and Cilantro
👑 Officially described as supporting the body's natural process of eliminating unwanted substances
👑 The previous formula of doTERRA's detox blend — its successor in newer catalogs is RevitaZen™ (see its own KB file)

## Composition and Key Ingredients
▫️ Tangerine (Citrus reticulata, peel), Rosemary (Rosmarinus officinalis, leaf), Geranium (Pelargonium graveolens), Juniper Berry (Juniperus communis), Cilantro (Coriandrum sativum, herb)
▫️ Unlike RevitaZen™, Zendocrine contains no Ginger

## Indications
🔹 Support for the body's natural process of eliminating unwanted substances (per the official description)
🔹 Flavoring for water — a use the official page mentions
🔹 Freshening the air: a fresh, sharp, "cleansing" aroma when diffused

## Directions for Use
🔹 Aromatic: 3-4 drops in a diffuser
🔹 Internal: the official page mentions adding 1-2 drops to food; per the knowledge-base rules internal use is a higher-risk category (only if the bottle carries a "Supplement Facts" panel), the bot does not give dosages and routes such questions to a specialist
🔹 Topical: the official page gives no instructions — by the general KB rules only diluted

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products
🔹 Zendocrine® Detoxification Complex — the capsule form (separate KB file); in the "Symphony of the Cells" reference cards the blend and the capsules are listed together for the digestive system
🔹 RevitaZen™ Detoxification Blend — the updated formula (with Ginger)
🔹 Single oils from the blend: Rosemary, Geranium, Juniper Berry, Cilantro, Tangerine

## Fun Facts
👑 Its Ukrainian name in the books is "Зендокрин"; the "Symphony of the Cells" glossary lists it as the Detoxification Blend
👑 In the "Symphony of the Cells" substitution table Zendocrine is listed as a replacement for Geranium (next to Copaiba and Rose) and for Juniper Berry (next to Coriander and Celery Seed)
👑 doTERRA pages for several markets (for example South Africa, the UAE, Israel, Kazakhstan) still present Zendocrine as a separate product

## Cautions
> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
