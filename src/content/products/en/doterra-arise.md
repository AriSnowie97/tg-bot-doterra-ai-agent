# doTERRA Arise — Enlightening Blend

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Blend (Enlightening Blend, part of the Yoga Collection)


---

## Brief Description

👑 An inspiring blend that promotes a joyful and courageous atmosphere at the end of practice
👑 A bright, sweet citrus-floral aroma — Lemon, Grapefruit, Siberian Fir, Osmanthus, Melissa
👑 Ready to use in a roll-on bottle without additional dilution

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Lemon (Citrus limon)
▫️ Grapefruit (Citrus paradisi)
▫️ Siberian Fir (Abies sibirica) — adds a fresh, piney note that balances the sweetness of the citrus and floral components
▫️ Osmanthus (Osmanthus fragrans) — a rare flower from Asia that gives the composition a soft, apricot-leathery depth
▫️ Melissa (Melissa officinalis) — traditionally used for its ability to uplift mood and bring clarity

## Indications

🔹 End of practice: ideal for the final portion of a yoga session
🔹 Joy and courage: helps foster an uplifting feeling after physical exertion
🔹 Striving for goals: supports progress toward reaching your full potential and self-improvement
🔹 Stability and freedom: balances a sense of stability with a feeling of lightness

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply to diffusing jewelry at the end of practice for a bright, inspiring aroma
🔹 Diffuse during meditation for a bright, uplifting atmosphere
🔹 Apply to temples, wrists, and neck at the end of practice
🔹 Combine with strong standing poses, such as Half Moon
🔹 Not recommended for internal use — the blend is intended for aromatic and topical application

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Pairs well with other products in the Yoga Collection (Anchor, Align)
🔹 Morning inspiration (diffuser): 2 drops of Arise, 1 drop of Wild Orange
🔹 End of practice (diffuser): 3 drops of Arise

## Interesting Facts

👑 Arise is the concluding blend of the Yoga Collection, designed specifically for the finale of practice and moments of self-improvement
👑 The company recommends pairing the blend with strong standing poses, such as Half Moon
👑 Combining citrus with piney Siberian Fir is an unusual and refreshing choice for a concluding blend

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas. Avoid sunlight or UV rays for up to 12 hours after applying product to the skin (citrus components).
