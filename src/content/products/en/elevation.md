# Elevation™ — Joyful Blend

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Blend (Joyful Blend, 15 ml)


---

## Short Description
👑 A floral-citrus blend of 11 oils with a sweet, uplifting aroma
👑 Officially described as an energizing, refreshing aroma that promotes a revitalizing environment
👑 Designed to "elevate" your mood and the space around you

## Composition and Key Ingredients
▫️ 11 essential oils: Lavandin (flower), Tangerine (peel), Lavender (flower/leaf), Amyris (bark), Clary Sage, Hawaiian Sandalwood, Ylang Ylang, Ho Wood, Osmanthus, Lemon Myrtle, Melissa
▫️ Latin names are not given on the official page

## Indications
🔹 Lifting the mood and freshening the atmosphere at home or at work
🔹 An uplifting aromatic accent through the day
🔹 Unwinding in a bath after a stressful day

## Directions for Use
🔹 Aromatic: 3-4 drops in a diffuser at any time of day
🔹 Topical: 1-2 drops on the desired area (wrists, temples, over the heart), dilute with a carrier oil to minimize skin sensitivity
🔹 Bath: add a few drops (first mixed with salt or a carrier oil) after a long, stressful day
🔹 The official page does not mention internal use

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products
🔹 Cheer®, Console® — other blends from the "Sadness / Grief" theme of "Emotions and Essential Oils"
🔹 Lavender, Tangerine, Clary Sage — listed as Elevation alternatives in the Symphony of the Cells substitution table
🔹 Melissa, Ylang Ylang, Hawaiian Sandalwood, Lemon Myrtle — single oils from the blend

## Fun Facts
👑 The blend contains rare oils — Melissa, Osmanthus, Hawaiian Sandalwood and Lemon Myrtle
👑 The "Symphony of the Cells" glossary calls Elevation the Invigorating Blend
👑 It appears on official pages for South Africa, Brazil and Australia — check availability in your region

## Cautions
> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
