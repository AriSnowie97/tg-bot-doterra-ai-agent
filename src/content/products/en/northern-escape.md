# Northern Escape — Woodland Blend

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Blend (Woodland Blend)


---

## Short Description

👑 A woodland blend that transports you to the atmosphere of a peaceful winter forest
👑 Coniferous, fresh, clean aroma — Black Spruce, Siberian and Balsam Fir, Lavandin, Cedarwood, Cypress, Hinoki, Frankincense, Nootka, Cananga, Clove
👑 Eleven different oils simultaneously — one of the most complex compositions in the doTERRA portfolio

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Black Spruce (Picea mariana), Siberian Fir (Abies sibirica), Balsam Fir (Abies balsamea), Lavandin (Lavandula hybrida), Cedarwood (Cedrus atlantica), Cypress (Cupressus sempervirens), Hinoki (Chamaecyparis obtusa), Frankincense (Boswellia), Nootka (Callitropsis nootkatensis), Cananga (Cananga odorata), Clove (Eugenia caryophyllata)
▫️ Chemical profile is rich in α-pinene and bornyl acetate

## Indications

🔹 Recovery after a hectic day: diffusing creates an atmosphere of peace and harmony at home
🔹 Refreshing space: coniferous aroma refreshes the office, kitchen, car, or any room
🔹 Grounding: helps return to a feeling of stability and connection with nature
🔹 Skin care: adding to lotion provides a soothing effect for the skin

## Directions for Use

🔹 Diffuse for a calm, harmonious atmosphere at home or apply a drop to palms, rub together, cover face, and inhale deeply
🔹 Add a drop to body lotion or cream, or apply to wrists or neck for a personal woodland scent

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 With Wild Orange (Winter Comfort: 3 drops Northern Escape, 1 drop Wild Orange)

## Interesting Facts

👑 Northern Escape combines eleven different oils — one of the most multi-component blends in the entire doTERRA portfolio
👑 The aroma is described as an "escape to the forest where time doesn't matter" — the blend was developed specifically for an emotional shift away from the urban rhythm

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.

## Additional Information

👑 Impact on the physical body: grounding, invigorating; soothing for muscles; refreshing for the air; cleansing effect (according to component studies).
👑 Impact on the emotional level: removes the hustle of everyday life, feelings of being overwhelmed by the urban rhythm, internal restlessness; fosters harmony and calm, a sense of connection with nature, grounding.
👑 Origin and composition: Black Spruce is a powerful oil of the pine family, slightly sweeter than other conifers; Siberian Fir grows in the remote forests of Siberia. In vitro studies on the blend's components show promising results regarding cleansing properties.
👑 Diffuser blends: Forest Calm (4 drops Northern Escape), Winter Comfort (3 drops Northern Escape, 1 drop Wild Orange), Office Refresh (3 drops Northern Escape).
👑 More about application: Northern Escape was developed as a quick way to emotionally "escape" the hustle of everyday life. Researchers note the potential soothing effect of lavandin alongside the stimulating effect of Siberian fir and hinoki. The formula is well-suited for seasonal winter use.
