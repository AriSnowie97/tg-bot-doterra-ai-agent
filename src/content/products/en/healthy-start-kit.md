# Healthy Start Kit

**Category:** Kits

**Type:** Kit with diffuser

## Short Description

👑 The perfect start for those striving for a healthy lifestyle
👑 Combines the full Family Essentials Kit with the Pebble™ portable diffuser
👑 10 oils and blends + diffuser in one kit

▫️ Family Essentials Kit — 10 bottles of 5 ml each (Lavender, Lemon, Peppermint, Tea Tree, Oregano, Frankincense, Deep Blue®, dōTERRA Breathe®, DigestZen®, dōTERRA On Guard®)

## Indications

🔹 A comprehensive start into the world of essential oils with the ability to diffuse immediately

## Directions for Use

🔹 Use oils according to the reference guide, diffuse the selected oil in the Pebble Diffuser

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
