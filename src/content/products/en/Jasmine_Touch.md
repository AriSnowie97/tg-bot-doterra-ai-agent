## Short Description

👑 Jasmine absolute, known as the "King of Flowers"
👑 Calming yet uplifting aroma
👑 Promotes a radiant skin appearance

▫️ Fractionated Coconut Oil
▫️ Jasmine Absolute (Jasminum grandiflorum)

## Indications

🔹 Personal fragrance, morning mood ritual, targeted skin care

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply to pulse points and feet in the morning for a good mood
🔹 Gently massage onto the abdomen area for relaxation
🔹 Apply to skin areas twice daily

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Pairs well with Lavender Touch and Frankincense Touch for an evening ritual

## Interesting Facts

👑 It takes about six million flowers to produce one ton of jasmine absolute
👑 The flowers are hand-picked early in the morning in Egypt
