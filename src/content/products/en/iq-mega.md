**Type:** Dietary Supplement (Omega-3 supplement)

## Short Description

👑 Fish oil-based Omega-3 supplement with wild orange flavor instead of the typical "fishy" taste
👑 A source of DHA and EPA to support brain, cardiovascular, immune, and joint function
👑 Formulated for regular use by children and adults alongside a2z Chewable

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Fish oil, molecularly filtered and deodorized
▫️ 900 mg DHA and 400 mg EPA per serving (1,300 mg total omega-3)
▫️ Wild Orange essential oil (Citrus sinensis) for flavor
▫️ Antioxidants: rosemary extract, tocopherols, sunflower oil, and lecithin

## Indications

🔹 Brain function: DHA is a key structural component of the brain
🔹 Cardiovascular system: support for healthy lipid levels
🔹 Immune function: omega-3s support the body's inflammatory balance
🔹 Joints: additional support for joint function

## Directions for Use

🔹 1 teaspoon (5 mL) daily
🔹 Store away from direct heat and light
🔹 Refrigerate after opening and consume within 100 days
