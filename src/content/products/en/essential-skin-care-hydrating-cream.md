▫️ Lactococcus Ferment Lysate (probiotic)

🔹 Need for intense hydration
🔹 Skin fatigue and dullness
🔹 Fine lines

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply to clean face and neck in the morning and/or evening

## Interesting Facts

👑 Cocoa butter acts as an occlusive ingredient that locks moisture in the skin
👑 Probiotic lysate strengthens the skin's natural barrier

## Cautions
