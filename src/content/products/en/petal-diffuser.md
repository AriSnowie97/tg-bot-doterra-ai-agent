# Petal Diffuser 2.0

**Category:** Diffusers & Accessories → Essential Oil Diffusers

**Type:** Ultrasonic Diffuser


---

## Short Description

👑 Compact ultrasonic diffuser for home or office with soft lighting
👑 Diffuses an ultra-fine, yet rich mist covering an area up to 30 sq. m
👑 Three time settings: 2 and 6 hours continuous or 12 hours intermittent (5 min. ON / 5 min. OFF)

## Composition & Key Features

▫️ Simple three-piece design — easy to clean and assemble
▫️ Optional LED light (nightlight) or light-free mode
▫️ Mains powered: 100–240V AC
▫️ Materials: glass cover, polypropylene body

## Indications

🔹 Home aromatherapy: creates an atmosphere for relaxation, energy, or focus depending on the chosen oil
🔹 Nightlight: soft lighting is suitable for a nursery or bedroom
🔹 Office and small rooms: compact size fits well on a desktop
🔹 Ease of use: suitable for both beginners and experienced users

## How to Use

🔹 Fill with water up to the maximum level mark
🔹 Add 3–8 drops of chosen essential oil
🔹 Select operating mode (2, 6, or 12 hours intermittent) and light settings if desired
🔹 Clean periodically with a water and vinegar mixture to prevent oil buildup

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Pairs well with citrus oils (Wild Orange, Bergamot) for an invigorating morning
🔹 Or with Lavender, Sandalwood, or Roman Chamomile for evening relaxation
🔹 Included in the Petal Essentials Collection along with Wild Orange and Lavender

## Interesting Facts

👑 The diffuser is an ultrasonic type — it diffuses oil without heat, preserving its beneficial properties
👑 Regular cleaning of the diffuser prevents oil buildup that could interfere with device operation
👑 Petal is one of the most compact and affordable diffusers in the doTERRA lineup

## Cautions

> ⚠️ The information provided is for informational purposes only. Follow the operating instructions on the device packaging. Keep out of reach of children.

## Additional Information

**Precautions:**
🔹 Use only with water and essential oils — do not fill with other liquids
🔹 Place on a flat, stable surface away from the edge of the table
🔹 Do not leave unattended near small children or pets
🔹 Regularly clean the reservoir to prevent residue buildup
