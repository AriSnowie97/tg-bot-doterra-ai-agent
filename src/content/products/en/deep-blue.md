# Deep Blue — Soothing Blend

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Blend (Soothing Blend)


---

## Brief Description

👑 Soothing blend for muscles and joints with a simultaneous cooling and warming effect
👑 Minty-camphorous aroma combining wintergreen, camphor, peppermint, ylang ylang, helichrysum, blue tansy, blue chamomile, and osmanthus
👑 Favorite oil of massage therapists and athletes for caring for tired muscles

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Wintergreen — source of methyl salicylate, a component with a characteristic "cool" aroma and cooling effect
▫️ Blue tansy and blue chamomile acquire a rich blue color due to chamazulene during steam distillation
▫️ Camphor is traditionally used in folk medicine across many cultures for muscle comfort
▫️ Composition: wintergreen (Gaultheria procumbens), camphor (Cinnamomum camphora), peppermint (Mentha piperita), ylang ylang (Cananga odorata), helichrysum (Helichrysum italicum), blue tansy (Tanacetum annuum), blue chamomile (Matricaria chamomilla), osmanthus (Osmanthus fragrans)

## Indications

🔹 Muscle comfort: massage with Deep Blue helps with feelings of tension and fatigue in muscles
🔹 Joints: topical application on knees and joints before and after physical activity
🔹 Post-workout recovery: suitable for athletes and people with active lifestyles
🔹 Growing children: light leg massage before bed for actively growing children
🔹 After heavy physical labor: back massage after gardening, moving, or an intense physical day

## Directions for Use

🔹 Dilute with a carrier oil and apply to fingers, wrists, shoulders, and neck after a long day at the computer
🔹 Apply to feet and knees before and after workout
🔹 Massage into the back after heavy physical labor or moving
🔹 Apply to legs of actively growing children before bed (diluted with carrier oil)
🔹 Aromatic method: Deep Blue's aroma mainly unfolds through topical application rather than a diffuser — a warm, herbal, soothing scent
🔹 Internal method: not recommended for internal consumption — Deep Blue Polyphenol Complex in capsules is used for dietary joint and muscle support

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Deep Blue Rub, Deep Blue Stick + Copaiba, Deep Blue Polyphenol Complex (dietary supplement)
🔹 Diffuser blends:
- Post-workout (for room): 3 drops Deep Blue, 2 drops peppermint
- Recovery: 3 drops Deep Blue, 2 drops lavender
- Body vigor: 2 drops Deep Blue, 2 drops eucalyptus, 1 drop rosemary
