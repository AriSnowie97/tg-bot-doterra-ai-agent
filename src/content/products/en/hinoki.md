# Hinoki

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Essential Oil (Single, limited edition)


---

## Short Description
👑 A fresh, woody, balsamic aroma of the Japanese cypress
👑 Creates a relaxing environment when diffused
👑 Gentle on the skin — an alternative for people sensitive to other evergreen oils

## Composition and Key Ingredients
▫️ Chamaecyparis obtusa (hinoki, Japanese cypress) essential oil, wood
▫️ Main components: alpha-pinene and delta-cadinene

## Indications
🔹 A relaxing atmosphere when diffused
🔹 Soothing the skin — used in personal care and perfumes
🔹 For people sensitive to other evergreen trees (per the official description it is gentle on the skin)

## Directions for Use
🔹 Aromatic: diffuse, or rub two drops into your palms and inhale deeply
🔹 Topical: apply to the feet, back and chest before bedtime or add to a skincare routine; suitable for massage (dilute with a carrier oil)
🔹 Bath: 2-3 drops (first mixed with salt or a carrier oil); hardwood floor cleaner: 4-5 drops
🔹 The official page does not mention internal use

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products
🔹 Siberian Fir, Douglas Fir, White Fir, Cedarwood — other woody conifer oils in the KB
🔹 Lavender, Frankincense — for relaxing blends

## Fun Facts
👑 In Japan the oil is often used in baths for relaxation
👑 Hinoki wood is durable: the Horyuji Temple pagoda in Nara, reportedly built around 600 AD, is still intact
👑 The doTERRA page reports that Japanese law allows the oil to be extracted only from fallen trees
👑 doTERRA says it uses timber-industry byproducts and culled trees
👑 This is a limited-time item — not available for regular purchase, only during promotions

## Cautions
> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
