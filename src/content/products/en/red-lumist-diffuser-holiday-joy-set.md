## Short Description

👑 Gift-ready holiday set pairing the festive **Red Lumist™** waterless, rechargeable atomizing diffuser with a 15 mL bottle of **Holiday Joy®**.
👑 Features motion-activated diffusion, timed mist settings, and ambient lighting for effortless seasonal aroma in any room.
