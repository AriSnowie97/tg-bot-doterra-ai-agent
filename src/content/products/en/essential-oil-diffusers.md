# Essential Oil Diffusers — Petal Diffuser 2.0

**Category:** Diffusers

**Type:** Ultrasonic Diffuser


---

## Short Description

👑 Compact ultrasonic diffuser for home or office with soft lighting
👑 Mists an ultra-fine yet substantial mist covering an area of up to 30 sq. m
👑 Three settings: 2 and 6 hours continuous operation or 12 hours intermittent (5 min on / 5 min off)

## Composition and Key Features

▫️ Simple three-piece design — easy to clean and assemble
▫️ Optional LED light (night light) or light-free mode
▫️ Mains powered: 100–240V AC
▫️ Materials: glass cover, polypropylene body

## Indications

🔹 Home aromatherapy: creates an atmosphere for relaxation, vigor, or focus depending on the chosen oil
🔹 Night light: soft illumination suitable for a nursery or bedroom
🔹 Office and small rooms: compact size fits well on a desktop
🔹 Ease of use: suitable for both beginners and experienced users

## Directions for Use

🔹 Fill with water up to the maximum level mark
🔹 Add 3–8 drops of the chosen essential oil
🔹 Select operating mode (2, 6, or 12 hours intermittent) and light settings if desired
🔹 Periodically clean with a mixture of water and vinegar to prevent oil residue buildup

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Pairs well with citrus oils (Wild Orange, Bergamot) for an invigorating morning
🔹 Or with Lavender, Sandalwood, or Roman Chamomile for evening relaxation
🔹 Included in the Petal Essentials Collection along with Wild Orange and Lavender

## Interesting Facts

👑 The diffuser is an ultrasonic type — it diffuses oil without heat, preserving its properties
👑 Regular cleaning of the diffuser prevents oil residue buildup that could interfere with the device's operation
👑 Petal is one of the most compact and affordable diffusers in the doTERRA lineup

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
