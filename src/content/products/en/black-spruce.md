# Black Spruce

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Essential Oil (Single, 5 ml)


---

## Short Description

👑 Woody oil for a soothing, harmonious massage
👑 Supports feelings of easy breathing
👑 Soothes after a stressful day

## Ingredients & Key Components

▫️ Picea mariana (black spruce) essential oil

## Uses / Indications

🔹 Soothing massage after strenuous activity
🔹 Support for easy breathing
🔹 Stress relief in challenging situations

## Directions for Use

🔹 Diffusion: 3–4 drops in a diffuser
🔹 Topical use: 1–2 drops diluted with a carrier oil

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Cedarwood, Douglas Fir — other woody oils in the product line

## Interesting Facts

👑 Historically used by the Indigenous peoples of Canada for skin care
👑 Co-Impact Sourcing® from Canada

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
