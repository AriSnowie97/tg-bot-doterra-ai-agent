# doTERRA Console — Comforting Blend

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Blend (Comforting Blend, Emotional Aromatherapy)


---

## Short Description

👑 Comforting blend for moments of sadness, loss, or emotional discomfort
👑 Sweet, musky aroma — Frankincense, Patchouli, Ylang Ylang, Labdanum, Amyris, Sandalwood, Rose, Osmanthus
👑 Helps take the first step on the path toward emotional healing

## Ingredients and Key Components

▫️ Frankincense (Boswellia), Patchouli (Pogostemon cablin), Ylang Ylang (Cananga odorata), Labdanum (Cistus ladanifer), Amyris (Amyris balsamifera), Sandalwood (Santalum album), Rose (Rosa damascena), Osmanthus (Osmanthus fragrans)
▫️ Labdanum is rich in alpha-pinene, known for its renewing properties and ability to balance competing aromas; Rose and Osmanthus are rare, valuable floral oils

## Indications

🔹 Loss and grief: supports in moments of separation or difficult life changes
🔹 Emotional discomfort: calms feelings of anxiety or uncertainty
🔹 Healing after difficult events: regular application over the heart area supports the emotional recovery process
🔹 Supporting a loved one: can be used together with someone going through a difficult time

## Directions for Use

🔹 Add 1–2 drops to a diffuser in moments of sadness or loss, or inhale directly from the bottle for instant comforting
🔹 Apply over the heart area morning and evening, or on the wrists or neck (Touch version is ready to use)

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 What to blend with: Emotional support (2 drops doTERRA Console, 1 drop Frankincense); Gentle evening (2 drops doTERRA Console, 1 drop Lavender)

## Interesting Facts

👑 Console is one of six official Emotional Aromatherapy blends, formulated specifically to work through grief and loss
👑 The combination of floral and woody oils is rare and precious, making Console one of doTERRA's most exquisite blends

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
