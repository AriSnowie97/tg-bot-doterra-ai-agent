# dōTERRA Emotional Aromatherapy® System / Touch Kit

**Category:** Kits

**Type:** Emotional Blends Kit


---

## Short Description

👑 Six proprietary blends covering key categories of emotional well-being
👑 Available in the classic format (5 mL) or Touch format — roll-ons (10 mL)
👑 A simple, thoughtful approach to daily aromatherapy

## Contents and Key Ingredients

▫️ dōTERRA Motivate® — confidence and motivation
▫️ dōTERRA Cheer® — optimism and joy
▫️ dōTERRA Passion® — inspiration and passion
▫️ dōTERRA Forgive® — forgiveness and relief
▫️ dōTERRA Console® — comfort and hope
▫️ dōTERRA Peace® — peace and confidence
▫️ Emotional Aromatherapy System Wheel — included in the classic version

## Indications

🔹 Daily support for emotional balance
🔹 Addressing specific emotional states — from sadness to anxiety

## Directions for Use

🔹 Classic version: diffuse or apply topically diluted with a carrier oil
🔹 Touch version: apply directly to the skin, no dilution required

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Each of the six blends is also sold separately in both formats

## Interesting Facts

👑 For Cheer, Forgive, and Motivate, avoid sunlight and UV rays for up to 12 hours after application due to phototoxic citrus components

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
