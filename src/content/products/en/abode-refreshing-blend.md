# abōde Refreshing Blend

**Category:** Home Care

**Type:** Essential oil blend for home (15 mL)


---

## Brief Description

👑 Signature aromatic blend of the abōde line for natural home cleansing
👑 Provides a pleasant fresh aroma without toxic components
👑 Foundation for the entire line of abōde home care products

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Lime (distilled)
▫️ Litsea
▫️ Cassia
▫️ Lemon Eucalyptus
▫️ Tea Tree
▫️ Arborvitae
▫️ Kochii Eucalyptus
▫️ Cilantro (leaf)
▫️ Lavandin
▫️ Lemongrass / Lemon Myrtle

## Uses

🔹 Natural cleansing of surfaces and air in the home
🔹 Elimination of household odors
🔹 Creating a fresh indoor atmosphere

## Directions for Use

🔹 Diffusion: 3-4 drops in a diffuser
🔹 Add 10-15 drops to water for a non-toxic surface cleaner spray
🔹 A few drops on dryer balls for a fresh aroma
🔹 Topically: 1-2 drops to desired area, diluted with a carrier oil

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 abōde Surface Cleaner
🔹 Entire abōde line (household cleaning products)
🔹 Citrus and herbal oils — Bergamot, Wild Orange, Grapefruit

## Interesting Facts

👑 High content of limonene, geranial, neral, and 1,8-cineole provides powerful cleansing properties
👑 Kochii Eucalyptus from Western Australia has the highest concentration of 1,8-cineole among all eucalyptus species

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas. Avoid direct sunlight and UV rays for up to 12 hours after application.
