# abōde Foaming Hand Wash Concentrate + Dispenser

**Category:** Home Products

**Type:** Foaming hand wash concentrate + reusable dispenser


---

## Short Description

👑 Foaming hand wash concentrate with Citrus Bloom scent
👑 Gently cleanses without drying out the skin
👑 Included — reusable glass dispenser with pump

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Naturally derived surfactants
▫️ Banana fruit extract — to moisturize and soften the skin
▫️ Citrus Bloom® Springtime Blend (essential oils)
▫️ Free of parabens, phthalates, phosphates, dyes, and synthetic fragrances

## Indications

🔹 Daily hand washing at home
🔹 Need for gentle cleansing without drying out the skin

## Directions for Use

🔹 Pour concentrate into the abōde Hand Wash Dispenser to the fill line
🔹 Add purified water to the fill line (recommended dilution — 1 part concentrate to 3 parts water)
🔹 Attach the pump and shake gently
🔹 Use as needed for hand washing

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 abōde Liquid Dish Soap — for washing dishes using the same style of dispensers
🔹 Citrus Bloom® Springtime Blend — the same scent in an essential oil

## Fun Facts

👑 One bottle of concentrate allows filling the 16 oz dispenser approximately twice
👑 The foaming texture makes hand washing a more pleasant experience for the whole family

## Cautions

> ⚠️ For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes and damaged skin. Store in a cool, dry place, avoiding direct sunlight and excessive heat.
