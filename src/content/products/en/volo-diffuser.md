# dōTERRA Volo™ Ultrasonic Diffuser (Onyx & Marble)

**Category:** Diffusers

**Type:** Ultrasonic Diffuser (Onyx / Marble)

---

## Short Description

👑 Sleek ultrasonic diffuser featuring a handcrafted polyresin stone top with a custom diamond pattern, available in Onyx and Marble finishes
👑 Covers rooms up to 300 sq. ft. (28 sq. m.) — ideal for living rooms, bedrooms, or offices
👑 Customizable output with 1-, 3-, and 6-hour settings on high mist, or up to 14 hours continuous on the lowest mist setting

## Key Features & Specifications

▫️ Handcrafted polyresin stone top in Onyx or Marble finish
▫️ Optional ambient light settings: light-free, warm light, or bright amber
▫️ Automatic safety shut-off when water runs low
▫️ Ultrasonic cool-mist technology preserves the natural aromatic compounds of essential oils

## Uses & Benefits

🔹 Everyday aromatic diffusion for medium to large rooms
🔹 Evening relaxation atmosphere with customizable warm ambient lighting
🔹 All-night diffusion support with up to 14 hours of run time

## Directions for Use

🔹 Remove the top and fill with clean room-temperature water to the maximum fill line
🔹 Add 3–5 drops of your chosen doTERRA essential oil or blend
🔹 Select your desired time and light settings
🔹 Clean regularly according to the product manual to prevent oil buildup

## Precautions

> ⚠️ Use only clean water and pure essential oils. Place on a flat, stable surface. Keep out of reach of children.
