# doTERRA Hope Touch

**Category:** Roll-ons (Touch)

**Type:** Blend (Charity Line)


---

## Short Description

👑 A fresh, uplifting blend that creates a life-affirming, optimistic atmosphere
👑 Woody-citrus aroma — cedarwood, wild orange, lemon, petitgrain, pink pepper, bergamot, sandalwood, vetiver, magnolia, two types of jasmine
👑 With every purchase of this blend, the company donates funds to fight human trafficking through the doTERRA Healing Hands Foundation

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Cedarwood (Juniperus virginiana), Wild Orange (Citrus sinensis), Lemon (Citrus limon), Petitgrain (Citrus aurantium, leaf)
▫️ Pink Pepper (Schinus molle), Bergamot (Citrus bergamia), Sandalwood (Santalum album), Vetiver (Vetiveria zizanioides), Magnolia (Magnolia alba), Jasmine (Jasminum grandiflorum, two types)

## Indications

🔹 Loss of hope or motivation: helps restore a sense of resilience and faith in the future
🔹 Regular positive mindset adjustment: morning application as a reminder of a hopeful mindset
🔹 Support for a socially important cause: every purchase supports the fight against human trafficking

## Directions for Use

🔹 Aromatic method: apply to diffusion jewelry, natural dolomite, or lava stones for an inspiring aroma
🔹 Topical method: apply to pulse points while focusing on positive, uplifting thoughts; on wrists and temples; on hands, rub together, and inhale deeply as needed; use during shoulder and neck massage
🔹 Internal method: not recommended for internal use — the blend is intended for aromatic and topical application

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Wild Orange (to support positive thinking)

## Interesting Facts

👑 With every purchase of doTERRA Hope, the company donates a portion of the proceeds to doTERRA Healing Hands Foundation projects fighting human trafficking
👑 The formula combines ten different ingredients — a rare complexity even for doTERRA blends
👑 The name and purpose of the blend go far beyond just a pleasant aroma — it symbolizes life, resilience, and hope

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor’s care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas. Avoid contact with eyes and mucous membranes, do not apply to broken or irritated skin. The information provided is for informational purposes only and does not constitute medical advice.

## Additional Information

**Effect on the physical body:**
▫️ Toning, refreshing
▫️ Grounding (thanks to cedarwood and vetiver)
▫️ Soothing

**Effect on an emotional level:**
Removes: feelings of hopelessness, loss of faith in a better future.
Fosters: hope and resilience, faith in the possibility of reaching a better version of oneself.

**Origin of ingredients:**
▫️ Petitgrain is distilled from the leaves of the bitter orange tree, lemon petitgrain — from the leaves of the lemon tree.
▫️ Pink pepper originates from the steamed pinkish-red berries of a tree related to cashew — spicy and fruity at the same time.
▫️ Two types of jasmine (absolute and distilled essential oil) give the blend depth and complexity.
