👑 Earthy oil, calming and uplifting for mood
👑 Cleanses the skin, leaving a healthy glow
👑 Rare oil with an ancient history of use

▫️ Nardostachys jatamansi essential oil (spikenard)

## Indications

🔹 Calming and uplifting mood
🔹 Cleansing and healthy-looking skin

## Directions for Use

🔹 Diffuse for calming
🔹 Apply topically combined with a carrier oil for skin

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Console®, Peace® — calming blends with a similar profile

## Fun Facts

👑 Earthy, deep aroma, rare among modern essential oils

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
