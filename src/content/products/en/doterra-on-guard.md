# doTERRA On Guard — Protective Blend

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Blend (Protective Blend)


---

## Short Description

👑 Protective blend to support immune function — one of doTERRA's most popular and versatile blends
👑 Warm, spicy, camphoraceous, woody aroma with Wild Orange, Clove, Cinnamon, Eucalyptus, and Rosemary
👑 Versatile: suitable for internal use, household cleaning, and topical application

## Ingredients and Key Components

▫️ Wild Orange (Citrus sinensis), Clove (Eugenia caryophyllata), Cinnamon Leaf and Bark (Cinnamomum zeylanicum), Eucalyptus (Eucalyptus globulus), Rosemary (Rosmarinus officinalis)
▫️ Ingredient Highlights: The Clove tree is native to Southeast Asia, matures for about three years, but can live for over 100 years. Rosemary was considered a sacred plant by ancient Greeks, Romans, Egyptians, and Hebrews. The Eucalyptus in On Guard (Eucalyptus globulus) is a different species than in doTERRA's signature Eucalyptus essential oil

## Indications

🔹 Immune support: regular internal use supports healthy immune function of the body
🔹 Household cleaning: a natural non-toxic alternative to chemical surface cleaners
🔹 Air freshening: diffusion helps freshen the air and create an energizing atmosphere
🔹 Antioxidant protection: Clove and Rosemary in the formula support the body's antioxidant defenses
🔹 Fresh breath: gargling/rinsing with the diluted blend freshens breath and soothes an irritated throat

## Directions for Use

🔹 Aromatic use: add 3–4 drops to a diffuser during seasonal changes or to freshen the air at home; diffuse to create an uplifting, refreshing atmosphere
🔹 Topical use: apply 1–2 drops to the soles of the feet or chest, diluting with a carrier oil as needed; add to Fractionated Coconut Oil for a natural hand cleanser; rinse mouth with a few drops diluted in water for fresh breath
🔹 Internal use: dilute 1 drop in 120 mL (4 fl oz) of liquid for regular immune support; add to tea or water to support the body during seasonal threats; soak apple slices in water with a few drops for a healthy snack

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products
