# Veráge Cleanser

**Category:** Personal Care

**Type:** Facial Cleansing Gel


---

## Brief Description

👑 The first step of the Veráge system — a gel formula for cleansing and toning the skin
👑 Nourishes and hydrates while cleansing
👑 Leaves skin looking radiant and youthful

## Ingredients and Key Ingredients

▫️ Essential oils of Wild Orange, Tea Tree, and Basil

## Indications

🔹 Daily facial skin cleansing
🔹 Need for a toning effect while washing the face

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply a small amount to moist face and neck, massage in circular motions upward
🔹 Rinse thoroughly with water, pat dry with a towel
🔹 Use morning and evening before Veráge Toner

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Veráge Toner — the next step of the system
🔹 Veráge Immortelle Hydrating Serum, Veráge Moisturizer — final steps

## Fun Facts

👑 Wild Orange, Tea Tree, and Basil together create an invigorating herbaceous aroma

## Cautions

> ⚠️ For external use only. Avoid direct contact with eyes. If skin irritation occurs, discontinue use. Keep out of reach of children.
