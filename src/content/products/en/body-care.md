# Body Care — Fractionated Coconut Oil

**Category:** Body Care

**Type:** base (carrier) body oil


---

## Short Description

👑 Fractionated Coconut Oil is a carrier oil for diluting essential oils and body care
👑 Light, colorless, odorless; completely soluble with all doTERRA essential oils
👑 Leaves no greasy residue and does not clog pores

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride — the main component derived from the fractionation of coconut oil
▫️ 100% plant origin (Cocos nucifera)

## Indications

🔹 When and what to use for: carrier oil for dilution (reduces skin sensitivity when applying concentrated essential oils), hydration (softens dry skin on hands, legs, elbows), massage (ideal texture for massage blends with essential oils), long shelf life (does not solidify and lasts almost indefinitely)

## Directions for Use

🔹 For dilution: 1 part essential oil to 5 or more parts Fractionated Coconut Oil — depending on skin sensitivity
🔹 Topically: apply to skin to hydrate without interfering with the fragrance of other blends
🔹 For massage: mix with your favorite essential oils and apply to the neck and shoulders

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 A universal carrier component for almost all topical application recipes for doTERRA essential oils
🔹 Often used together with Deep Blue, AromaTouch, and other massage blends

## Fun Facts

👑 Fractionation removes long-chain triglycerides, which cause regular coconut oil to solidify at room temperature
👑 The fractionation process also increases the proportion of caprylic and capric acids, which are beneficial for skin health
👑 Main chemical component — Caprylic/capric triglyceride

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
