# 10 mL Amber Roller Bottle 6 Pack

**Category:** Diffusers & Accessories → Accessories

**Type:** Roller bottles for custom essential oil blends


---

## Short Description

👑 Set of six 10 mL amber glass roller bottles for custom essential oil blends
👑 Same glass and metal roller used in doTERRA Touch bottles
👑 Ideal for blending, storing, and personal application of essential oils

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Amber glass — protects essential oils from sunlight
▫️ Stainless steel roller — even application without oil waste
▫️ 10 mL volume — convenient compact size for a bag or pocket

## Indications

🔹 Custom blends: allow you to create your own essential oil combinations with a carrier oil
🔹 Convenient application: the roller provides targeted application without direct hand contact with the oil
🔹 Portability: compact size suitable for travel and everyday carry
🔹 Cost-effective: diluted blend in a roller bottle lasts longer than oil from a dropper

## Directions for Use

🔹 Fill the bottle about three-quarters full with a carrier oil (e.g., Fractionated Coconut Oil)
🔹 Add chosen essential oils (typically 10-15 drops per 10 mL of carrier oil)
🔹 Snap in the roller and screw on the cap, invert several times to mix
🔹 Apply to desired areas of the skin — wrists, temples, neck

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Frequently used with Fractionated Coconut Oil as a base
🔹 Suitable for any doTERRA essential oils and blends — Deep Blue, AromaTouch, custom recipes

## Fun Facts

👑 Amber glass is traditionally used for storing essential oils because it blocks ultraviolet rays
👑 The exact same type of bottle and roller is used in pre-made doTERRA Touch line products
👑 Roller bottles are one of the most popular accessories among users who create custom recipes

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor’s care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.

## Additional Information

🔹 Thoroughly dilute concentrated essential oils with a carrier oil before applying to skin
🔹 Label bottles for convenience and safety
🔹 Wash and dry bottles before reusing with a new blend

> ⚠️ The information provided is for informational purposes only. Essential oil dilution depends on skin type and the specific oil — consult a specialist if necessary.
