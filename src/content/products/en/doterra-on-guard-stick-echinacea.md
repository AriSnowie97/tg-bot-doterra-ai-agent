# doTERRA On Guard® Stick + Echinacea

**Category:** Personal Care

**Type:** Topical stick (30 g)


---

## Short Description

👑 A solid stick based on coconut oil and sunflower seed wax with the On Guard blend and echinacea
👑 Officially: echinacea is said to promote a cleansing effect when applied topically, and coconut oil is deeply moisturizing
👑 The stick glides easily over the skin — convenient to apply with no dilution and no spills

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Coconut oil (Cocos nucifera), sunflower seed wax (Helianthus annuus), tapioca starch
▫️ On Guard blend: orange peel oil (Citrus aurantium dulcis), clove bud oil (Eugenia caryophyllus), cinnamon leaf and bark oil (Cinnamomum zeylanicum), eucalyptus leaf oil (Eucalyptus globulus), rosemary leaf/stem oil (Rosmarinus officinalis)
▫️ Elderberry fruit extract (Sambucus nigra) and echinacea purple flower oil
▫️ Glycerin, glyceryl laurate, glyceryl caprylate, glyceryl undecylenate, lauric acid

## Indications

🔹 Topical application to key points of the body — a convenient on-the-go form of the On Guard blend
🔹 The cleansing effect of echinacea when applied topically (per the official description)
🔹 Moisturizing the skin thanks to coconut oil

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply to the spine, neck, soles of the feet and pulse points
🔹 For external use only; do not apply to the face
🔹 Internal use is not intended

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products

🔹 doTERRA On Guard Touch — the 10 ml roll-on (separate KB file)

## Fun Facts

👑 Echinacea is also known as purple coneflower; per the doTERRA description it was used by early indigenous cultures and historically in traditional practice
👑 Shelf life after opening is 12 months; the batch number and expiration date are printed on the product
👑 The product description mentions "echinacea essential oil", while the ingredient list names Echinacea Purple Flower Oil

## Cautions

> ⚠️ For external use only. Avoid applying to the face and avoid contact with the eyes. Discontinue use if irritation occurs. Not for children under 1 year of age; for older children only under adult supervision. Keep out of reach of children. Avoid sunlight and UV rays for at least 12 hours after application. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician.
