## Short Description

👑 Natural antiseptic No. 1 — tea tree is one of the most powerful antibacterial and antifungal agents among essential oils

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ 100% pure Tea Tree essential oil (Melaleuca alternifolia) CPTG® standard

## Indications

🔹 **Acne and problem skin:** kills P. acnes bacteria without over-drying the skin
🔹 **Fungal infections:** effective for nail fungus (onychomycosis), athlete's foot, skin candidiasis
🔹 **Herpes and viral lesions:** reduces the duration of a herpes episode and decreases vesicle size

## Directions for Use

🔹 **Aromatic method:** Add 3–4 drops to a diffuser to purify the air in a room or inhale during nasal congestion and cold symptoms.
🔹 **Topical application:** Apply 1 drop undiluted directly to pimples or an infected area, or dilute 2–3 drops with a carrier oil to apply to areas affected by fungus, wounds, burns, or insect bites.
🔹 **Hair care:** Add 3–4 drops to shampoo for seborrheic dermatitis or dandruff.
🔹 **Internal use:** Generally NOT recommended for internal use. Consult a qualified specialist.

### Origin
Tea tree (Melaleuca alternifolia) is an evergreen plant endemic to the Australian state of New South Wales. The indigenous people of Australia—the Bundjalung aboriginal people—used melaleuca leaves to treat wounds and skin conditions for millennia.
The essential oil is obtained by steam distillation of the tree's leaves and twigs. doTERRA partners with certified Australian plantations that ensure the CPTG® (Certified Pure Tested Grade) standard.

### Research
👑 **Efficacy of tea tree oil against Propionibacterium acnes** (Medical Journal of Australia, 1990)
A randomized study showed that 5% tea tree gel had comparable efficacy to 5% benzoyl peroxide gel in treating acne, but with fewer side effects (dryness, scaling).
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2145499/

👑 **Antifungal activity of Melaleuca alternifolia against Candida** (Journal of Antimicrobial Chemotherapy, 2004)
Terpinen-4-ol killed Candida albicans cells by compromising cell membrane integrity — a mechanism of action distinct from standard antifungal drugs, making it valuable for resistant strains.
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15073153/

👑 **Antimicrobial activity against MRSA** (Journal of Hospital Infection, 2008)
Tea tree oil showed significant activity against methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus (MRSA) under laboratory conditions, opening prospects for application in infection control.
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18261823/

### Traditions and History
Tea tree was considered a sacred healing tree in the tradition of the indigenous peoples of Australia. An aboriginal person with skin ailments would immerse themselves in natural lagoons where tea trees grew.
In a modern context, tea tree oil is one of the most thoroughly researched natural antiseptics in scientific literature — more than 300 peer-reviewed studies confirm its antimicrobial activity.
It is a "guardian" oil — protecting physical space and the body from microbial threats, reminding us of the power of natural defense mechanisms.

🔹 Do not apply to mucous membranes (nose, mouth, eyes) without significant dilution.
