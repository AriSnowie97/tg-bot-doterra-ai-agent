# doTERRA Passion — Inspiring Blend

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Blend (Inspiring Blend, Emotional Aromatherapy)


---

## Short Description

👑 An inspiring blend that ignites excitement, passion, and joy for life
👑 Spicy, sweet aroma — cinnamon, ginger, cardamom, clove, damiana, with notes of tonka bean and vanilla
👑 One of six official Emotional Aromatherapy blends — designed specifically for inspiration and excitement

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Damiana — a rare oil rich in 1,8-cineole and alpha-pinene, known for its renewing properties
▫️ A combination of cinnamon (Cinnamomum zeylanicum), ginger (Zingiber officinale), cardamom (Elettaria cardamomum), clove (Eugenia caryophyllata), damiana (Turnera diffusa), with notes of tonka bean and vanilla absolute

## Indications

🔹 Creative work: diffusing at your desk helps ignite a creative spark
🔹 Routine and apathy: helps feel novelty and excitement in everyday tasks
🔹 Romantic evening: a warm, spicy aroma creates an inviting atmosphere for special moments
🔹 Loss of interest: supports bringing joy back to activities that once brought pleasure

## Directions for Use

🔹 Aromatic use: diffuse in the morning to start the day with energy and enthusiasm; apply to pulse points throughout the day for inspiration and clarity; diffuse at your desk to spark creativity
🔹 Topical use: apply to wrists or neck for a personal, warm fragrance (Touch version is ready to use)
🔹 Internal use: not recommended for internal use — the blend is intended for aromatic and topical use

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Creative inspiration: 3 drops doTERRA Passion
🔹 Romantic evening: 2 drops doTERRA Passion, 1 drop Ylang Ylang
🔹 Energetic morning: 2 drops doTERRA Passion, 1 drop Wild Orange

## Interesting Facts

👑 Passion is one of six official Emotional Aromatherapy blends, developed specifically to overcome boredom and indifference
👑 Damiana was traditionally used by the indigenous peoples of Central America

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas. (It is also recommended to dilute with a carrier oil for sensitive skin, as clove and cinnamon may irritate).
