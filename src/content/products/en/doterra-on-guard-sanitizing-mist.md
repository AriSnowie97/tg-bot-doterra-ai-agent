# dōTERRA On Guard® Sanitizing Mist

**Category:** Home Products

**Type:** Hand Sanitizing Mist


---

## Short Description

👑 Convenient hand sanitizing spray based on a natural antiseptic
👑 Compact format for a bag, pocket, or car
👑 Fragrance of On Guard blend instead of artificial fragrances

## Ingredients and Key Components

▫️ Ethyl alcohol (natural antiseptic)
▫️ On Guard® essential blend (wild orange, clove, cinnamon, eucalyptus, rosemary)

## Indications

🔹 Hand sanitization away from home — on the road, at work, at school
🔹 Situations where there is no access to water and soap

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply 1-2 sprays directly to hands
🔹 Rub together until completely dry

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 dōTERRA On Guard® Sanitizing Hand Wipes — sanitizing wet wipes
🔹 dōTERRA On Guard® Foaming Hand Wash — for complete hand washing

## Interesting Facts

👑 Flammable liquid — keep away from open flames, sparks, and temperatures above 40°C

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Flammable substance — keep away from fire, sparks, and sources of high temperature. For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes. Discontinue use and consult a doctor if skin irritation or redness develops. Keep out of reach of children.
