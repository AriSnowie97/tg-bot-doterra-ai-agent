# Citronella

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Essential Oil (Single, 15 ml)


---

## Short Description

👑 Herbaceous oil — a powerful natural insect repellent
👑 Refreshes and cleans surfaces at home
👑 Creates an invigorating, positive atmosphere

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Cymbopogon winterianus (citronella) essential oil

## Indications

🔹 Natural protection against insects, especially mosquitoes
🔹 Cleaning surfaces at home
🔹 Hair care

## Directions for Use

🔹 Outdoor diffusion to ward off insects
🔹 Topically with a carrier oil as a repellent
🔹 Add a few drops to water for a surface spray cleaner

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Peppermint, Grapefruit — for an enhanced repellent diffuser blend

## Interesting Facts

👑 Masks human scents that attract insects
👑 Source — leaves of a tall grass native to Asia

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
