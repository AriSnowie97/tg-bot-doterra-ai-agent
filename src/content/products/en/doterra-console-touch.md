# dōTERRA Console® Touch

**Category:** Blends

**Type:** Touch Essential Oil Blend (roll-on, 10 ml)


---

## Short Description

👑 Soothing floral and woody blend for support in moments of loss
👑 Helps foster feelings of comfort and hope
👑 Convenient format to carry with you throughout the day

## Ingredients and Key Components

▫️ Frankincense
▫️ Patchouli
▫️ Ylang Ylang
▫️ Labdanum
▫️ Amyris
▫️ Sandalwood
▫️ Rose
▫️ Osmanthus

## Indications

🔹 Support during periods of sadness, anxiety, or inner confusion
🔹 Emotional support in times of loss
🔹 Creating a calming, comforting atmosphere

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply over heart or bottoms of feet in the morning during difficult periods
🔹 Massage into shoulders to relieve emotional tension
🔹 Apply to a sachet and place in a drawer or closet for a pleasant aroma
🔹 Carry in a bag or pocket, inhale the aroma throughout the day

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 dōTERRA Console® — classic (undiluted) blend
🔹 Other Touch blends of the emotional line — Cheer, Forgive, Motivate, Peace

## Interesting Facts

👑 Aromatic description: sweet, musky, floral
👑 Part of the dōTERRA Emotional Aromatherapy® Touch Kit along with other emotional blends

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas. Avoid direct sunlight or UV rays for up to 12 hours after application.
