# Cardamom

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Essential Oil (Single, 15 mL)


---

## Short Description

👑 Spicy, minty oil for respiratory system support
👑 Promotes feelings of easy breathing
👑 Creates a calm atmosphere when diffused

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Elettaria cardamomum (cardamom) essential oil

## Indications

🔹 Support for clear breathing
🔹 Occasional digestive discomfort
🔹 Creating a calm atmosphere

## Directions for Use

🔹 Diffusion for a soothing atmosphere
🔹 Internally: 1 drop in a capsule for digestive discomfort

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 DigestZen® — Cardamom is an ingredient in this blend
🔹 dōTERRA Breathe® — for respiratory support

## Interesting Facts

👑 One of the ingredients in the classic DigestZen blend
👑 Spicy, cool, minty aroma

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
