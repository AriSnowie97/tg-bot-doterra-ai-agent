# Cedarwood

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Essential oil (single, 15 ml)


---

## Brief Description

👑 Woody oil — a true powerhouse for skincare
👑 Improves the appearance of skin imperfections
👑 Soothes the mind after a hard day

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Juniperus virginiana (Virginian cedarwood) essential oil

## Indications

🔹 Care for problem skin
🔹 Calming the mind and relieving tension

## Directions for Use

🔹 Diffuse in the evening to soothe
🔹 Topically: add to cream or dilute with a carrier oil

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Lavender, Vetiver — for calming evening blends

## Interesting Facts

👑 One of the most popular woody ingredients in skincare formulations

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
