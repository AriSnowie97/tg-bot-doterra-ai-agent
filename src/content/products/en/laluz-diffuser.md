# Laluz® Diffuser

**Category:** Home Products

**Type:** Ultrasonic Diffuser


---

## Brief Description

👑 Sculptural ultrasonic diffuser with a frosted glass top
👑 Up to 8 hours of continuous operation
👑 Three diffusion-independent mood light settings

## Ingredients & Key Components

▫️ Not applicable (device, not a topical product)

## Indications

🔹 Aromatizing the living room, bedroom, or office
🔹 Creating a cozy, stylish indoor atmosphere

## Directions for Use

🔹 Fill the tank with water (120 ml capacity) and add essential oils
🔹 Select mode: high mist output — up to 4 hours, low — up to 8 hours
🔹 Choose one of the three ambient light options as desired

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Citrus Bliss® — for an energetic, inviting aroma in the living room
🔹 dōTERRA On Guard® — for refreshing the air in the bathroom
🔹 Lavender with Cedarwood — for a soothing bedtime scent

## Interesting Facts

👑 Recommended coverage area — up to 330 sq. ft. (30 sq. m)
👑 For cleaning: fill halfway with water, add 10 drops of white vinegar, and let run for 5 minutes

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Use according to the manufacturer's instructions. Clean regularly to prevent oil residue buildup.
