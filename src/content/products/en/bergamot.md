## Short Description

👑 Citrus oil for confidence and stress relief
👑 Supports a healthy metabolism when used internally
👑 Calms the nervous system

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Citrus bergamia (Bergamot) essential oil

## Indications

🔹 Feelings of stress and a need for confidence
🔹 Support for a healthy metabolism
🔹 Calming the nervous system

## Directions for Use

🔹 Topically or aromatically during stress
🔹 Internally as a supplement to support metabolism

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Frankincense, Lavender — for calming blends

## Interesting Facts

👑 Used in classic cologne due to its fresh citrus aroma
👑 Phototoxic — avoid sunlight after application

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor’s care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas. Avoid direct sunlight and UV rays for up to 12 hours after applying to skin.
