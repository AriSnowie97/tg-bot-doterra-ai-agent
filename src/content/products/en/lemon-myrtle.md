# Lemon Myrtle

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Essential Oil (Single, limited edition)


---

## Short Description
👑 A sweet, lemony, slightly herbal aroma — both soothing and uplifting to the mood
👑 Officially described as a cleansing oil whose main active component is citral
👑 Promotes feelings of positivity and calm

## Composition and Key Ingredients
▫️ Backhousia citriodora (lemon myrtle) essential oil, leaf, steam distilled
▫️ The main component is citral

## Indications
🔹 Feelings of positivity and calm
🔹 Cleansing and purifying the air and surfaces in home cleaning
🔹 An addition to a skincare routine (one drop in a cleanser or moisturizer)

## Directions for Use
🔹 Aromatic: diffuse to help clear and purify the air
🔹 Topical: add 1 drop to a facial cleanser or moisturizer; always dilute with a carrier oil
🔹 Household: several drops in a spray bottle with water and white vinegar for an all-purpose cleaner (the official page pairs it with Wild Orange)
🔹 The official page does not mention internal use

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products
🔹 Wild Orange — for the household cleaning spray (official tip)
🔹 Elevation — lemon myrtle is an ingredient of this blend
🔹 Lemon, Lemongrass — other lemony oils in the KB

## Fun Facts
👑 Native to Australia: the subtropical and tropical East Coast, 50-800 m above sea level
👑 Aboriginal Australians and Torres Strait Islanders have long used it for various purposes
👑 Medium-sized trees with dark-green lance-shaped leaves that stay on year-round; creamy white flowers appear from summer to autumn
👑 Leaves are distilled right after harvest; it is a limited-time item — only during promotions

## Cautions
> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas. Always dilute with a carrier oil to minimize skin sensitivity.
