# Rose Touch

**Category:** Rollers (Touch)

**Type:** Touch Essential Oil (roll-on, 10 ml)

## Short Description

👑 Pre-diluted version of the royal Rose oil
👑 Balances skin moisture levels and evens out skin tone
👑 Romantic, refined personal fragrance

## Ingredients and Key Components

▫️ Fractionated Coconut Oil
▫️ Rose Essential Oil (Rosa damascena)

## Indications

🔹 Moisture balance and evening out skin tone
🔹 Personal romantic perfume

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply to the neck and wrists as a perfume
🔹 Spot-apply to specific areas of the skin for care

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Rose (5 ml) — classic undiluted version

## Interesting Facts

👑 Rose is historically called the "queen of essential oils" due to its popularity and powerful topical properties

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
