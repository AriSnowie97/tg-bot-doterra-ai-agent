# Collections — Athlete's Kit

**Category:** Kits

**Type:** themed kit


---

## Short Description

👑 A themed kit for an active lifestyle and athletes
👑 Combines five essential oils and Deep Blue Rub in a compact zippered bag
👑 Created in collaboration with athletic organizations to support activity and recovery

## Contents and Key Ingredients

▫️ doTERRA Breathe (5 ml) — respiratory blend
▫️ doTERRA On Guard (5 ml) — protective blend
▫️ Lavender (5 ml)
▫️ Tea Tree / Melaleuca (5 ml)
▫️ Peppermint (5 ml)
▫️ Deep Blue Rub (120 ml) — topical cream for muscles and joints

## Indications

🔹 Active lifestyle: body support during and after physical exertion
🔹 Muscle comfort: Deep Blue Rub is applied to areas targeted during a workout
🔹 Breathing: doTERRA Breathe supports feelings of clear breathing during activity
🔹 Energy: Peppermint helps with feelings of fatigue or low energy

## Directions for Use

🔹 Deep Blue Rub — apply to muscles and joints after a workout or an active day
🔹 Peppermint — apply to temples or back of the neck for an invigorating feel
🔹 Lavender — use in the evening for relaxation after an event
🔹 Each oil has its own recommended method of use indicated on the label

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Deep Blue Polyphenol Complex — dietary supplement to complement topical muscle care
🔹 Roller bottles for creating personal travel blends using the kit's oils

## Interesting Facts

👑 doTERRA partners with athletic facilities, providing elite athletes with access to essential oils
👑 The kit is packaged in a compact zippered bag — convenient to take to workouts or competitions
👑 The kit's contents are tailored specifically to the needs of an active body: breathing, muscle comfort, and recovery

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
