# Petitgrain

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Essential Oil (Single, 15 ml)

## Short Description

👑 A woody-green oil, sometimes referred to as "lavender for men"
👑 Calms the nervous system and promotes restful sleep
👑 Supports the cardiovascular, digestive, and immune systems

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Citrus aurantium essential oil (bitter orange leaf)

## Indications

🔹 Relieving feelings of tension
🔹 Supporting restful sleep
🔹 Overall systemic support

## Directions for Use

🔹 Diffusion before sleep
🔹 Topically, diluted with a carrier oil

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

## Interesting Facts

👑 Obtained from the leaves of the same tree that produces Neroli (from the flowers)

## Cautions
