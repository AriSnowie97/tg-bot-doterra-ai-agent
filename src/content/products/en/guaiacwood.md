# Guaiacwood

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Essential Oil (Single, 15 ml)


---

## Short Description

👑 Earthy-sweet woody oil for meditation and relaxation
👑 Soothes minor skin irritations
👑 Supports skin's natural hydration

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Bulnesia sarmientoi (Guaiacwood) essential oil

## Indications

🔹 Creating a calm, grounding atmosphere
🔹 Soothing minor skin irritations

## Directions for Use

🔹 Diffusion: 3-4 drops in a diffuser
🔹 Topical: 1-2 drops to desired area

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Frankincense, Sandalwood — for meditative blends

## Fun Facts

👑 Has become a sustainable alternative to endangered Palo Santo
👑 Sustainably sourced from the Gran Chaco forest in Paraguay

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
