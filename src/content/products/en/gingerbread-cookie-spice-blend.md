## Directions for Use

🔹 **Aromatic:** Add 3–4 drops to your diffuser of choice
🔹 **DIY Room Spray:** Add a few drops to a glass spray bottle with water and witch hazel, shake well, and mist into living spaces and entryways
🔹 **Tree Ornaments:** Place a few drops onto a terracotta or cotton ornament for a subtle, lasting holiday scent
🔹 **Topical:** Apply 1–2 drops diluted well with a carrier oil to minimize skin sensitivity (contains warm spice oils)
🔹 **Culinary:** Add 1–2 drops to frostings, lattes, or hot cocoa, or 8+ drops to a full baked goods recipe (follow local market labeling)
