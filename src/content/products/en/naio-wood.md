# Naio™ Wood Essential Oil — 5 mL (New 2026)

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Single Essential Oil (5 mL)

---

## Short Description

👑 Rare single essential oil released at the 2026 Global Convention (available in the US starting October 1, 2026)
👑 Sustainably sourced from *Myoporum sandwicense* at the Kealakekua Mountain Reserve on the Big Island of Hawaiʻi through Co-Impact Sourcing® reforestation initiatives
👑 Naturally rich in **α-bisabolol**, supporting a balanced complexion, skin comfort, and a warm, grounding aroma

## Directions for Use

🔹 **Topical:** Add 1–2 drops to your moisturizer, serum, or carrier oil to soothe and balance the skin
🔹 **Aromatic:** Diffuse 3–4 drops to create a warm, grounding atmosphere

## Precautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. Consult your physician if pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
