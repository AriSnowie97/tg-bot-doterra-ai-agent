# Cypress

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Essential Oil (single, 15 ml)


---

## Brief Description

👑 Woody oil to balance emotions during times of change
👑 Gives an energetic boost before physical activity
👑 Soothes anxious feelings

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Cupressus sempervirens (cypress) essential oil

## Indications

🔹 Emotional balance during periods of change
🔹 Energetic booster before a workout

## Directions for Use

🔹 Diffuse during emotional transitions
🔹 Apply to the skin before physical activity

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products

🔹 Douglas Fir, Siberian Fir — woody oils for complex blends

## Interesting Facts

👑 Traditionally associated with resilience and balance

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.

## From Literature & Reference Guides

- **Emotional Profile ('Emotions & Essential Oils'):** Helps release perfectionism and rigidity; encourages adaptability, flow, and trust.
- **Safety & Practical Notes:** 'Aromatherapy for Mother & Child' & 'Aromalogy': avoid in early pregnancy; consult a specialist if prone to clotting or using anticoagulants, and observe the 21-day cycle rule.
