# White Fir

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Essential Oil (Single)


---

## Short Description
👑 A clean, crisp, woody-forest aroma
👑 Calming after strenuous activity and energizing on tired afternoons
👑 A versatile oil for diffusion, home cleaning and holiday décor

## Composition and Key Ingredients
▫️ Abies alba (white / silver fir) essential oil, obtained from the needles
▫️ Main components: limonene, beta-pinene, bornyl acetate

## Indications
🔹 Calming after strenuous physical activity
🔹 Energizing when diffused during tired or lazy parts of the day
🔹 A sense of stability when feeling unsettled
🔹 Moderating stress at work and in tense situations

## Directions for Use
🔹 Topical: dilute 1-2 drops with a few drops of fractionated coconut oil and gently rub onto the skin
🔹 Diffusion: in a diffuser of choice; the official page suggests a blend of Wintergreen, Juniper Berry, Cinnamon Bark, Cypress and White Fir
🔹 Shower: add 1-2 drops in the morning shower
🔹 Inhalation: rub a few drops between palms and inhale
🔹 Household: for homemade cleaners (for example a pine floor cleaner) and holiday décor (pinecones, potpourri)

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products
🔹 Siberian Fir, Douglas Fir — related conifer oils in the KB (interchangeable with White Fir in Symphony of the Cells)
🔹 Wintergreen, Juniper Berry, Cinnamon Bark, Cypress — for a woody-spicy diffuser blend
🔹 Cedarwood, Blue Tansy — alternatives in the substitution table

## Fun Facts
👑 The tree is native to European forests and can grow taller than 100 feet (about 30 m)
👑 The tree has been used as building material and as a Christmas tree
👑 The oil undergoes CPTG (Certified Pure Tested Grade) testing

## Cautions
> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas. Book "Aromalogiya": fir (Abies alba) — avoid during flare-ups of kidney disease; fir oils can irritate the digestive mucosa, so do not take on an empty stomach.
