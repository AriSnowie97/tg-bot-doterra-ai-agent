# doTERRA Whisper Touch — Personal Fragrance Blend

**Category:** Rollers (Touch)

**Type:** Blend (personal fragrance for women)


---

## Brief Description

👑 A luxurious personal blend that interacts with individual body chemistry, creating a unique fragrance for each person
👑 Warm, musky, floral-spicy aroma — jasmine, ylang ylang, patchouli, vanilla, cinnamon, cocoa, and others
👑 A natural alternative to synthetic perfumes, ready to use without dilution

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Patchouli (Pogostemon cablin), bergamot (Citrus bergamia), sandalwood (Santalum album), rose (Rosa damascena), jasmine (Jasminum grandiflorum), cinnamon (Cinnamomum zeylanicum), vetiver (Vetiveria zizanioides), labdanum (Cistus ladanifer), ylang ylang (Cananga odorata), cocoa, vanilla absolute
▫️ Jasmine and ylang ylang are considered the most expensive floral absolutes in the perfume industry for their euphoric, floral aroma

## Indications

🔹 Personal fragrance: A natural alternative to synthetic perfumes without artificial ingredients
🔹 Special occasion: A luxurious, complex fragrance for evening outings or special moments
🔹 Everyday confidence: Regular application as part of a self-expression ritual

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply to wrists after a shower for a unique personal fragrance
🔹 Carry in a purse or backpack to refresh the scent throughout the day

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Not intended for diffusion — the blend unfolds specifically on the skin

## Interesting Facts

👑 Whisper Touch interacts with each person's individual skin chemistry, so the fragrance may smell different on different people
👑 Combines eleven different ingredients — one of the most complex personal blends in the doTERRA portfolio
