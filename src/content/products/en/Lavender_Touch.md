# Lavender Touch

**Category:** Roll-ons (Touch)

**Type:** Single oil, Touch (10 mL roll-on)


---

## Brief Description

👑 Classic calming oil in a child-friendly format
👑 Gentle on sensitive skin, requires no dilution
👑 Helps to calm down before bedtime

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Fractionated Coconut Oil
▫️ Lavender (Lavandula angustifolia)

## Indications

🔹 Evening ritual, calming a child, spot care for irritated skin

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply to temples and the back of the neck for a soothing effect
🔹 Apply to the bottom of a child's feet before sleep
🔹 Apply topically to areas with skin irritation

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Classically pairs with Peppermint Touch and Frankincense Touch

## Interesting Facts

👑 Lavender is one of the most popular essential oils in the world due to its variety of uses
👑 The roll-on format is designed specifically for children and sensitive skin

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
