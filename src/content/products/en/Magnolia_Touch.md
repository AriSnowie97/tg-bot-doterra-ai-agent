# Magnolia Touch

**Category:** Rollers (Touch)

**Type:** Single oil, Touch (10 mL roll-on)


---

## Short description

👑 Fruity-floral magnolia aroma with a relaxing effect
👑 Moisturizes and soothes the skin
👑 Valuable ingredient in high-end perfumery

## Composition and key ingredients

▫️ Fractionated Coconut Oil
▫️ Magnolia (Magnolia spp.)

## Indications

🔹 Personal fragrance, evening relaxation ritual, dry skin care

## Directions for use

🔹 Apply to wrists when feeling tension throughout the day
🔹 Combine with Bergamot or Ylang Ylang for healthy-looking skin
🔹 Apply to the bottoms of feet before bed for a sense of calm

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products

🔹 Pairs well with Lavender, Bergamot, doTERRA Serenity for an evening ritual

## Interesting facts

👑 The main chemical component of magnolia is linalool, similar to lavender and bergamot
👑 The flower is traditionally used in Chinese health practices

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
