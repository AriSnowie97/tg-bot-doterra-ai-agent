# Cinnamon Bark

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Essential Oil (Single, 15 mL)


---

## Brief Description

👑 Spicy oil to support the urinary system and kidneys  
👑 Part of a dental routine for clean gums and teeth  
👑 Warm, spicy aroma  

## Ingredients and Key Components

▫️ *Cinnamomum zeylanicum* (cinnamon) essential oil  

## Indications

🔹 Urinary system and kidney support  
🔹 Oral care  

## Directions for Use

🔹 Internal: 1 drop in a capsule  
🔹 Add to toothpaste or mouthwash (in a small amount)  

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 dōTERRA On Guard® — cinnamon is a key ingredient in the blend  

## Fun Facts

👑 One of the five key oils in the On Guard® blend  

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Strong ("hot") oil — always dilute with a carrier oil before applying to the skin, especially for children and sensitive skin. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
