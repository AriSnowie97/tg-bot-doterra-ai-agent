# dōTERRA Roam™ Portable Diffuser

**Category:** Diffusers

**Type:** Portable Cordless Ultrasonic Diffuser

---

## Short Description

👑 Cordless, battery-powered ultrasonic diffuser with a charging dock so you can diffuse essential oils anywhere in your home
👑 Crafted with natural, polished ground stone so each diffuser has a one-of-a-kind appearance
👑 Made with eco-friendly post-consumer recycled resin

## Key Features & Specifications

▫️ Water capacity: 100 mL; room coverage up to 270 sq. ft. (25 sq. m.)
▫️ Battery run time: up to 4 hours continuous or 8 hours intermittent
▫️ Docked run time: up to 5 hours continuous or 10 hours intermittent
▫️ Ambient white, warm, and cyan light options; automatic safety shut-off

## Directions for Use

🔹 Fill with clean water to the max line and add 3–5 drops of doTERRA essential oil
🔹 Use docked or cord-free on battery power
🔹 Choose continuous or intermittent mist mode

## Precautions

> ⚠️ Use only clean water and pure essential oils. Keep upright when filled with water. Keep out of reach of children.
