# Blue Tansy

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Essential Oil (single, 5 ml)


---

## Short Description

👑 Rich blue oil for a restorative massage
👑 Soothes skin irritation and redness
👑 Soft, sweet, herbaceous aroma

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Tanacetum annuum (Blue Tansy) essential oil

## Indications

🔹 Recovery after physical exertion
🔹 Care for sensitive or irritated skin

## Directions for Use

🔹 Topically: dilute with a carrier oil for massage (may stain skin/fabrics — dilute carefully)

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Peace® Touch — contains Blue Tansy in its formulation

## Fun Facts

👑 Rich blue color comes from chamazulene, which is formed during distillation
👑 Sold in a 5 ml bottle due to the high concentration of active substances

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
