# Pilōt™ Portable Diffuser (and Pilōt™ MAX)

**Category:** Diffusers

**Type:** Portable Car & Travel Ultrasonic Diffuser

---

## Short Description

👑 Compact, rechargeable diffuser designed for your car, office, or travels
👑 Fits neatly in car cup holders and includes a dual-port 2.4A USB car adapter and carrying case
👑 Also available in **Pilōt™ MAX** with 120 mL water capacity, up to 300 sq. ft. coverage, Ultra Mist Mode, and up to 6 hours continuous / 12 hours intermittent run time

## Key Features & Specifications

▫️ Rechargeable USB battery for cord-free diffusion
▫️ Pilōt™ run time: up to 4 hours continuous or 8 hours intermittent
▫️ Eco-friendly materials and neutral, sleek design
▫️ Includes custom travel carrying case

## Directions for Use

🔹 Add clean water to the fill line and 2–4 drops of your favorite doTERRA essential oil
🔹 Select continuous or intermittent mist mode
🔹 Empty and wipe clean before packing for travel

## Precautions

> ⚠️ Do not overfill. Empty water before tilting or storing in luggage. Keep out of reach of children.
