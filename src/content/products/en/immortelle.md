# Immortelle — Anti-Aging Blend

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Blend (Anti-Aging Blend)


---

## Brief Description

👑 One of doTERRA's most precious blends for skin care of the face, neck, and décolletage
👑 Warm, woody-spicy, rich aroma — Frankincense, Hawaiian Sandalwood, Lavender, Myrrh, Helichrysum, Rose
👑 Ready to use in a roll-on bottle, easily absorbed into the skin

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Frankincense (Boswellia), Hawaiian Sandalwood (Santalum paniculatum), Lavender (Lavandula angustifolia), Myrrh (Commiphora myrrha), Helichrysum (Helichrysum italicum), Rose (Rosa damascena)
▫️ Hawaiian Sandalwood was traditionally used as incense in religious ceremonies for its sweet, woody, grounding aroma. Ancient records show: Myrrh was once worth its weight in gold — it has been used in perfumery and religious rituals for thousands of years. Helichrysum comes from Albania and Corsica — a mountain wildflower from the Asteraceae family

## Indications

🔹 Fine lines and wrinkles: regular use reduces their visible appearance
🔹 Loss of skin firmness: supports smoothness and a radiant appearance of the face
🔹 Problem areas: spot application on blemishes or skin imperfections
🔹 Nail care: rolling over the nail bed nourishes and strengthens
🔹 Spiritual practice: associated with peace, trust, and inner stillness — popular for morning mindfulness rituals

## Directions for Use

🔹 Aromatic use: the scent unfolds mainly when applied topically to the skin, rather than through a diffuser
🔹 Topical use: apply a thin layer to the face, neck, and décolletage, repeat on problem areas; incorporate into morning and evening skincare routines; roll over the nail bed; follow with a moisturizer of choice
🔹 Internal use: not recommended for internal consumption — the blend is intended exclusively for topical use

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 What it can be paired with: Frankincense, Lavender, as well as used as a base for Veráge Immortelle Hydrating Serum. Diffuser blends: Morning Ritual (2 drops Immortelle, 2 drops Frankincense), Evening Calm (2 drops Immortelle, 1 drop Lavender)
