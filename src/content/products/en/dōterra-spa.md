# dōTERRA Spa — Hand & Body Lotion

**Category:** Personal Care

**Type:** Hand & Body Lotion


---

## Short Description

👑 Light, weightless hand and body lotion with no added fragrance — a base for personal essential oil blends
👑 Absorbs quickly, leaves no greasy residue
👑 Part of the dōTERRA SPA line for home spa care

## Ingredients & Key Ingredients

▫️ Sunflower seed oil, macadamia nut oil, jojoba seed oil
▫️ Murumuru seed butter, theobroma (cocoa) seed butter, coconut oil, botanical extracts

## Indications

🔹 Hydration: nourishes dry skin on hands and body, suitable for frequent use without feeling greasy
🔹 Personalization: neutral base allows you to add your favorite essential oil for your own custom scent
🔹 Home spa ritual: creates a pleasant, relaxing body care experience

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply generously to hands and body to relieve dry skin
🔹 Mix a drop of your favorite doTERRA essential oil in your palm with the lotion for a personalized fragrance. Suitable for regular use

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Pairs well with citrus oils (Wild Orange, Bergamot) for an invigorating scent, or with Lavender, Roman Chamomile for a relaxing evening ritual. Part of the broader dōTERRA SPA line

## Interesting Facts

👑 The formula is fragrance-free specifically so that a person can create their own aromatic composition
👑 Murumuru seed butter originates from the Amazon and is traditionally used to nourish skin and hair
👑 The lotion is formulated for quick absorption without feeling greasy on the skin
