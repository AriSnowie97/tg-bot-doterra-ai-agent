# TerraShield — Repellent Blend

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Blend (Repellent Blend)

## Short Description

👑 Natural essential oil-based insect repellent — an alternative to synthetic products
👑 Fresh, woody aroma — citronella, lemongrass, thyme, cedarwood, geranium, peppermint in a sesame seed oil base
👑 EPA-registered repellent in the US, over 90% effective for four hours

## Ingredients & Key Components

▫️ Citronella and Cedarwood — the base of the formula (1.5% of each component), complemented by eleven other therapeutic oils
▫️ Sesame Seed Oil — used as a base that enhances essential oil absorption and extends the duration of the scent on the skin
▫️ Ingredients: Citronella (Cymbopogon nardus), Lemongrass (Cymbopogon flexuosus), Thyme (Thymus vulgaris), Cedarwood (Juniperus virginiana), Geranium (Pelargonium graveolens), Peppermint (Mentha piperita), in a Sesame Seed Oil base

## Indications / Uses

🔹 Mosquito protection outdoors: an effective natural repellent for hiking, camping, or picnics
🔹 Gardening: protects while working in the garden or yard
🔹 Family safety: a gentle and safe formula for both children and adults
🔹 Outdoor activities: suitable for any outdoor activity

## Directions for Use

🔹 Aromatic method: diffuse on a patio or in the garden for added insect protection; spray around doors, windows, and tents to protect space
🔹 Topical method: apply to arms, legs, and neck before going outdoors; dilute 1-3 drops with a carrier oil and apply to the desired area; spray version can be sprayed directly onto skin or clothing
🔹 Internal method: not recommended for internal use — the blend is intended for aromatic and topical use

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Can be blended with: Lemongrass (for diffuser: 3 drops TerraShield, 1 drop Lemongrass)

## Interesting Facts

👑 The primary function of essential oils in plants is natural protection against insects and predators, which is the exact principle this formula is based on
👑 One package provides protection for up to six hours after a single application
