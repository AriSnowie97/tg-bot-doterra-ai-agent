# Air-X® Air Blend

**Category:** Blends

**Type:** Essential Air Blend (15 ml)


---

## Brief Description

👑 Refreshes indoor air with a citrusy-woody note
👑 Creates an invigorating yet calming atmosphere
👑 Can also be used topically thanks to its cleansing properties

## Ingredients and Key Components

▫️ Litsea
▫️ Mandarin / Tangerine
▫️ Grapefruit
▫️ Frankincense
▫️ Cardamom

## Indications

🔹 Refreshing the air in the home or office
🔹 Creating an invigorating, clean atmosphere
🔹 Gentle topical support for skin cleanliness

## Directions for Use

🔹 Diffusion: 3–5 drops in a diffuser
🔹 Topical use: 1–2 drops to desired area, diluted with a carrier oil
🔹 Room spray: a few drops in a spray bottle with water

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 abōde Refreshing Blend — for comprehensive home cleansing
🔹 dōTERRA On Guard® — for seasonal atmospheric support in the home

## Interesting Facts

👑 Limonene, a key chemical component of most oils in the blend, is known for its cleansing properties
👑 Frankincense and Cardamom create a deep, grounding base note for the aroma

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas. Avoid direct sunlight and UV rays for up to 12 hours after applying product.
