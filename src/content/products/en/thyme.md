## Cautions

> ⚠️ Powerful ("hot") oil — always dilute with a carrier oil before applying to skin, especially for children and sensitive skin. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
