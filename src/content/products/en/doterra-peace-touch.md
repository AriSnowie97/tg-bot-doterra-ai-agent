# dōTERRA Peace® Touch

## Short Description

👑 Minty-floral blend to soothe anxious feelings
👑 A reminder that you don't have to be perfect to find peace
👑 Convenient format for quick application during stressful moments

## Indications

🔹 Feelings of anxiety, fear, or overwhelm
🔹 Support during meditation or yoga
🔹 Calming a restless child

🔹 Apply to temples while repeating affirmations of peace
🔹 Apply to tense shoulders
🔹 Apply to palms, rub together, and inhale deeply during anxious moments
🔹 Apply to the bottoms of a child's feet to soothe
