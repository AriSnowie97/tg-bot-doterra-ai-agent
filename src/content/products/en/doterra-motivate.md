# doTERRA Motivate — Encouraging Blend

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Blend (Encouraging Blend, Emotional Aromatherapy)


---

## Brief Description

👑 An encouraging blend that helps you believe in yourself and find the courage to take action
👑 Fresh, minty-citrus aroma — Peppermint, Clementine, Coriander, Basil, Yuzu, Melissa, Rosemary, Vanilla
👑 One of the six official Emotional Aromatherapy blends — specifically designed for confidence and courage

## Ingredients and Key Components

▫️ Peppermint (Mentha piperita), Clementine (Citrus clementina), Coriander (Coriandrum sativum), Basil (Ocimum basilicum), Yuzu (Citrus junos), Melissa (Melissa officinalis), Rosemary (Rosmarinus officinalis), Vanilla
▫️ Yuzu — the first use of this rare Japanese citrus in doTERRA products, rich in limonene

## Indications

🔹 New Beginning: supports at the start of a new project, study, or career change
🔹 Self-doubt: helps find the courage to believe in yourself again
🔹 Creative block: diffusion helps unlock creative potential
🔹 Tough decision: application before an important choice adds confidence

## Directions for Use

🔹 Aromatic Use: diffuse before an important event or new endeavor; inhale directly from the bottle before a difficult decision
🔹 Topical Use: apply to wrists or neck before a new project (Touch version is ready for use); apply to temples for clarity of thought
🔹 Internal Use: not recommended for internal use — the blend is intended for aromatic and topical application

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 doTERRA Peace (often used together to balance calming anxiety with moving forward with confidence)
🔹 Wild Orange (in the "Creative Energy" diffuser blend)
🔹 Rosemary (in the "Clarity of Mind" diffuser blend)

## Interesting Facts

👑 Motivate is one of six official Emotional Aromatherapy blends, formulated specifically to overcome self-doubt and fear of starting
👑 Yuzu, the Japanese citrus in the blend, is rarely found in other doTERRA products
👑 The company describes the blend as a companion for "every journey that begins with a first step"

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
> ⚠️ Avoid sunlight or UV rays for at least 12 hours after applying product.
> ⚠️ The information provided is for informational purposes only and does not constitute medical advice.
