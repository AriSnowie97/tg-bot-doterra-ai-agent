# doTERRA Cheer — Uplifting Blend

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Blend (Uplifting Blend, Emotional Aromatherapy)


---

## Short Description

👑 An invigorating blend that lifts your mood on gloomy, gray days
👑 Warm, spicy aroma — Wild Orange, Clove, Star Anise, Lemon Myrtle, Nutmeg, Vanilla, Ginger, Cinnamon, and Zdravetz
👑 One of six official Emotional Aromatherapy blends — specifically for optimism and joy

## Ingredients and Key Components

▫️ Wild Orange (Citrus sinensis), Clove (Eugenia caryophyllata), Star Anise (Illicium verum), Lemon Myrtle (Backhousia citriodora), Nutmeg (Myristica fragrans), Vanilla, Ginger (Zingiber officinale), Cinnamon (Cinnamomum zeylanicum), Zdravetz (Geranium macrorrhizum)
▫️ Star Anise consists almost entirely of the phenylpropene anethole — a compound with a powerful energizing effect
▫️ Zdravetz — a rare Bulgarian plant whose name literally translates to "health"
▫️ The chemical profile of the blend is rich in monoterpenes and aldehydes — compounds known for their invigorating and warming properties

## Indications

🔹 Gloomy mood: diffusing on a gray, gloomy day helps uplift the energy of the space
🔹 Seasonal blues: regular use in autumn and winter supports a positive mood
🔹 Morning alertness: applying to palms in the morning sets the tone for the day
🔹 Holiday or special event: warm, spicy aroma creates a festive atmosphere

## Directions for Use

🔹 Aromatic use: add 1–2 drops to a diffuser for an invigorating, energetic atmosphere; apply a drop to palms, rub together, and inhale deeply for an instant mood boost
🔹 Topical use: apply to wrists or neck (the Touch version is ready to use without dilution); add to an Epsom salt bath for invigorating relaxation
🔹 Internal use: not recommended for internal consumption — the blend is intended for aromatic and topical use

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 doTERRA Emotional Aromatherapy System / Touch Kit
🔹 Wild Orange (in the "Sunny Morning" blend)
🔹 Cinnamon (in the "Cozy Evening" blend)

👑 doTERRA Cheer is part of the official Emotional Aromatherapy system developed by the company to manage emotions through aroma

👑 One drop on the palms and a deep inhale is the company's recommended simplest way to quickly boost your mood

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas. Avoid sunlight or UV rays for up to 12 hours after applying product (photosensitizing effect of citrus and spices).
