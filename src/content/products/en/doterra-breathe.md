# doTERRA Breathe — Respiratory Blend

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Blend (Respiratory Blend)


---

## Brief Description

👑 Respiratory blend that supports feelings of clear airways and easy breathing
👑 Airy, fresh, minty aroma — a combination of Laurel Leaf, Peppermint, Eucalyptus, Tea Tree, Lemon, Cardamom, Ravintsara, and Ravensara
👑 One of doTERRA's most popular blends — helps during seasonal respiratory threats

## Ingredients and Key Components

▫️ Laurel Leaf (Laurus nobilis), Peppermint (Mentha piperita), Eucalyptus (Eucalyptus spp.), Tea Tree (Melaleuca alternifolia)
▫️ Lemon (Citrus limon), Cardamom (Elettaria cardamomum), Ravintsara (Cinnamomum camphora ct. cineole), Ravensara (Ravensara aromatica)

## Indications

🔹 Seasonal respiratory threats: helps minimize the impact of seasonal factors on feelings of clear breathing
🔹 Nighttime rest: diffusing in the evening creates conditions for a calm, restorative sleep
🔹 Congestion: topical application to the chest and back provides a cooling, soothing effect
🔹 Outdoor activity: use before a walk or exercise during seasonal respiratory threats
🔹 Room air freshness: diffusing refreshes the air and creates a pleasant atmosphere

## Directions for Use

🔹 Aromatic method: add 3-4 drops to a diffuser, inhale directly from the bottle, or spray in the room
🔹 Topical method: apply 1-2 drops to the chest, back, or bottoms of feet; dilute with a carrier oil for sensitive skin
🔹 Internal method: not recommended for internal use — the blend is intended for aromatic and topical application

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Nighttime Rest: 3 drops doTERRA Breathe, 2 drops Lavender
🔹 Fresh Air: 3 drops doTERRA Breathe, 2 drops Wild Orange, 1 drop Bergamot
🔹 For Outdoor Activity: 3 drops doTERRA Breathe, 2 drops Spearmint
🔹 Room Refresh: 3 drops doTERRA Breathe, 2 drops Lime

## Fun Facts

👑 One of the primary chemical constituents of Laurel Leaf is eucalyptol, the same constituent that gives Eucalyptus its characteristic properties
👑 Cardamom is a close relative of ginger with a distinct spicy, warm aroma
👑 doTERRA Breathe is one of the most multi-component blends in the company's portfolio, combining eight different oils
👑 Laurel Leaf symbolized victory in Greek mythology
👑 Ravintsara and Ravensara are closely related plants from the Lauraceae family that are often confused
👑 Eucalyptus belongs to the Myrtaceae family; doTERRA uses a blend of five eucalyptus species

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
> Dilute with a carrier oil for children and sensitive skin. Do not apply directly under the nose of young children — the effect is too intense. The information provided is intended solely for educational purposes and does not constitute medical advice.
