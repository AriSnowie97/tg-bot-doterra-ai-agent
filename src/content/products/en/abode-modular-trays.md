# abōde Modular Trays

**Category:** Home Products

**Type:** Accessory — modular storage trays

## Description

👑 Stylish modular walnut wood trays for abōde products
👑 Keep kitchen or bathroom countertops organized
👑 Removable silicone insert for a non-slip, easy-to-clean surface

## Materials and Key Components

▫️ Natural walnut wood
▫️ Removable silicone insert

## Indications

🔹 Organizing abōde line products on the countertop
🔹 Aesthetic addition to kitchen or bathroom decor

## Directions for Use

🔹 Place abōde dispenser bottles on trays (dispensers not included)
🔹 Hand wash with mild soap and warm water, do not soak
🔹 Dry thoroughly with a microfiber cloth

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 abōde Surface Spray Dispenser, Dish Soap Dispenser, Hand Wash Dispenser — fit on the trays

## Interesting Facts

👑 Set includes two trays, each holding up to two products
👑 Modular design allows combining trays as needed

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Do not soak in water. Hand wash with mild soap and dry immediately to preserve the wood.
