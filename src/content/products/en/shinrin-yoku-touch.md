# Shinrin-Yoku® Touch

**Category:** Blends

**Type:** Essential Touch Blend (roll-on, 10 ml)

## Brief Description

👑 Calming blend inspired by the practice of "forest bathing"
👑 Creates a forest atmosphere for slowing down and focusing
👑 Rich in terpenes and phytoncides — compounds characteristic of forest air

▫️ Lemon
▫️ Patchouli

▫️ Cardamom

▫️ Lavandin
▫️ Grapefruit
▫️ Geranium
▫️ Lemon Petitgrain

## Indications

🔹 Need to slow down the pace and regain focus
🔹 Creating a calming, grounding atmosphere at home
🔹 Supporting mindfulness and relaxation practices

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply to desired area of the skin — no dilution required
🔹 Use before meditation or mindfulness practice

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Shinrin-Yoku® Forest Bathing Blend — classic (undiluted) version

## Interesting Facts

👑 The practice of "shinrin-yoku" (forest bathing) originated in Japan in the early 1980s as a way to reconnect with nature
👑 Phytoncides — limonene, alpha- and beta-pinene — are compounds that trees use to protect themselves from pests

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas. Avoid direct sunlight or UV rays for up to 12 hours after applying product.
