# Vetiver Touch

**Category:** Roll-ons (Touch)

**Type:** Single oil, Touch (10 ml roll-on)


---

## Short Description

👑 Warm, woody oil with a gentle effect
👑 Nourishes and moisturizes the skin
👑 Ideal for an evening bedtime ritual

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Fractionated Coconut Oil
▫️ Vetiver — grass root (Vetiveria zizanoides)

## Indications

🔹 Evening bedtime ritual, skin care, post-workout massage

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply to wrists, temples, neck, or feet before sleep
🔹 Use as part of your skin care routine
🔹 Add to a massage after physical exertion

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Pairs well with Lavender and Frankincense in an evening ritual

## Interesting Facts

👑 Vetiver is obtained from grass roots that are left in the soil longer for high oil quality
👑 doTERRA sources vetiver through a partnership in Haiti

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
