# Birch

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Essential Oil (Single, 15 ml)


---

## Short Description

👑 Rare woody oil with a cooling effect for massage
👑 Supports healthy-looking skin when applied topically
👑 Powerful, minty-spicy aroma similar to Wintergreen

## Ingredients and Key Components

▫️ Betula lenta (black/sweet birch) essential oil

## Uses

🔹 Cooling back and leg massage post-workout
🔹 Masking unwanted odors
🔹 Skin care

## Directions for Use

🔹 Diffusion: 3-4 drops (powerful aroma, 1-2 drops are sufficient)
🔹 Topical: dilute with a carrier oil

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Wintergreen — similar aromatic profile, but different plants

## Fun Facts

👑 doTERRA is the first company to establish a sustainable commercial supply of 100% pure Birch oil
👑 It takes about 3 tons of bark to produce 1 quart of oil

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
