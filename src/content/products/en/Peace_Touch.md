# Peace Touch

**Category:** Touch (Roll-ons)

**Type:** Essential oil blend, Touch (10 ml roll-on)


---

## Short Description

👑 A gentle blend of floral and mint oils for peace
👑 A reminder: you don't have to be perfect to find peace
👑 Portable format for any moment of the day

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Fractionated Coconut Oil
▫️ Vetiver, Lavender, Ylang Ylang
▫️ Frankincense, Clary Sage, Marjoram
▫️ Cistus, Spearmint

## Indications

🔹 Feelings of anxiety or overwhelm, the need to pause and exhale

## Directions for Use

🔹 Rub into tense shoulders
🔹 Apply one drop to palms, rub together, and inhale deeply
🔹 Apply to the feet of a restless child

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Part of the doTERRA Emotional Aromatherapy Touch Kit

## Interesting Facts

👑 The formula combines floral, mint, and resinous oils
👑 Created as a reminder to pause and restore inner balance

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
