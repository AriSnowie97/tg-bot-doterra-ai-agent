## Short Description

👑 Soothing, cooling vapor of Breathe oil in a roll-on format
👑 Promotes feelings of easy breathing
👑 Suitable for the whole family

▫️ Fractionated Coconut Oil
▫️ Laurel Leaf, Peppermint, Eucalyptus

▫️ Ravintsara, Ravensara

## Indications

🔹 Seasonal respiratory threats, preparation for rest

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply to chest, back, or feet during times of seasonal threats
🔹 Use when outdoors
🔹 Apply to neck, chest, back, or feet before bedtime

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

👑 The main component of eucalyptus is 1,8-cineole, known for its refreshing properties
👑 The Touch format is safe for children thanks to pre-dilution

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
