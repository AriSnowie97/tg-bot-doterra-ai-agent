# AromaTouch — Massage Blend

**Category:** Essential oils

**Type:** Blend (Massage Blend)


---

## Short Description

👑 Signature massage blend, specifically developed to complement the AromaTouch Technique
👑 Fresh, clean, minty-powdery aroma that combines Cypress, Peppermint, Marjoram, Basil, Grapefruit, and Lavender
👑 Soothes and softens the skin, making any massage more comfortable

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Cypress (Cupressus sempervirens) — a tall evergreen tree common in southern Europe and western Asia, rich in monoterpenes beneficial for the skin
▫️ Peppermint (Mentha piperita) — a hybrid of watermint and spearmint, first described by Carl Linnaeus in 1753
▫️ Basil (Ocimum basilicum) — a member of the mint family, traditionally used in both culinary arts and aromatherapy
▫️ Marjoram (Origanum majorana)
▫️ Grapefruit (Citrus paradisi)
▫️ Lavender (Lavandula angustifolia)

## Indications

🔹 Muscle tension: helps reduce the feeling of tension in muscles during and after a massage
🔹 Stressful situations: application before an important event helps calm nerves
🔹 Home spa ritual: creates a professional spa atmosphere without visiting a salon
🔹 Skin care during massage: soothes and softens the skin thanks to the combination of Cypress and Lavender
🔹 Emotional connection: AromaTouch Hand Technique strengthens the emotional connection between people through touch

## Directions for Use

🔹 Add 3-4 drops to a diffuser to create a spa atmosphere at home
🔹 Inhale directly before a massage to tune in for relaxation
🔹 Apply to neck and shoulders to relieve tension
🔹 Dilute with a carrier oil (5 drops per 10 ml) for a full massage
🔹 Use in the AromaTouch Hand Technique for massaging a loved one's hands
🔹 Add to Epsom salt and dissolve in a hot bath for relaxation

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Lavender (for a spa atmosphere)
🔹 Peppermint and Eucalyptus (post-workout)
🔹 Vetiver (for evening relaxation)
🔹 Bergamot (before an important event)
🔹 Used in the AromaTouch Training Kit and Refill Kit for learning the technique

## Interesting Facts

👑 AromaTouch is the key oil in Dr. Hill's signature technique of the same name, which combines touch and aromatherapy
👑 α-Pinene, one of the main components of Cypress, helps reduce the appearance of skin imperfections
👑 High menthol content in Peppermint creates an invigorating, refreshing effect during physical activity
👑 Fresh minty-powdery aroma is a recognizable trait that sets AromaTouch apart from other doTERRA massage blends

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas. Dilute with a carrier oil before massage to minimize skin sensitivity. Do not apply to damaged or irritated skin.
