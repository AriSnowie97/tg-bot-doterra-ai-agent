# HD Clear® Topical Blend (Roll-On)

**Category:** Blends

**Type:** Essential oil blend for skin (roll-on, 10 ml)


---

## Description

👑 Foundation of the HD Clear system for clean, smooth skin
👑 Formulated with Black Cumin seed oil and essential oils
👑 Suitable for targeted application on problem skin areas

## Ingredients and Key Components

▫️ Black Cumin Seed Oil
▫️ Ho Wood
▫️ Melaleuca (Tea Tree)
▫️ Litsea
▫️ Eucalyptus
▫️ Geranium

## Indications

🔹 Occasional breakouts and skin imperfections
🔹 Need for skin hydration and cleansing
🔹 Skin care for teenagers and adults

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply a thin layer to targeted skin areas or use as a spot treatment
🔹 Dilute with a carrier oil to minimize skin sensitivity
🔹 Use in the evening for clearer skin in the morning

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 HD Clear® Foaming Face Wash — facial cleansing
🔹 Other products in the HD Clear system

## Interesting Facts

👑 The essential oils in the blend are soluble in skin lipids, ensuring deep absorption
👑 The formula is specifically designed for targeted application on the face and body

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas. Avoid direct sunlight and UV rays for up to 12 hours after applying product.
