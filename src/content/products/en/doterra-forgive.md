# doTERRA Forgive — Renewing Blend

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Blend (Renewing Blend, Emotional Aromatherapy)


---

## Short Description

👑 A renewing blend that helps release negative feelings and move forward
👑 Fresh, woody, herbaceous aroma — spruce, bergamot, juniper berry, myrrh, arborvitae, Nootka tree, thyme, citronella
👑 One of six official Emotional Aromatherapy blends — specifically for forgiveness and releasing

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Black Spruce (Picea mariana), Bergamot (Citrus bergamia), Juniper Berry (Juniperus communis), Myrrh (Commiphora myrrha), Arborvitae (Thuja plicata), Nootka tree (Callitropsis nootkatensis), Thyme (Thymus vulgaris), Citronella (Cymbopogon winterianus)
▫️ Nootka wood has a diverse chemical profile rich in soothing and grounding sesquiterpenes
▫️ Pure Thyme balances the blend with powerful renewing and toning properties
▫️ The chemical profile is rich in monoterpene esters and alcohols — compounds that help balance emotions in situations of frustration and stress

## Indications

🔹 Resentment and anger: helps release negative emotions associated with past experiences
🔹 Guilt: supports the process of forgiving oneself and moving forward
🔹 Meditation: diffusing during practice encourages patience and inner peace
🔹 Difficult relationships: application helps approach challenging conversations with greater calm

## Directions for Use

🔹 Diffuse during meditation to encourage patience and contentment
🔹 Inhale directly from the bottle in moments of frustration or resentment
🔹 Apply to the chest, temples, or bottoms of feet while setting an intention at the start of the day
🔹 Apply to wrists (Touch version ready for use)
🔹 Not recommended for internal use — the blend is intended for aromatic and topical application

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Part of the doTERRA Emotional Aromatherapy System / Touch Kit
🔹 Morning Intention (for diffuser): 2 drops doTERRA Forgive, 1 drop Wild Orange
🔹 Meditation (for diffuser): 3 drops doTERRA Forgive, 1 drop Frankincense
🔹 Release the Day (for diffuser): 3 drops doTERRA Forgive

## Interesting Facts

👑 Forgive is one of six official Emotional Aromatherapy blends, specifically formulated to address resentment and anger
👑 Many users apply the blend in the evening as part of a "letting go of the day" ritual before sleep
👑 The fresh, woody aroma distinguishes Forgive from sweeter floral blends in the emotional line

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
> ⚠️ Avoid sunlight or UV rays for up to 12 hours after applying product to the skin
