# doTERRA Anchor — Steadying Blend

**Category:** Essential oils

**Type:** Blend (Steadying Blend, part of the Yoga Collection)


---

## Short Description

👑 Grounding blend from the yoga collection — creates an atmosphere of stability and inner trust
👑 Rich, earthy, spicy-woody aroma — lavender, cedarwood, sandalwood, cinnamon, frankincense, black pepper, patchouli
👑 Ready to use in a roller bottle without additional dilution

## Ingredients and Key Ingredients

▫️ Lavender (Lavandula angustifolia)
▫️ Cedarwood (Cedrus atlantica)
▫️ Sandalwood (Santalum album)
▫️ Cinnamon (Cinnamomum zeylanicum)
▫️ Frankincense (Boswellia)
▫️ Black Pepper (Piper nigrum)
▫️ Patchouli (Pogostemon cablin)

## Indications

🔹 Grounding before practice: ideal for seated poses and meditation at the beginning of a class
🔹 Inner stability: helps to feel confident and stable during difficult periods
🔹 Trust in intuition: supports connection with inner wisdom during practice
🔹 Calming before a difficult decision: grounding effect helps to act with quiet strength

## Directions for Use

🔹 Aromatic method: apply to diffusion jewelry or lava stones before practice, or inhale directly from the bottle before seated meditation
🔹 Topical use: apply to ankles, base of the spine, and soles of feet before practice; combine with seated meditation poses, seated twists, Bhu mudra
🔹 Internal use: not recommended for internal use — the blend is intended for aromatic and topical application

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Part of the doTERRA Yoga Collection (together with Align and Arise)
🔹 Deep meditation (diffuser): 2 drops Anchor, 1 drop Vetiver

## Interesting Facts

👑 Anchor is one of three exclusive blends in the Yoga Collection, designed specifically for practice
👑 The company recommends combining the blend specifically with seated poses — meditation, twists, Bhu mudra
👑 The rich, earthy aroma sets Anchor apart from lighter, floral doTERRA blends

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas. Do not apply to damaged or irritated skin. The information provided is intended solely for informational purposes and does not constitute medical advice.

## Additional Information

Effects on the physical body:
▫️ Grounding, stabilizing
▫️ Toning, warming (thanks to black pepper and cinnamon)
▫️ Calming for the nervous system

Effects on the emotional level:
Removes: feelings of instability, inner insecurity, scattered attention.
Fosters: self-trust, quiet strength, connection with one's own inner wisdom.

Origin and composition:
👑 Cinnamon, along with black pepper, gives the blend a warm, spicy character that balances calming woody notes
👑 Patchouli is traditionally used in perfumery for its deep, grounding, musky aroma
👑 Frankincense and sandalwood are a classic pair for spiritual practices for thousands of years

More about usage:
Anchor is designed as the first step of practice — grounding before movement or meditation, when the body and mind tune into presence here and now. The company describes the blend as one that "brings firm self-trust" — focusing specifically on inner stability rather than external strength. Together with Align and Arise, it forms the complete concept of "grounding — centering — inspiring" for a full yoga practice.

Diffuser blends:
💦 Before practice: 3 drops Anchor
