# doTERRA Deep Blue® Rub

**Category:** Personal Care

**Type:** Topical cream (120 ml)


---

## Short Description
👑 A cooling and warming cream with the Deep Blue blend for massaging problem areas
👑 Gives a cooling, soothing effect on the chosen area
👑 A moisturizing emollient base — skin feels soft and not greasy

## Composition and Key Ingredients
▫️ Deep Blue blend (CPTG): Wintergreen (Gaultheria procumbens), Camphor (Cinnamomum camphora), Peppermint (Mentha piperita), Blue Tansy (Tanacetum annuum), German Chamomile (Matricaria recutita), Helichrysum (Helichrysum italicum), Ylang Ylang (Cananga odorata), Osmanthus (Osmanthus fragrans)
▫️ Other botanical oils and extracts: Eucalyptus (Eucalyptus globulus), Sweet Almond oil, Chlorella extract, Aloe leaf juice powder, Gardenia fruit extract and Capsicum fruit extract
▫️ Other ingredients: Menthol, Allantoin, Retinyl Palmitate, Squalane

## Indications
🔹 Massaging muscles and tension areas — a cooling and warming sensation
🔹 Used by massage therapists and sports practitioners together with the Deep Blue oil blend (per the doTERRA description)

## Directions for Use
🔹 Massage the cream into the affected area
🔹 For a more intensive experience, apply the Deep Blue essential oil blend to the skin first, then the cream
🔹 For external use only

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products
🔹 Deep Blue® Soothing Blend — the essential oil blend (separate KB file)
🔹 Deep Blue Polyphenol Complex — the capsule form for nutritional support (separate KB file)
🔹 Helichrysum, Lemongrass, Lavender — oils for sprains and muscle soreness in the book "Power in Every Drop"

## Fun Facts
👑 The Deep Blue blend is described on the doTERRA page as its top-selling blend
👑 The rub is part of the Deep Blue line alongside the essential oil blend, the stick with copaiba and the Polyphenol Complex

## Cautions
> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas. For external use only; avoid contact with eyes. Do not use on wounds or damaged skin. Do not bandage tightly after application or use with a heating pad. Keep out of reach of children; if swallowed, get medical help immediately. Book "Essential ABC": blends containing Wintergreen and Birch are excluded for people on anticoagulants — the bot routes such requests to a specialist.
