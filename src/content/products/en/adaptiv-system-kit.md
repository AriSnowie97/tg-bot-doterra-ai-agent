# Adaptiv® System

**Category:** Kits

**Type:** Stress management kit


---

## Short Description

👑 A comprehensive toolkit for managing stressful moments
👑 Combines capsules, an essential oil blend, and a roll-on for maximum application flexibility
👑 Provides at least a 30-day supply based on the recommended dosage

## Contents & Key Ingredients

▫️ Adaptiv® Calming Blend Capsules — capsules with clinically studied botanicals and essential oils
▫️ Adaptiv® Calming Blend — 15 mL essential oil blend (Lavender, Copaiba, Rosemary, Magnolia, Neroli, Wild Orange, Spearmint)
▫️ Adaptiv® Touch — 10 mL roll-on of the same blend in Fractionated Coconut Oil

## Indications

🔹 Stress and anxious feelings management
🔹 Adapting to new circumstances or difficult situations

## Directions for Use

🔹 Take capsules daily
🔹 Diffuse or apply Calming Blend or Touch after taking capsules for a synergistic effect

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Each of the three system products is also available separately

## Interesting Facts

👑 Purchasing the system as a kit is more cost-effective than buying each product separately

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
