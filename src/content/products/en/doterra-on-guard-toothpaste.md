# dōTERRA On Guard® Toothpaste

**Category:** Home Products

**Type:** Toothpaste


---

## Short Description

👑 Fluoride-free whitening toothpaste based on the On Guard protective blend
👑 Gently polishes and whitens teeth, reducing plaque
👑 Refreshing cinnamon and mint flavor

## Composition & Key Ingredients

▫️ On Guard® essential blend (wild orange, clove, cinnamon, eucalyptus, rosemary)
▫️ Peppermint and Wintergreen — for flavor
▫️ Hydrated silica — a gentle natural abrasive for polishing
▫️ Xylitol — a natural sweetener

## Indications

🔹 Daily oral hygiene
🔹 Maintaining clean and white teeth

## Directions for Use

🔹 After flossing, apply a pea-sized amount of paste to a moistened toothbrush
🔹 Brush teeth thoroughly, preferably after each meal, as well as morning and night
🔹 For enhanced results — use together with dōTERRA On Guard® Mouth Rinse

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 dōTERRA On Guard® Mouth Rinse — for comprehensive oral care

## Interesting Facts

👑 Fluoride-free formula, safe even if a child accidentally swallows a small amount of paste
👑 Hydroxyapatite in the ingredients promotes remineralization and strengthening of tooth enamel

## Cautions

> ⚠️ For external use only (do not swallow intentionally). Keep out of reach of children. Avoid contact with eyes.
