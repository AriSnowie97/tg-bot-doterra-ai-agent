# Lemon / Lemon

**Category:** Essential oils

**Type:** Essential oil (single)

## Short Description

👑 Fresh, invigorating oil with powerful cleansing and detoxifying properties
👑 Supports the immune system, improves mood and energy
👑 Used for purifying air, surfaces, as well as in food and beverage preparation

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Lemon essential oil (Citrus limon) — obtained by cold pressing the peel
▫️ d-Limonene (60–70%) — main active component, powerful antioxidant
▫️ β-pinene (10–15%)
▫️ γ-terpinene, α-terpinene, sabinene

## Indications

🔹 **Body detoxification:** Supports liver and lymphatic system function with regular consumption with water
🔹 **Immune support:** Rich in antioxidant limonene, strengthens immunity and protects cells from oxidative stress
🔹 **Mood enhancement:** The aroma of lemon is clinically proven to improve mood, reduce anxiety and depressive states
🔹 **Surface cleaning:** Powerful natural antibacterial agent for cleaning kitchen, bathroom, and household surfaces
🔹 **Normalization of digestion:** Stimulates the production of gastric juice, relieves nausea and bloating
🔹 **Skin care:** Lightens age spots, tightens pores, and regulates sebum production
🔹 **Cardiovascular system support:** Reduces LDL cholesterol levels and supports vascular elasticity
🔹 **Odor elimination:** Neutralizes unpleasant odors in the home, refrigerator, shoes

## Directions for Use

🔹 **Aromatic method:** Add 4-5 drops to a diffuser to purify the air and uplift the mood. Inhale directly from the bottle or palms for a quick mood boost and to overcome nausea.
🔹 **Topical method:** Dilute 1-2 drops with a carrier oil and apply to organ projection areas (lymph nodes, liver) or to nails to strengthen them. Add to cosmetic products (creams, masks) exclusively for evening care.
🔹 **Internal method:** Add 1-2 drops to a glass of water, tea, or another beverage. Use in cooking to add a fresh citrus flavor and aroma to dishes.

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

👑 Sailors in the British Navy were given lemons to prevent scurvy as early as the 18th century — this is the first documented case of disease prevention using citrus fruits.
👑 According to Japanese research, the aroma of lemon reduces computer work errors by 54%.
👑 1 drop of doTERRA lemon essential oil is equivalent in aroma and active components to the juice of a whole lemon.

### Precautions and Contraindications
🔹 Photosensitizer — avoid direct sunlight and UV rays for at least 12-24 hours after application to skin.
🔹 Do not apply to sensitive skin without dilution.
🔹 In large quantities, it may irritate mucous membranes during internal use.
🔹 Store in a dark, cool place — the oil is prone to oxidation upon contact with light.
🔹 Check production date — oxidized oil can cause skin irritation.
🔹 Gastric ulcer — avoid internal use in large quantities.
🔹 Hypersensitivity to citrus — perform a patch test before use.
🔹 Direct application to sensitive or damaged skin without dilution is prohibited.
