# Salon Essentials Daily Conditioner

**Category:** Personal Care

**Type:** Conditioner


---

## Short Description

👑 Daily conditioner for shiny, silky hair without weighing it down
👑 Preserves color vibrancy of color-treated hair
👑 Free of sulfates, silicones, phthalates, and parabens

## Ingredients and Key Components

▫️ Essential oils of Peppermint, Eucalyptus, Tea Tree, and Spearmint

## Indications

🔹 Dry, hard-to-style hair
🔹 Need for daily moisture without hair buildup

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply to wet hair after shampooing, focusing on the ends
🔹 Leave on for a few minutes and rinse

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Salon Essentials Protecting Shampoo — previous step
🔹 Hair Care Leave-in Conditioner — additional protection

## Interesting Facts

👑 The formula is designed to maintain color vibrancy without weighing hair down

## Cautions

> ⚠️ For external use only. Avoid direct contact with eyes. Discontinue use if skin irritation occurs. Keep out of reach of children.
