# dōTERRA Yoga Collection

**Category:** Kits

**Type:** Yoga Kit


---

## Short Description

👑 Exclusive trio of blends created specifically for yoga practice
👑 Each blend corresponds to a specific stage or mood of the practice
👑 Pre-diluted roll-ons in Fractionated Coconut Oil

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Anchor Steadying Blend — lavender, cedarwood, sandalwood, cinnamon, frankincense, black pepper, patchouli
▫️ Align Centering Blend — bergamot, coriander, marjoram, peppermint, jasmine, rose
▫️ Arise Enlightening Blend — grapefruit, lemon, osmanthus, melissa, siberian fir

## Indications

🔹 Grounding and focus before practice (Anchor)
🔹 Centering and self-trust during practice (Align)
🔹 Inspiration and unlocking potential (Arise)

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply to heart, wrists, or back of the neck before, during, or after yoga practice
🔹 Combine with specific asanas to enhance the effect

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Each of the three blends is also sold separately

## Fun Facts

👑 Anchor is recommended to be combined with meditation poses and seated twist, Align — with Warrior II and Triangle, Arise — with standing side bend

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
