# doTERRA SuperMint

**Category:** Essential oils

**Type:** Blend (cooling mint blend)


---

## Brief description

👑 Powerful refreshing blend of four different mint species in a single roller bottle
👑 Fresh, herbal, sweet aroma — Peppermint, Japanese Mint, Bergamot Mint, Spearmint
👑 Cools the skin and creates an invigorating, energetic atmosphere

## Composition and key ingredients

▫️ Menthol — the main component of the blend, promotes a cooling sensation when inhaled and on the skin
▫️ Ingredients: Peppermint (Mentha piperita), Japanese Mint (Mentha canadensis), Bergamot Mint (Mentha citrata), Spearmint (Mentha spicata)

## Indications

🔹 Mental fatigue: refreshes and supports concentration in the afternoon
🔹 Physical activity: cooling effect supports stamina during workouts
🔹 Sensation of clear breathing: menthol promotes a feeling of open airways
🔹 Heat: cools the skin in hot weather

## Directions for use

🔹 Inhale directly from the bottle for an instant boost (aromatic method)
🔹 Apply to temples, back of the neck, or wrists (ready for use without dilution)
🔹 Apply before physical activity for a cooling effect
🔹 Internal use: not recommended for internal consumption — the blend is intended for aromatic and topical application

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products

🔹 Focus: 2 drops of SuperMint, 1 drop of Rosemary (for diffuser)
🔹 Energy: 3 drops of SuperMint (for diffuser)

## Interesting facts

👑 SuperMint combines four different mint species at once — a rare combination for a single blend
👑 The roller bottle format makes the blend convenient for a quick refresh throughout the day without needing a diffuser
👑 The company describes the aroma as "crisp, clean, and compelling"

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor’s care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
> 
> ⚠️ Avoid contact with eyes and mucous membranes. Do not use on young children without consultation. Pregnant women and individuals with chronic conditions should consult a doctor before use.
