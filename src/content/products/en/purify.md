# Purify — Cleansing Blend

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Blend (Cleansing Blend)


---

## Brief Description

👑 A cleansing blend that neutralizes unpleasant odors and refreshes the air in a natural, safe way
👑 Fresh, airy, herbal aroma — a combination of Lemon, Siberian Fir, Citronella, Lime, Tea Tree, and Cilantro
👑 A favorite blend for combating stubborn odors at home or in the car

## Ingredients and Key Components

▫️ Lemon (Citrus limon), Siberian Fir (Abies sibirica), Citronella (Cymbopogon winterianus), Lime (Citrus aurantifolia), Tea Tree (Melaleuca alternifolia), Cilantro (Coriandrum sativum)
▫️ Citronella contains citronellal and geraniol; Tea Tree has over 90 different chemical compounds; coriander and cilantro come from the same plant

## Indications

🔹 Unpleasant odors: effectively neutralizes odors in the kitchen, bathroom, or car
🔹 Irritated skin: topical application (diluted) soothes minor skin irritations
🔹 Laundry freshness: adding to the rinse cycle removes stubborn odors from fabrics
🔹 Natural cleaning: the blend diluted with water effectively cleans surfaces
🔹 On-the-go air freshening: a cotton ball with the blend in an air vent refreshes the car interior

## Directions for Use

🔹 Aromatic use: add 3–4 drops to a diffuser to refresh the air, or put a drop on a cotton ball for a car/small room
🔹 Topical use: dilute with a carrier oil and apply to the irritated skin area to soothe
🔹 Household use: add 5 drops to a spray bottle with water to wipe down surfaces, or a few drops to the rinse cycle during laundry
🔹 Internal use: not recommended for internal consumption — the blend is intended for aromatic and topical use

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 What can be combined with: Lemon (for diffuser: 3 drops Purify, 2 drops Lemon)

## Interesting Facts

👑 Purify combines citrus and pine oils — an unusual combination of aromatic families for cleansing blends
👑 Linalool and trans-2-decenal, the main components of cilantro, have properties effective for cleaning surfaces
👑 One of the most versatile blends for practical household use in the doTERRA portfolio

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas. Avoid sunlight or UV rays for up to 12 hours after applying product (citrus components).

## Additional Information

Effect on the physical body:
▫️ Refreshing, cleansing
▫️ Soothing for irritated skin
▫️ Antiseptic (thanks to Tea Tree)
▫️ Toning for room air

Effect on the emotional level:
Removes: feelings of stuffiness or stagnant air, discomfort from unpleasant odors, feelings of a "polluted" space.
Fosters: a sense of cleanliness and freshness, lightness and openness of space, comfort in one's own home.

Diffuser blends:
💦 Kitchen Refresh: 3 drops Purify, 2 drops Lemon
💦 Car Freshness: 3 drops Purify (on a cotton ball in the vent)
💦 Natural Home Cleanliness: 4 drops Purify

More about application:
Purify is specially designed for practical, household use — unlike emotional or meditative blends, its main role is precisely to refresh the space.
A popular life hack is keeping a cotton ball with Purify in the car for continuous interior freshening without synthetic fragrances.
The blend diluted with water is a simple, safe alternative to chemical cleaning products for kitchen surfaces.
