# Holiday Love

**Category:** Seasonal Collection

**Type:** Essential Oil Blend (5 mL, seasonal)


---

## Short Description

👑 Rich, woody-spicy blend for cozy family gatherings
👑 Creates a warm, embracing atmosphere for intimate holidays
👑 The third blend included in the Holiday Trio

## Ingredients & Key Components

▫️ Frankincense, Peru Balsam, Nootka wood
▫️ Labdanum, Cinnamon, Tonka Bean

## Recommended Uses

🔹 Creating a warm, intimate atmosphere for family gatherings
🔹 Cozy candlelight evening with loved ones

## Directions for Use

🔹 Diffusion: Use 3–4 drops in the diffuser
🔹 Ideal for cozy evenings and small gatherings

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Holiday Joy®, Holiday Peace® — other blends in the seasonal collection
🔹 Holiday Trio — a set of all three blends in 5 mL bottles

## Fun Facts

👑 Tonka bean adds a rich, warm, almost almond-like note to the aroma
👑 Labdanum is a resinous oil obtained from the *Cistus ladaniferus* shrub

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
