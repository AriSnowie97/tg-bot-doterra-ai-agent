# Lavender (Lavender)

**Category:** Essential oils

**Type:** Essential oil


---

## Brief Description

👑 One of the most popular essential oils in the world — known for its calming and relaxing properties
👑 Promotes healthy sleep, reduces anxiety and stress
👑 Widely used for skin care: reduces irritation, redness, and accelerates healing

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Lavender (Lavandula angustifolia) — 100% pure therapeutic-grade essential oil
▫️ Linalool (25–38%)
▫️ Linalyl acetate (25–45%)
▫️ β-ocimene, trans-β-ocimene, Camphor

## Indications

🔹 **Sleep disorders and insomnia:** Helps with insomnia and restless sleep, promotes deeper and more restorative rest
🔹 **Stress and anxiety:** Lowers cortisol (stress hormone) levels, calms the nervous system, reduces feelings of panic attacks
🔹 **Headaches and migraines:** Application to the temples and back of the neck helps relieve pain and tension
🔹 **Allergic reactions:** Antihistamine properties help reduce allergy symptoms — itching, swelling, redness
🔹 **Skin care:** Accelerates healing of cuts, burns, insect bites, reduces scars and post-acne
🔹 **Pimples and oily skin:** Antibacterial properties help fight acne and regulate sebum production
🔹 **Muscle pain and spasms:** Relaxes tense muscles, relieves spasms after physical activity
🔹 **Menstrual discomfort:** Relieves cramps and pain during menstruation when applied to the lower abdomen in diluted form
🔹 **Cardiovascular support:** Lowers blood pressure and slows heart rate during stressful conditions

## Directions for Use

🔹 **Aromatic method:** Add 3-4 drops to a diffuser before bedtime; apply a drop to a pillow or bedding; inhale directly from palms for quick calming during moments of stress
🔹 **Topical method:** Apply 1-2 drops to temples and wrists during stress or headaches (can be used neat); apply to insect bites, burns, or cuts; add to cream or lotion to improve skin condition; apply to the soles of the feet before bedtime
🔹 **Internal method:** Take 1-2 drops under the tongue or in a veggie capsule — promotes relaxation and improves sleep quality

## Interesting Facts

👑 Lavender is one of the few essential oils that can be applied to the skin neat (undiluted)
👑 The word 'lavender' comes from the Latin 'lavare' — to wash. The Romans used lavender for bathing and washing clothes
👑 During World War I, lavender was used to disinfect soldiers' wounds when antiseptics were in short supply
👑 Studies show that inhaling lavender reduces heart rate and blood pressure in stressful situations
👑 French chemist René-Maurice Gattefossé discovered the wound-healing properties of lavender in 1910 — after a burn, he submerged his hand in a container of lavender oil and noticed remarkably rapid healing

### Origin and Extraction
* Lavender (Lavandula angustifolia) is an aromatic flowering plant of the Lamiaceae family native to the Mediterranean region. The highest quality oil is produced in France, Bulgaria, and Provence.
* Essential oil is extracted by steam distillation of lavender flowers and flower stalks. It takes approximately 150 kg of flowers to produce 1 kg of oil.

### Scientific Research
* **Effects of lavender aromatherapy on sleep quality in students** (*Journal of Alternative and Complementary Medicine, 2015*). A study involving 79 students showed that inhaling lavender oil significantly improved sleep quality, reduced daytime anxiety levels, and increased morning energy. [Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25849327/)
* **Lavender linalool reduces anxiety and aggression** (*Frontiers in Behavioral Neuroscience, 2018*). Linalool — the main component of lavender — directly activates GABA receptors in the brain, explaining the calming effect without CNS depression, similar to the action of benzodiazepines. [Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30416443/)
* **Antibacterial activity of Lavandula angustifolia** (*Evidence-Based Complementary and Alternative Medicine, 2016*). Lavender oil exhibited significant antibacterial activity against Staphylococcus aureus and Escherichia coli, supporting its use in skin infections. [Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27656239/)

### Drug Interactions
* May enhance the sedative effect of sleeping pills and sedatives (benzodiazepines, barbiturates).
* When taken concurrently with SSRI-type antidepressants — consult a physician.
* May enhance the effect of blood pressure-lowering drugs.

### Dosage
* **Aromatic:** 4-5 drops in a diffuser for 20-30 minutes before bed or during moments of stress.
* **Topical:** 1-3 drops (neat or 1:1 with a carrier oil) on temples, wrists, soles of feet.
* **Internal:** 1-2 drops under the tongue or in a capsule before sleep. Or 1 drop in a cup of chamomile tea.
* **For children:** Safe for children 2 years and older in high dilution (1 drop per 15 mL of carrier oil). Aromatic method — from birth.
* **Notes:** One of the safest oils for broad use. During pregnancy — avoid internal use in the first trimester.

### Additional Precautions and Contraindications
* Perform an allergy patch test on the inside of the wrist before first use.
* In very high doses, it may have a hormone-like effect — do not exceed recommended amounts.
* **Contraindications:** Allergy to plants of the Lamiaceae family (sage, mint, rosemary) — cross-reaction is possible. Excessive internal use during pregnancy (more than 3-4 drops per day) — potential stimulation of uterine contractions. Use with caution in hormone-dependent conditions (due to weak phytoestrogenic activity).
