# dōTERRA Bubble™ Motion-Activated Diffuser

**Category:** Diffusers

**Type:** Waterless Motion-Activated Nebulizing Mini-Diffuser

---

## Short Description

👑 Small, waterless nebulizing diffuser with an infrared motion sensor that hibernates until it detects movement, then releases a burst of aroma
👑 Attaches directly to standard 5 mL and 15 mL doTERRA essential oil bottles — no water or heat needed
👑 Ideal for entryways, bathrooms, closets, and small personal spaces

## Key Features & Specifications

▫️ Infrared motion sensor: 13-foot (4 m) range, 120-degree field of view
▫️ Coverage up to 110 sq. ft. (10 sq. m.)
▫️ Normal and high output modes; USB-rechargeable battery (up to 12 hours run time)
▫️ Compatible with both 5 mL and 15 mL doTERRA oil bottles

## Directions for Use

🔹 Remove the cap and orifice reducer from a 5 mL or 15 mL bottle and attach per the manual
🔹 Select normal or high mode and place upright on a flat surface

## Precautions

> ⚠️ Keep upright while a bottle is attached. Do not use with carrier oils. Keep out of reach of children.
