# Dill

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Essential Oil (Single, limited edition)


---

## Short Description
👑 An oil from dill seed (Anethum graveolens) with a fresh, grassy aroma
👑 Officially described as possibly helping lessen stress and anxious feelings when used aromatically
👑 A limited-time item — not available for regular purchase, only during promotions

## Composition and Key Ingredients
▫️ Anethum graveolens (dill) essential oil, seed
▫️ The main component is carvone; the doTERRA page cites a preclinical study on its calming effect

## Indications
🔹 Aromatic use to help lessen stress and anxious feelings (per the doTERRA description)
🔹 Nervous system support — the page cites a preclinical study on carvone
🔹 Culinary use: flavor for salads, potatoes, fish, curry, vegetables and rice

## Directions for Use
🔹 Aromatic: diffusion; the official page suggests pairing with citrus oils (Lime, Wild Orange, Bergamot, Lemon)
🔹 Internal use: per the knowledge-base rules this is a higher-risk category (only if the bottle carries a “Supplement Facts” panel); the bot does not give dosages — such questions are routed to a specialist
🔹 The official page does not describe topical use

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products
🔹 Lime, Wild Orange, Bergamot, Lemon — citrus oils for diffuser blends
🔹 Lemongrass, Fennel — Symphony of the Cells lists Dill as an alternative to these oils

## Fun Facts
👑 The name "dill" comes from the Norse "dilla", meaning "to lull"
👑 Dill is an annual that typically grows three to five feet (about 1-1.5 m) tall
👑 Dill oil is a limited-edition doTERRA product

## Cautions
> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
