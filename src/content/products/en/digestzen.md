# DigestZen — Digestive Blend

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Blend (Digestive Blend)


---

## Short Description

👑 Digestive blend known as "tummy tamer" — reduces occasional stomach discomfort and supports healthy digestion
👑 Spicy, warm aroma — a combination of ginger, fennel, peppermint, coriander, tarragon, anise, and caraway
👑 Can be used aromatically, topically, and internally — one of the most versatile blends by method of application

## Ingredients and Key Ingredients

▫️ Tarragon — a herbaceous plant traditionally used in cooking and herbal medicine to support digestion
▫️ Fennel — a plant of the celery family, long known for properties that support metabolism and digestion
▫️ Anise — one of the oldest spice plants, used in Ancient Egypt to support digestion
▫️ Main composition: ginger (Zingiber officinale), fennel (Foeniculum vulgare), peppermint (Mentha piperita), coriander (Coriandrum sativum), tarragon (Artemisia dracunculus), anise (Pimpinella anisum), caraway (Carum carvi)

## Indications

🔹 Occasional stomach discomfort: helps with feelings of heaviness or discomfort after meals
🔹 Bloating and gas: supports the natural reduction of gas formation
🔹 Motion sickness: aromatic use helps with nausea during travel
🔹 New food or travel: internal use supports digestion when changing diets
🔹 General gastrointestinal support: regular support for healthy digestive system function

## Directions for Use

🔹 **Aromatic method:** inhale directly from the bottle before a flight or car trip; apply a drop to a scarf or neck pillow while traveling
🔹 **Topical method:** dilute with a carrier oil and massage the abdomen for a soothing effect; apply to the abdomen before a flight or long ride
🔹 **Internal method:** add 1 drop to 120 ml of liquid and drink to support digestion; add to tea or water when trying new food or after a heavy meal; take on the road while traveling

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Stomach soothing: 3 drops DigestZen, 2 drops Ginger
🔹 Travel blend: 2 drops DigestZen, 2 drops Peppermint
🔹 After a heavy meal: 3 drops DigestZen, 1 drop Wild Orange

## Interesting Facts

👑 DigestZen received the informal name "tummy tamer" due to its popularity specifically for digestive comfort
👑 One of doTERRA's most multi-component blends — combines seven different oils
👑 The formula became the foundation for an entire line of products: Softgels, DigestTab, Touch, TerraZyme
👑 Especially popular among frequent travelers or those trying new cuisine in other countries
