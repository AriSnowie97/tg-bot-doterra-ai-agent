# Wintergreen

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Essential Oil (single, 15 ml)


---

## Short Description

👑 Herbal oil for massage during post-exertion recovery
👑 Helps reduce occasional stress
👑 Fresh, minty aroma

## Composition & Key Ingredients

▫️ Gaultheria procumbens (Wintergreen) essential oil

## Indications

🔹 Relaxation massage after exertion
🔹 Reducing feelings of stress

## Directions for Use

🔹 Massage with a carrier oil after a workout
🔹 Add a drop to a cleanser to target blemishes

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products

🔹 Deep Blue® — wintergreen is close in profile to the composition of this blend

## Fun Facts

👑 The aroma is similar to Birch, although these are different plants

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children (packaged with a child-resistant safety cap). Topical/aromatic use only — never ingest internally! Strictly contraindicated for individuals on anticoagulant medications (blood thinners), with bleeding disorders, pregnancy, or for small children.
