# dōTERRA Motivate® Touch

**Category:** Blends

**Type:** Essential Oil Touch Blend (roll-on, 10 ml)

## Short Description

👑 Mint and citrus blend for confidence and inspiration
👑 Helps you gather yourself before important tasks or events
👑 Fresh, invigorating aroma for daily use

## Ingredients and Key Components

▫️ Peppermint
▫️ Clementine
▫️ Coriander
▫️ Basil
▫️ Yuzu
▫️ Melissa
▫️ Rosemary
▫️ Vanilla Absolute

## Indications

🔹 Feelings of doubt, pessimism, or insecurity
🔹 Preparation for important events, presentations, or competitions
🔹 Maintaining motivation for study or work

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply to wrists, rub together, and inhale for a sense of confidence
🔹 Apply to shirt collar or hood for aroma throughout the day
🔹 Apply to temples to set a productive mood for the day
🔹 Apply to pulse points before athletic competitions

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 dōTERRA Motivate® — classic (neat) blend
🔹 InTune® Focus Blend — for concentration on tasks

## Interesting Facts

👑 High content of monoterpenes and monoterpene alcohols provides a toning effect
👑 Suitable for children as well, thanks to gentle pre-dilution

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas. Avoid sunlight and UV rays for at least 12 hours after applying product.
