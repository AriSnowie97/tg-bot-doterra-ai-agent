# PastTense — Tension Blend

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Blend (Tension Blend)


---

## Short Description

👑 A soothing blend that helps stay focused and grounded during stressful moments
👑 Fresh, minty, herbal aroma — wintergreen, lavender, peppermint, frankincense, cilantro, marjoram, Roman chamomile, basil, rosemary
👑 Provides a cooling, comforting effect when applied to the neck and temples

## Ingredients and Key Components

▫️ Wintergreen (Gaultheria procumbens)
▫️ Lavender (Lavandula angustifolia)
▫️ Peppermint (Mentha piperita)
▫️ Frankincense (Boswellia)
▫️ Cilantro (Coriandrum sativum)
▫️ Marjoram (Origanum majorana)
▫️ Roman Chamomile (Anthemis nobilis)
▫️ Basil (Ocimum basilicum)
▫️ Rosemary (Rosmarinus officinalis)

## Indications

🔹 Before an exam or performance: helps stay focused and grounded during a stressful moment
🔹 Muscle tension in the neck and shoulders: massage with a diluted blend provides a cooling, comforting effect
🔹 Travel: application to the wrists soothes on the go
🔹 Difficult conversation or event: applying to the temples before the event helps maintain inner calm

## Directions for Use

🔹 Inhale directly from the bottle before an exam, presentation, or date
🔹 Diffuse for a calming, balanced atmosphere
🔹 Apply to the neck, shoulders, or behind the ears for a cooling, comforting effect
🔹 Massage shoulders, neck, and back to relieve tension
🔹 Apply to temples and the back of the neck before a test or public speaking
🔹 Apply to wrists while traveling for a soothing aroma

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Lavender (to create a calming diffuser blend)
🔹 Peppermint (to create a travel diffuser blend)

## Interesting Facts

👑 PastTense combines nine different oils — one of the most multi-component tension relief blends in the doTERRA portfolio
👑 The company supports local communities in Nepal through the Co-Impact Sourcing program during wintergreen harvesting
👑 The cooling effect of wintergreen and peppermint makes the blend a popular alternative to classic warming products for the neck and shoulders

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
