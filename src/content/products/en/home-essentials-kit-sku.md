# Home Essentials Kit

**Category:** Kits

**Type:** Kit with diffuser

## Brief Description

👑 Expanded version of the starter kit featuring full-size oil bottles
👑 Combines 6 single oils and 4 blends (15 mL each) with the Laluz® diffuser
👑 The most cost-effective way to equip your home with a complete arsenal of essential oils

## Contents & Key Ingredients

▫️ Lavender, Lemon, Peppermint, Tea Tree, Oregano, Frankincense — single oils, 15 mL
▫️ Deep Blue® — 5 mL
▫️ dōTERRA Breathe®, DigestZen®, dōTERRA On Guard® — blends, 15 mL
▫️ Laluz® Diffuser

## Indications

🔹 Comprehensive provision of essential oils in large volumes for the home
🔹 Diffusion and daily use without the need to restock in the near future

## Directions for Use

🔹 Diffuse chosen oil in the Laluz Diffuser, apply oils topically or internally according to the label

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Healthy Start Kit — a more compact version featuring 5 mL bottles and a Pebble diffuser

## Interesting Facts

👑 15 mL bottles contain three times more oil than the standard 5 mL starter kit — more economical in the long run

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
