# Citrus Bliss — Invigorating Blend

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Blend (Invigorating Blend)


---

## Short Description

👑 An invigorating citrus blend that uplifts the mood and refreshes the air
👑 Fresh, sunny aroma — Wild Orange, Lemon, Grapefruit, Bergamot, Lime, Red and Green Mandarin with notes of Vanilla
👑 Rich in limonene — a natural component with cleansing properties, effective even as a surface cleaner

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Wild Orange (Citrus sinensis), Lemon (Citrus limon), Grapefruit (Citrus paradisi), Bergamot (Citrus bergamia), Lime (Citrus aurantifolia), Red and Green Mandarin (Citrus reticulata), with notes of Vanilla Absolute
▫️ Red Mandarin originates from Brazil and contains 65–75% limonene; Green Mandarin is cold-pressed from unripe fruit; Lime is an effective natural cleanser

## Indications

🔹 Morning energy boost: applying to the feet in the morning helps get ready for the day
🔹 Air freshening: diffusing in the kitchen or bedroom neutralizes unwanted odors
🔹 Concentration and focus: topical application helps maintain focus during work or study
🔹 Natural cleanliness: diluted with water, the blend effectively cleans kitchen surfaces
🔹 Positive mood: diffusion creates a cheerful, warm atmosphere at home

## Directions for Use

🔹 Add 3–4 drops to a diffuser for a positive atmosphere at home or in the office, or to neutralize unwanted odors
🔹 Apply a drop to wrists and rub for an everyday personal fragrance; add 1–2 drops to dryer sheets for fresh-scented laundry
🔹 Dilute with a carrier oil for an invigorating hand massage
🔹 Dilute with water for a natural surface cleaner (not intended for regular internal use)

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products

🔹 With Lavender and Peppermint (for example, for a focus diffuser blend: 2 drops Citrus Bliss, 2 drops Lavender, 2 drops Peppermint)
🔹 With Peppermint (for an "Invigorating Morning" diffuser blend: 3 drops Citrus Bliss, 1 drop Peppermint)

## Fun Facts

👑 Citrus Bliss is one of the few doTERRA blends that works effectively both as a perfume and as a cleaning agent
👑 High concentration of limonene makes the blend a powerful natural cleanser when mixed with water
👑 The formula combines seven different citrus fruits at once — a rarity even for citrus blends
👑 The aroma is often described as "joy in a bottle" for its cheerful, sunny character

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Avoid sunlight or UV rays for up to 12 hours after applying to skin (photosensitizing effect of citrus). Dilute with a carrier oil for sensitive skin. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician.
