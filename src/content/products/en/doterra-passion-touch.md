# dōTERRA Passion® Touch

**Category:** Blends

**Type:** Essential oil blend Touch (roll-on, 10 ml)


---

## Short Description

👑 Spicy-floral blend to restore life's passion
👑 Helps bring back feelings of excitement, creativity, and joy
👑 Can be used as a personal fragrance

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Cardamom
▫️ Cinnamon
▫️ Ginger
▫️ Clove
▫️ Sandalwood
▫️ Jasmine (absolute)
▫️ Vanilla absolute
▫️ Damiana

## Indications

🔹 Feelings of boredom, apathy, or loss of interest in life
🔹 Seeking inspiration for new endeavors
🔹 Creating a warm, passionate atmosphere

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply over the heart area
🔹 Apply to wrists to stimulate creativity and clarity of mind
🔹 Use as a personal fragrance — on the neck, wrists, décolletage

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 dōTERRA Passion® — classic (neat/undiluted) blend
🔹 Other Touch blends for emotional well-being — Motivate, Cheer

## Interesting Facts

👑 Spicy, warm aroma — a popular choice as a personal perfume
👑 Damiana was traditionally used in practices related to vital energy

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas. Avoid direct sunlight and UV rays for up to 12 hours after application.
