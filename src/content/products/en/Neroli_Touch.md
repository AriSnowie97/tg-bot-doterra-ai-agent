# Neroli Touch

**Category:** Roll-ons (Touch)

**Type:** Single Oil, Touch (10 ml roll-on)


---

## Short Description

👑 Floral, citrusy, and fresh aroma of bitter orange
👑 Soothes the skin and creates an uplifting atmosphere
👑 One of the most precious floral oils due to the labor-intensive harvesting process

## Ingredients and Key Components

▫️ Fractionated Coconut Oil
▫️ Neroli — bitter orange flower (Citrus x aurantium)

## Indications

🔹 Reducing feelings of anxiety, skin care, personal fragrance

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply to pulse points throughout the day for an uplifting mood
🔹 Use for a relaxing massage along with Lavender and Ylang Ylang
🔹 Apply to the skin for a soothing effect

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Blends well with Lavender Touch, Ylang Ylang, Marjoram

## Interesting Facts

👑 Three different oils are obtained from a single bitter orange tree: from the flowers (neroli), from the leaves (petitgrain), and from the peel (orange)
👑 The flowers are hand-picked in the spring right before distillation

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
