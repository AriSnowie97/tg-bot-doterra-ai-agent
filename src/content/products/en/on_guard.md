## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ **Wild Orange Peel** — contains limonene, a powerful antioxidant and immunostimulant.
▫️ **Clove Bud** — contains eugenol, which is a strong natural antiseptic.
▫️ **Cinnamon Bark** — contains eugenol and cinnamaldehyde, provides antimicrobial action.
▫️ **Eucalyptus Leaf** — contains 1,8-cineole, possesses expectorant and bronchodilator properties.
▫️ **Rosemary Leaf** — contains α-pinene and 1,8-cineole, provides a general strengthening effect.

🔹 **Cold and flu prevention:** Regular use during the autumn-winter season significantly reduces the frequency and duration of viral illnesses.
🔹 **ARVI / Cold support:** At the first symptoms, speeds up recovery and reduces the severity of the illness.

## Directions for Use

🔹 **Aromatic use:** Add 3–4 drops to a diffuser (especially during the autumn-winter season or when family members are sick). Inhale directly from the bottle at the first symptoms of a cold.
🔹 **Topical use:** Apply 2–3 drops diluted with a carrier oil to the bottoms of the feet morning and evening. Apply to the chest and throat at the first symptoms, or to the wrists and behind the ears for continuous protection.
🔹 **Internal use:** Take 2–3 drops in a veggie capsule or 1–2 drops in warm water with honey at the first symptoms and for prevention. Maintenance course: 2–3 weeks during the season.

### Drug Interactions:
* Eugenol (clove in the blend) may enhance the effect of anticoagulants — exercise caution when taking warfarin.
* May interact with nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs).
* When taking immunosuppressants, consult a doctor before regular internal use.

### Additional Precautions and Contraindications:
* Contains clove and cinnamon — potential irritants. Always dilute the blend with a carrier oil when applying to the skin (1:5 or more). For sensitive skin, use the Touch version.
* Avoid contact with eyes and mucous membranes.
* **Contraindications:** Pregnancy (avoid internal use and application to the abdomen due to cinnamon content); serious liver disease (eugenol content requires medical supervision); allergy to clove, cinnamon, or eucalyptus.
