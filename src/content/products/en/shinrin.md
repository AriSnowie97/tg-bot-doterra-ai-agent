# Shinrin-Yoku — Forest Bathing Blend

**Category:** Essential oils

**Type:** Blend (Forest Bathing Blend)


---

## Short Description

👑 A blend inspired by the Japanese practice of shinrin-yoku ("forest bathing") — a mindful immersion into the forest atmosphere
👑 Fresh, woody-citrus aroma (Lemon, Patchouli, Magnolia, Siberian Fir, Cypress, Cardamom, Hinoki, Lavandin, Grapefruit, Geranium, Petitgrain)
👑 Rich in terpenes, which clinical research connects to a calming effect when diffused

## Ingredients and Key Components

▫️ Siberian Fir — primary chemical component is bornyl acetate, gentle on the skin
▫️ Cypress — rich in monoterpenes, beneficial for oily skin
▫️ Ingredients: Lemon (Citrus limon), Patchouli (Pogostemon cablin), Magnolia (Michelia alba), Siberian Fir (Abies sibirica), Cardamom (Elettaria cardamomum), Cypress (Cupressus sempervirens), Hinoki (Chamaecyparis obtusa), Lavandin (Lavandula hybrida), Grapefruit (Citrus paradisi), Geranium (Pelargonium graveolens), Petitgrain (Citrus limon, twigs and leaves)

## Indications

🔹 Digital overload: helps disconnect from technology and slow down
🔹 Meditation and mindfulness: diffusion creates an atmosphere for centering practice
🔹 Calming massage: gentle effect on the skin makes the blend comfortable for massage
🔹 Easy breathing: Siberian Fir supports feelings of clear breathing

## Directions for Use

🔹 Diffuse for a practice of slowing down and focusing inspired by Japanese tradition, or inhale directly from the bottle for instant grounding
🔹 Apply to wrists or neck for a personal forest aroma (Touch version is ready to use) or use during a calming massage

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Morning mindfulness: 3 drops Shinrin-Yoku, 1 drop Grapefruit
🔹 Evening slowdown: 3 drops Shinrin-Yoku, 1 drop Lavender

## Fun Facts

👑 Clinical research shows: diffusing terpene-rich essential oils promotes a calming atmosphere when slowing down and focusing
👑 The practice of shinrin-yoku literally translates to "bathing in the forest atmosphere" and has been studied by researchers in Japan for decades (originating in the early 1980s)

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas. Dilute with a carrier oil before applying to skin. Not recommended for internal use.
