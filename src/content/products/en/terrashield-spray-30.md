# TerraShield® Spray (30 ml)

**Category:** Blends

**Type:** Repellent Spray

## Short Description

👑 Natural insect repellent in a convenient spray format
👑 Registered as a repellent in accordance with US EPA requirements
👑 Safe for the whole family when used as directed

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Citronella
▫️ Lemongrass
▫️ Thyme
▫️ Cedarwood
▫️ Geranium
▫️ Peppermint
▫️ Sesame Seed Oil base

## Indications

🔹 Protection against mosquitoes and other insects outdoors
🔹 Outdoor activities — hiking, picnics, camping

## Directions for Use

🔹 Spray on arms, legs, and neck before going outdoors
🔹 Reapply as needed during outdoor activities

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 TerraShield® (15 ml) — the same formula in a bottle with a dropper cap

## Interesting Facts

👑 Demonstrated over 90% efficacy for more than four hours in relevant testing
👑 The scent masks human odors that attract insects

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. In case of eye contact, rinse thoroughly with water.
