# Blue Lotus Touch

**Category:** Roll-ons (Touch)

**Type:** Single oil, Touch (10 mL roll-on)


---

## Brief Description

👑 A rare lotus floral extract, known in Egyptian culture and art
👑 Calming, slightly sweet scent for meditation and massage
👑 Natural skin moisturizer thanks to squalene

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Fractionated Coconut Oil
▫️ Blue Lotus Extract (Nymphaea caerulea)

## Indications

🔹 For meditation, massage, and regular skin hydration

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply to wrists and neck for a calming fragrance
🔹 Use during meditation or yoga
🔹 Apply as part of your morning or evening skin care routine

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products

🔹 Pairs well with Jasmine Touch, Magnolia Touch, and Lavender Touch to create a personal fragrance

## Fun Facts

👑 Blue lotus flowers were placed on the body of Pharaoh Tutankhamun prior to burial
👑 Obtained through solvent extraction (absolute) rather than steam distillation

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
