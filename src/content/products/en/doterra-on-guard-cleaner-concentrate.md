# dōTERRA On Guard® Cleaner Concentrate

**Category:** Household Products

**Type:** Cleaner Concentrate


---

## Short Description

👑 Multi-purpose natural concentrate for home cleaning
👑 Effectively cleans and eliminates odors without toxic chemicals
👑 Biodegradable, non-toxic formula

## Ingredients and Key Ingredients

▫️ On Guard® Essential Oil Blend (Wild Orange, Clove, Cinnamon, Eucalyptus, Rosemary)
▫️ Plant-based surfactants
▫️ Preservative-free

## Indications

🔹 Cleaning the kitchen, bathroom, and other areas
🔹 Eliminating household odors

## Directions for Use

🔹 Multi-purpose cleaning: 2 tbsp. concentrate per 24 oz of water
🔹 For bathrooms: 3 tbsp. concentrate per 24 oz of water
🔹 For dishes: 3 tbsp. concentrate per gallon of water
🔹 For tough stains: apply concentrate directly to the area and allow to soak

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 dōTERRA On Guard® Laundry Detergent — for laundry
🔹 dōTERRA On Guard® Foaming Hand Wash — for hand washing

## Interesting Facts

👑 Avoid direct application of the concentrate on hardwood floors and natural stone
👑 Plant-based formula, safe for the environment

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Use only as directed as a household product. Avoid direct application on hardwood floors and natural stone. Keep out of reach of children.
