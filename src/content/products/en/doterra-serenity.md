# doTERRA Serenity — Restful Blend

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Blend (Restful Blend)


---

## Brief Description

👑 A calming blend for quality rest and restful sleep — part of the Serenity Sleep System
👑 Warm, floral-herbal aroma — Lavender, Cedarwood, Coriander, Ylang Ylang, Marjoram, Roman Chamomile, Vetiver, Sandalwood, with notes of Tonka Bean and Vanilla
👑 Can be used during the day to reduce stress and in the evening to prepare for sleep

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Lavender (Lavandula angustifolia)
▫️ Cedarwood (Cedrus atlantica)
▫️ Ho Wood (Cinnamomum camphora ct. linalool)
▫️ Ylang Ylang (Cananga odorata)
▫️ Marjoram (Origanum majorana)
▫️ Roman Chamomile (Anthemis nobilis)
▫️ Vetiver (Vetiveria zizanioides)
▫️ Hawaiian Sandalwood (Santalum paniculatum)
▫️ Tonka Bean and Vanilla Absolute

## Indications

🔹 Bedtime preparation: evening diffusion creates an atmosphere conducive to peaceful falling asleep
🔹 Daytime stress: applying to palms and inhaling throughout the day helps reduce everyday tension
🔹 Restless children: diffusing in a child's room helps soothe the child before sleep
🔹 Relaxing bath: adding to a warm bath with Epsom salt for an evening ritual
🔹 Skin care: promotes a healthy, smooth appearance of the skin when applied topically

## Directions for Use

🔹 Add 3-4 drops to a diffuser in the bedroom before sleep, or apply a drop to palms and inhale during the day to reduce stress
🔹 Apply 1-2 drops to the bottoms of feet before sleep, to the back of the neck, or pulse points (dilute if necessary)
🔹 Add 2-3 drops to a warm bath with Epsom salt
🔹 Internal use: not recommended for internal consumption — Serenity Softgels are used for dietary sleep support

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Blends well with: Vetiver, Lavender, Roman Chamomile, Epsom salt. The blend is also part of the line: Serenity Softgels, Serenity Stick + Valerian, Serenity Combo Pack, Serenity Bath Bar

## Interesting Facts

👑 The chemical composition of the blend is close to that of pure lavender — explaining its pronounced calming effect
👑 doTERRA Serenity is the central oil of an entire "sleep system" (Serenity Sleep System), which includes the oil, the stick with valerian, and softgels
👑 The formula was updated and balanced to create an "instant" sense of calm right after application or inhalation
👑 Tonka Bean, Vanilla Absolute, Vetiver, and Hawaiian Sandalwood give the aroma a special cozy and grounding feel

Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas. Do not apply to damaged or irritated skin.
