# Veráge Toner

**Category:** Personal Care

**Type:** Facial Toner

---

## Short Description

👑 Second step of the Veráge system — tones, tightens pores, and evens out skin texture
👑 Refreshing, cooling formula for use throughout the day
👑 Prepares the skin for serum and moisturizer application

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Ylang Ylang essential oil
▫️ Coriander and Cypress essential oils
▫️ Witch hazel
▫️ Aloe vera juice

## Indications

🔹 Enlarged pores and uneven skin texture
🔹 Need for a refreshing addition to your routine throughout the day

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply to face with a cotton pad after cleansing, morning and evening
🔹 Can be used as a refreshing mist throughout the day

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Veráge Cleanser — previous step
🔹 Veráge Immortelle Hydrating Serum — next step

## Fun Facts

👑 Coriander and Cypress are known for their toning and rejuvenating properties for the skin

## Cautions

> ⚠️ For external use only. Avoid direct contact with eyes. Discontinue use if skin irritation occurs. Keep out of reach of children.
