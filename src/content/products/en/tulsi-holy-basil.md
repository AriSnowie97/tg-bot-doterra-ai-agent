# Tulsi (Holy Basil)

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Essential Oil (single, 5 ml)


---

## Short Description

👑 Sacred Indian herb — the "Queen of Herbs" of Ayurvedic tradition  
👑 Promotes a clean, healthy appearance of the skin  
👑 Important part of oral hygiene  

## Composition & Key Ingredients

▫️ Ocimum sanctum (tulsi, holy basil) essential oil

## Indications

🔹 Creating a calming atmosphere for meditation or yoga  
🔹 Skincare  
🔹 Oral hygiene  

## Directions for Use

🔹 Diffuse for a meditative atmosphere  
🔹 Add a drop to your moisturizer  
🔹 Mouth rinse: 1 drop in 60 ml of water  

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products

🔹 Basil — a related plant with a different aromatic profile  

## Fun Facts

👑 The Sanskrit word "tulsi" means "incomparable"  
👑 Traditionally grown as a sacred plant in Hindu homes and temples  

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
