# doTERRA Align — Centering Blend

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Blend (Centering Blend, part of the Yoga Collection)


---

## Short Description

👑 Centering blend that invites calm confidence into your daily routine and practice
👑 Floral-citrus aroma — bergamot, coriander, marjoram, peppermint, jasmine, rose
👑 Ready to use in a roller bottle without additional dilution

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Composition: Bergamot (Citrus bergamia), Coriander (Coriandrum sativum), Marjoram (Origanum majorana), Peppermint (Mentha piperita), Jasmine (Jasminum grandiflorum, absolute), Rose (Rosa damascena)
▫️ Bergamot is unique among citrus fruits — capable of simultaneously invigorating and calming
▫️ Jasmine and rose are among the most precious floral absolutes in perfumery, adding depth and luxury to the aroma
▫️ Coriander is traditionally used for mental clarity and energetic balance

## Indications

🔹 Centering before active poses: supports balance and focus during dynamic practice
🔹 Trust in your own path: helps stay open to opportunities instead of doubts
🔹 Harmony in daily routine: regular use supports inner balance
🔹 Focus: peppermint and coriander add mental clarity

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply to the heart area, wrists, and back of the neck for a supportive, focused aroma
🔹 Apply to diffusion jewelry before practice or throughout the day, or inhale directly from the bottle for instant centering
🔹 Combine with Warrior II, Triangle, and Gate poses
🔹 Not recommended for internal use — the blend is intended for aromatic and topical application

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Together with Anchor and Arise, it forms the complete concept of practice — from grounding through centering to inspiration
🔹 Diffuser blends: Centering: 3 drops Align; Dynamic practice: 2 drops Align, 1 drop Peppermint

## Interesting Facts

👑 Align is the central blend of the Yoga Collection, specifically developed for balance between activity and calm
👑 The company recommends pairing the blend with active poses — Warrior II, Triangle, Gate
👑 The combination of citrus, herbal, and floral notes is a rare balance of lightness and depth at the same time
