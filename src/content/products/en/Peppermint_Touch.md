# Peppermint Touch

**Category:** Roll-ons (Touch)

**Type:** Single oil, Touch (10 ml roll-on)

## Short Description

👑 Minty-fresh aroma in a convenient pre-diluted format
👑 Creates a cooling effect on the skin
👑 Naturally repels insects

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Fractionated Coconut Oil
▫️ Peppermint (Mentha piperita)

## Indications

🔹 Morning boost, cooling after physical exertion, outdoor walks

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply to temples in the morning for a fresh feeling
🔹 Massage onto the back of the neck for a cooling effect
🔹 Apply to skin before going outdoors

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Pairs well with Lavender and Frankincense for a relaxing massage

## Interesting Facts

👑 Peppermint is a hybrid of watermint and spearmint
👑 One of doTERRA's best-selling oils

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
