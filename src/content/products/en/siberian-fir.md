# Siberian Fir

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Essential Oil (Single, 15 mL)


---

## Short Description

👑 Woody oil — classic coniferous forest aroma
👑 Stimulates the senses and reduces stress during difficult times
👑 Soothing comfort after physical exertion

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Abies sibirica (Siberian fir) essential oil

## Indications

🔹 Stress reduction in difficult situations
🔹 Post-workout or massage comfort

## Directions for Use

🔹 Diffuse for a winter, forest atmosphere
🔹 Apply topically after a workout

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Shinrin-Yoku® — contains Siberian Fir

## Fun Facts

👑 Aroma resembles a classic Christmas tree

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
