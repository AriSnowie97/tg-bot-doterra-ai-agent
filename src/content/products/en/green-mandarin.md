# Green Mandarin

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Essential Oil (Single, 15 ml)


---

## Short Description

👑 Unique citrus oil with floral notes
👑 Supports healthy digestive, immune, and nervous systems
👑 Refreshes and energizes the space when diffused

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Citrus reticulata essential oil (unripe mandarin peel)

## Indications

🔹 Support for digestive and immune systems
🔹 Soothing the nervous system
🔹 Refreshing and purifying the air

## Directions for Use

🔹 Diffusion: 3-4 drops in a diffuser
🔹 Internal use: 1-2 drops in a veggie capsule
🔹 Add to toothpaste for a bright smile

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Red Mandarin — the same fruit, but from the ripe fruit

## Interesting Facts

👑 Pressed from the peel of the unripe fruit of the mandarin tree, unlike Red Mandarin

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas. Avoid direct sunlight or UV rays for up to 12 hours after applying product.
