# Jasmine Touch

**Category:** Roll-ons (Touch)

**Type:** Touch Essential Oil (roll-on, 10 ml)


---

## Short Description

👑 Luxurious floral roll-on with Jasmine Absolute
👑 Promotes a healthy, radiant skin appearance
👑 Romantic, uplifting personal fragrance

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Fractionated Coconut Oil
▫️ Jasmine Absolute (Jasminum grandiflorum)

## Indications

🔹 Personal perfume throughout the day
🔹 Facial skin care against imperfections
🔹 Soothing massage

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply to wrists and neck as a fragrance
🔹 Spot apply to skin imperfections twice a day
🔹 Does not require dilution

## Compatibility with other doTERRA products

🔹 Jasmine (Absolute) — classic version

## Fun Facts

👑 Jasmine has long been called the "King of Flowers" due to its prized fragrance in perfumery

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.

## From Literature & Reference Guides

- **Emotional Profile ('Emotions & Essential Oils'):** Gently supports emotional vulnerability and self-trust; nurtures tenderness and balance.
- **Safety & Practical Notes:** 'Aromatherapy for Mother & Child' & 'Aromalogy': avoid pure oil during pregnancy (outside labor support); caution with hypotension; always dilute (at least 1:5) and use topically only with GI ulcers or kidney concerns.
