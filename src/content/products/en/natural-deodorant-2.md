# Natural Sensitive Deodorant infused with Douglas Fir & Greek Orange

**Category:** Personal Care

**Type:** Sensitive Skin Deodorant

## Short Description

👑 Baking soda-free sensitive skin deodorant
👑 Douglas fir and Greek orange scent
👑 Magnesium instead of baking soda for a gentler neutralizing effect

## Ingredients & Key Ingredients

▫️ Douglas fir and Greek wild orange essential oils
▫️ Magnesium — gently neutralizes odor
▫️ Tapioca starch — absorbs moisture

## Indications

🔹 Sensitive skin prone to irritation from baking soda
🔹 Need for a gentler deodorant formula

## Directions for Use

🔹 Apply to clean, dry underarm skin
🔹 Do not apply to broken skin

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Natural Deodorant infused with dōTERRA Balance — baking soda version for regular skin

## Interesting Facts

👑 The formula was specially developed without baking soda for people with sensitive skin

## Cautions

> ⚠️ For external use only. Avoid direct contact with eyes. If skin irritation occurs, discontinue use. Keep out of reach of children.
