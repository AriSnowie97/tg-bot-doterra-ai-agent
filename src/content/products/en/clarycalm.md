# ClaryCalm — Monthly Blend for Women

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Blend (Monthly Blend, specially formulated for women)


---

## Short Description

👑 A soothing blend to balance mood and cool the skin during the monthly cycle
👑 Floral-herbaceous aroma — Clary Sage, Lavender, Bergamot, Roman Chamomile, Cedarwood, Ylang Ylang, Geranium, Fennel, Carrot Seed, Palmarosa, Vitex
👑 Ready to use in a roll-on bottle without additional dilution

## Ingredients and Key Components

▫️ Clary Sage (Salvia sclarea), Lavender (Lavandula angustifolia), Bergamot (Citrus bergamia), Roman Chamomile (Anthemis nobilis), Ylang Ylang (Cananga odorata), Cedarwood (Juniperus virginiana), Geranium (Pelargonium graveolens), Fennel (Foeniculum vulgare), Carrot Seed (Daucus carota), Palmarosa (Cymbopogon martini), Vitex (Vitex agnus-castus)
👑 The primary chemical component of Clary Sage is linalyl acetate, an ester, which is one of the most relaxing aromatic compounds
👑 Bergamot is unique among citrus fruits — capable of simultaneously uplifting and calming; in Greece, unripe fruits are traditionally eaten as a dessert
👑 Vitex has long been known in many cultures as a "women's herb" — its connection to female balance is embedded in the very history of the plant

## Indications

🔹 Monthly cycle: soothes and balances mood throughout the month
🔹 Feelings of excess heat: cooling effect when applied to the skin during moments of heat
🔹 Self-care ritual: abdominal massage with a warm heating pad as part of regular self-care
🔹 Emotional instability: helps balance heightened emotions during sensitive periods

## Directions for Use

🔹 Diffuse for a soothing aroma that helps balance heightened emotions
🔹 Apply to the lower abdomen during your cycle for a soothing massage
🔹 Apply to the back of the neck, temples, or bottoms of feet for a calming aroma
🔹 Apply to the lower abdomen and place a warm heating pad as part of a self-care ritual
🔹 Apply to the back of the neck during moments of excess heat for a cooling effect
🔹 Internal use: not recommended for internal use — the blend is intended for aromatic and topical application

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Can be combined with: Calming — 3 drops of ClaryCalm in a diffuser alone; Gentle Balance — 2 drops of ClaryCalm, 1 drop of Lavender

## Interesting Facts

👑 ClaryCalm is one of the few doTERRA blends formulated specifically and exclusively for women
👑 The Bergamot used is FCF (furanocoumarin-free), which does not cause increased sun sensitivity, unlike regular bergamot
👑 The roll-on bottle format makes the blend convenient for quick application throughout the day without needing a diffuser
