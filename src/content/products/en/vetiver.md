# Vetiver

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Essential Oil (Single, 15 ml)


---

## Short Description

👑 Earthy oil with a grounding effect on emotions
👑 Supports the immune system when used internally
👑 "Fragrance of the soil" — deep and calming

## Composition and Key Ingredients

▫️ Vetiveria zizanioides (vetiver) essential oil

## Indications

🔹 Grounding and stabilizing emotions
🔹 Immune system support

## Directions for Use

🔹 Diffuse before bedtime for grounding
🔹 Internal use: 1 drop in a capsule

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Vetiver Touch — roll-on version
🔹 Peace® — contains vetiver

## Interesting Facts

👑 Sourced from the roots of grass that can reach up to 4 meters deep into the soil

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor's care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas.
