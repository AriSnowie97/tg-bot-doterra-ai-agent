# Veráge Toner

**Category:** Skin & Hair Care → Veráge Skin Care

**Type:** Facial Toner Mist


---

## Short Description

👑 Refreshing facial toner mist that balances skin pH and tightens pores
👑 Enriched with CPTG Ylang Ylang, Palmarosa, Cypress, and Coriander essential oils
👑 Hydrates, revitalizes, and primes the skin for serums and moisturizers

## Ingredients & Key Components

▫️ Ylang Ylang essential oil (soothing and balancing)
▫️ Coriander essential oil (rejuvenating and toning)
▫️ Cypress essential oil (pore-refining and clarifying)
▫️ Palmarosa essential oil (cellular regeneration and hydration)
▫️ Witch Hazel extract (calming astringent and antioxidant)
▫️ Aloe Vera juice (deep cooling hydration)

## Indications

🔹 Toning and rebalancing skin after cleansing
🔹 Hydration boost during dry days, flights, or post-workout
🔹 Refining visible pores and balancing skin moisture

## How to Use

🔹 Close eyes and mist lightly over clean face and neck from 15–20 cm away
🔹 Alternatively, spray onto a cotton pad and smooth gently across skin
🔹 Use morning and evening before Veráge Immortelle Serum
🔹 Re-apply throughout the day for an invigorating skin refresh

## Complementary doTERRA Products

🔹 Veráge Cleanser — step one facial wash
🔹 Veráge Immortelle Hydrating Serum — applied to damp skin right after toning
🔹 Veráge Moisturizer — step four sealing hydration

## Interesting Facts

👑 Natural witch hazel and aloe calm redness and inflammation, suitable even for sensitive skin
👑 100% alcohol-free formula preserves skin barrier without tightness or dryness

## Precautions

> ⚠️ For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes. Discontinue use if irritation occurs. Keep out of reach of children.
