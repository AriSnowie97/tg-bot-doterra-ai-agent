# Adaptiv — Calming Blend

**Category:** Essential Oils

**Type:** Blend (Calming Blend)


---

## Short Description

👑 A calming blend that helps adapt to new circumstances, stress, and tension
👑 Sweet, citrus-floral aroma (Wild Orange, Lavender, Copaiba, Spearmint, Magnolia, Rosemary, Neroli, Sweetgum)
👑 Combines calming and uplifting oils simultaneously — for "calm alertness" rather than drowsiness

## Ingredients and Key Components

▫️ Wild Orange (Citrus sinensis)
▫️ Lavender (Lavandula angustifolia)
▫️ Copaiba (Copaifera reticulata)
▫️ Spearmint (Mentha spicata)
▫️ Magnolia (Magnolia grandiflora)
▫️ Rosemary (Rosmarinus officinalis)
▫️ Neroli (Citrus aurantium)
▫️ Sweetgum (Liquidambar styraciflua)

## Indications

🔹 New environment or situation: Helps adapt during a move, a new job, or in an unfamiliar place
🔹 Important event: Use before an exam, meeting, or presentation to soothe nerves
🔹 Fatigue and restlessness: Balances the body when feeling tired or internally restless
🔹 Indecisiveness and irritability: Helps find balance during emotional instability

## Directions for Use

🔹 Diffuse during a move, job change, or in an unfamiliar place for adaptation, or during meditation and relaxation
🔹 Mix 3-4 drops with Epsom salt and add to a bath for a relaxing soak; dilute with a carrier oil for a soothing massage; apply to wrists or neck (Touch version)
🔹 Diffuser Blends:
- New Beginning: 3 drops Adaptiv
- Before an Important Event: 3 drops Adaptiv, 1 drop Wild Orange
- Calm Focus: 3 drops Adaptiv, 1 drop Rosemary
🔹 Internal use: Not recommended for internal use of the oil — use Adaptiv Calming Blend Capsules for dietary support

## Compatibility with Other doTERRA Products

🔹 Pairs well with Wild Orange, Rosemary
🔹 Recommended for use alongside Adaptiv Calming Blend Capsules and Adaptiv Touch for a synergistic effect and to build a complete "mental wellness system"

## Interesting Facts

👑 Adaptiv is one of the few doTERRA blends that simultaneously calms and uplifts, rather than acting in only one direction
👑 Formed the basis of an entire "mental wellness system" — oil, Touch version, and capsules for internal use

## Cautions

> ⚠️ Possible skin sensitivity. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under a doctor’s care, consult your physician. Avoid contact with eyes, inner ears, and sensitive areas. Do not apply to broken or irritated skin.

▫️ Calming and uplifting at the same time

👑 Emotional Effects:

👑 Origin and Research:
Research shows that the aroma of lavender, a key component of the blend, promotes an environment that supports sustained attention. Magnolia, neroli, and sweetgum provide anti-stress effects, wild orange and spearmint uplift, while copaiba and rosemary soothe anxious feelings.

Adaptiv is unique in that it creates a state of "calm alertness," which is especially useful when you need to remain productive despite stress. A bath with Epsom salt and a few drops of Adaptiv is a popular way to end a stressful day.
