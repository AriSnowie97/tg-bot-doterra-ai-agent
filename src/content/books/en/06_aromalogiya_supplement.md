# Knowledge Base: Additions from "Aromalogy" (Svetlana Mirgorodskaya)

**Source:** "Aromalogy", S. Mirgorodskaya — a dedicated authorial volume on clinical and holistic aromatherapy.
**⚠️ Copyright:** Published commercial book. The following is a structured condensation of principles (dosage rules, contraindications per oil), avoiding direct verbatim reproduction.
**Significance:** A systematic framework where safety precautions are organized both by user health risk factors and by individual oil profiles.

---

## 1. General Principles of Dosage and Application

- **Pediatric Dosage Formula:** 0.12 × standard adult dose × child's age (in years), rounded normally. Example: 0.12 × 5 drops × 3 years ≈ 2 drops diluted in carrier oil.
- **Elderly and Pregnant Individuals:** dosage is halved compared to typical healthy adult doses.
- **The "21 Days On / 7–14 Days Off" Rule:** most essential oils should not be used continuously for longer than 3 consecutive weeks without a rest interval, preventing receptor fatigue and sensitization.
- **Internal Intake:** carries elevated liability and should be limited to specific, short-term wellness protocols under guidance. The bot refers targeted internal dosage inquiries to Nataliia Kotelianska.
- **Synergistic Blends:** 2 to 7 oils represent the optimal range for home blends.
- **3-Day Sensitivity Patch Test:**
  - Day 1: aromatic inhalation from a handkerchief several times during the day.
  - Day 2: apply a single drop of diluted oil (1:5 in carrier oil) to the inner wrist or bend of the elbow.
  - Day 3: warm 5-minute foot soak or bath with 1–2 drops thoroughly dispersed in Epsom salt.
  - If no nasal congestion, burning eyes, skin erythema, or nausea occurs across the 3 days, individual tolerance is confirmed.

## 2. Health Risk Factors & Specific Contraindications

| Risk Factor | Restricted / Contraindicated Essential Oils |
|---|---|
| Epilepsy / Seizure Susceptibility | Most stimulating oils contraindicated. Mild exceptions: Lavender, Wild Orange, Lemon. Clinical conditions require specialist medical oversight. |
| Muscle Spasm Proneness | Hyssop, Rosemary, Nutmeg |
| Pregnancy (General Rule) | See the conservative permissive list in Section 3 below |
| Phototoxicity | Wild Orange, Grapefruit, Litsea, Lime, Lemon, Mandarin, Petitgrain, Rosemary, Thyme — do not expose treated skin to UV rays for at least 12 hours |
| GI Mucosal Sensitivity | Anise, Bay, Verbena, Geranium, Oregano, Jasmine, Ginger, Cedarwood, Pine, Patchouli, Palmarosa, Fir, Citronella, Thyme — avoid on an empty stomach; mix with fats/food |
| Kidney Pathology | Spruce, Jasmine (external only), Juniper, Fir, Sandalwood |
| Bleeding Disorders / Anticoagulants | Anise, Basil, Hyssop, Cypress, Geranium, Wintergreen, Birch |
| Severe Hypertension | Bay, Clove, Oregano, Ginger, Cinnamon, Juniper, Nutmeg, Rosemary, Citronella, Thyme, Sage |
| Hypotension (Low Blood Pressure) | Valerian, Jasmine |
| Insomnia (Avoid in late evening) | Nutmeg, Sage, Citronella, Thyme |
| Nervous Exhaustion | Clove, Cedarwood, Cinnamon, Nutmeg, Citronella, Sage |
| Incompatible with Alcohol | Cedarwood, Thyme |
| Anemia / Iron & Iodine Therapies | Lavender |
| Homeopathic Remedies | Peppermint, Roman Chamomile |
| Children under 7 | Peppermint (avoid applying near the face or chest) |
| Children under 3 | Tea Tree (use extreme dilution only under guidance) |

## 3. Cautiously Permitted Oils During Pregnancy

Wild Orange, Bergamot, Geranium (cautiously), Jasmine (cautiously), Cajeput, Lavender (cautiously), Frankincense (cautiously), Lime, Lemon, Peppermint (cautiously), Niaouli, Neroli, Petitgrain, Rose, Roman Chamomile (cautiously), Pine (cautiously), Tea Tree, Eucalyptus.  
*All usage during pregnancy requires primary physician validation.*

## 4. First Aid for Essential Oil Mishaps

- **Oil in Eyes:** flush immediately with clean pure vegetable or carrier oil (such as coconut or olive oil), NEVER water, as water drives essential oils deeper into mucous membranes. Follow with gentle warm water rinse.
- **Skin Irritation or Burning:** apply generous amounts of carrier oil (Fractionated Coconut Oil) directly to the affected skin to dilute the concentration.
- **Heartburn or Stomach Burning after Ingestion:** drink whole milk, yogurt, or rich plant-based milk.
- **Key Principle:** essential oils are lipophilic and hydrophobic. Carrier fats dilute them; water spreads them.
