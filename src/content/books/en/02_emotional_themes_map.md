## Source Classification Structure

The book provides a two-way index: users can identify recommended oils based on negative emotional states, and vice versa. Each major category is broken down into specific nuances of emotion.

## Application in Bot Dialogue

This map is activated when the user specifies emotional concerns. The bot asks 1–2 gentle clarifying questions ("Does this feel closer to stress, anxiety, sadness, or fatigue?") and selects 2–3 suitable oils with full safety disclaimers.
