## 1. Useful and Safe Foundations: Essential Oil Application Principles

General educational concepts:

- **Diffusion (Aromatic):** evaporation using ultrasonic waves or gentle heat; aroma molecules interact with the olfactory system within seconds. For beginners, start with 1–2 drops.
- **Topical Application:** place a drop in the palm and dilute with carrier oil (fractionated coconut oil, jojoba, sweet almond) — essential oils do not mix with water, only with lipids.
- **Rollers:** pre-diluted roller bottles with carrier oil — convenient for portable and frequent application.
- **Massage:** light to moderate pressure; do not perform prenatal massage without certified specialized training.
- **Compresses:** a drop of oil on a warm or cold damp cloth.
- **Baths:** essential oils do not disperse in water directly — mix into Epsom salt or carrier oil first before adding to bath water.
- **Culinary Use:** oils as flavor enhancers (1 drop ≈ 1 tsp citrus zest/mint leaves, 1 drop ≈ 1 tbsp ground cinnamon).
- **Foot Reflexology:** reflex maps serve as supportive wellness routines, never as standalone medical therapy.

## 2. Safety Icon Classification System

| Icon / Indicator | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Neat application permitted | Does not require carrier oil dilution for healthy adult skin |
| Dilution advised for sensitive/child skin | Mild caution advised |
| Dilution strongly required | Must always be diluted in carrier oil prior to skin contact |
| Aromatic use suitable | Safe for ultrasonic diffuser dispersion |
| Avoid direct vapor inhalation | May irritate respiratory tract if inhaled undiluted directly from opening |
| Internal use safe for adults; ages 6+ only diluted/micro-dose; under 6 do not ingest | Crucial guideline for child intake safety |
| Household cleaning suitable | Excellent for natural surface cleaning |
| ☀️ Avoid direct sun for 12 hours | Phototoxicity risk (citrus, bergapten-containing oils) |
| ☀️☀️ Avoid direct sun for 72 hours | High phototoxicity (e.g. cold-pressed bergamot) |

Oils noted as irritating upon direct dry inhalation from diffuser openings: **Clove, Cinnamon, Oregano, Thyme**.

## 4. ⚠️ CRITICAL SAFETY NOTICE: What Must Never Be Recommended by the Bot

Unlike basic lifestyle wellness guides, some traditional index references pair oils directly with acute, critical medical conditions:

- Epilepsy, convulsions, seizures
- Stroke, aneurysm, ALS, Multiple Sclerosis
- Parkinson's disease, Alzheimer's disease, Huntington's disease
- Diabetes, severe thyroid diseases (Hashimoto's, Graves', Addison's, Cushing's)
- HIV/AIDS
- Autism, ADD/ADHD clinical diagnoses
- Leukemia, malignant neoplasms / oncology
- Traumatic brain injury, coma, concussions
- Sepsis, tuberculosis, malaria, whooping cough
- Venomous snake and insect/spider bites

**Why this is an absolute red flag:**
Matching oils to critical pathology violates our medical safety boundaries in `03_intake_flow_and_routing.md`. The bot must NEVER suggest oils as treatments for serious illnesses, and must immediately route any such inquiry to a medical professional and Nataliia Kotelianska.
