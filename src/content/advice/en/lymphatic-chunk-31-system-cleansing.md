# Lymphatic system: how it works and how to support it (chunk 31)

## How lymph moves

Blood is driven by the heart. The lymphatic system has no such pump — lymphatic vessels contract on their own and push lymph from bottom to top (never the other way around). Reaching the "top," lymph flows into the bloodstream and returns downward together with the blood.

The lymphatic system is one of the body's cleansing systems: it removes metabolic waste products from the body. Immunity depends on it.

## Lymph nodes

Dilated lymphatic vessels made of bean-shaped lymphoid tissue are the most important part of the immune system. Lymphocytes "grow" in them, and toxins, viruses, fungi, and cancer cells are trapped in them. During lymph stasis, "trapped" pathogens accumulate in the lymph nodes.

## What is lymph

Lymph is intercellular fluid: it bathes cells, delivers necessary substances to them, and takes away waste products and toxins. Unlike the circulatory system, the lymphatic system is not closed. The fluid passes through the lymph nodes (where it is partially neutralized) and then enters the liver through the circulatory system, where neutralization is completed. Lymph is sometimes called "serous fluid" — the clear fluid from a wound that protects it from bacteria.

## Methods for supporting lymph flow

- **Hydration regime:** gradually bring to 30–40 ml of water per 1 kg of body weight — lymph becomes more fluid, bile becomes thinner, and its outflow improves.
- **Intestinal barrier:** restoration of the mucosa and microflora — diet, pre- and probiotics, herbs and supplements if needed.
- **Sufficient protein intake** — for high-quality collagen of the valve apparatus of lymphatic vessels.
- **Fiber** (greens, berries, vegetables) — good peristalsis acts as a natural self-massage of lymph nodes and abdominal vessels.
- **Breathing exercises and morning vacuum** — for the same reason.
- **Movement** — muscles act as a "pump" for lymph (there is no lymph heart). Physical activity can accelerate lymph drainage significantly. Moderate daily activity is better than rare intense workouts — the habit itself is what matters.
- **Contrast shower** — start with the legs, gradually (over 2–3 months) move to the whole body; ice-cold water is not required.
- **Bathhouse/sauna** — 2–3 times a month, no higher than 65–70°C.
- **Vitamin support and enzyme therapy** (up to 6 months, between meals) — in correct forms and doses selected by a specialist.
- **Lymphatic drainage massage** — in the direction of lymph movement (from the fingertips and toes toward the center of the body). ⚠️ The source explicitly warns: without prior preparation of the elimination organs, such massage can be dangerous.
- **Herbal teas/mixtures** — enhance lymph drainage, but some have clear contraindications; consultation with a nutritionist/doctor is recommended.
