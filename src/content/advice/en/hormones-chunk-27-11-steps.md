# How to Balance Hormones Without Pills: 11 Steps (Chunk 27)

## Step 1 — Diagnostics
Identify problems with specific hormones based on complaints/symptoms, perform a hormonal check-up (FSH, LH, DHEA, estradiol, free testosterone, prolactin, SHBG, etc.) by consulting an integrative specialist for prescription and interpretation.

## Step 2 — Nutrition
Less fast food, refined foods, sugar, glycation. More vegetables, whole protein, healthy fats, and slow carbohydrates. Increase fluid intake.

## Step 3 — Caffeine
Reduce consumption of coffee and caffeinated beverages (black and green tea), replacing them with herbal teas/chicory. Caffeine can negatively affect the adrenal glands, disrupt estrogen detoxification, and lower estradiol levels in perimenopausal women.

## Step 4 — Circadian Rhythms
Fall asleep before 11:00 PM. Low melatonin in women is linked to an increased risk of endometrial cancer (estrogen-dependent cancer). In men, sleeping less than 5 hours per day reduces testosterone levels by 10–15%.

## Step 5 — Activity
10,000–12,000 steps a day + regular workouts.

## Step 6 — Stress Management
Meditation, working with a psychologist, relaxation, diaphragmatic breathing, essential oils, magnesium baths. In women, stress is linked to anovulation, amenorrhea, cycle disorders, elevated prolactin, insulin resistance; in men — to decreased testosterone and libido.

## Step 7 — Liver and Bile Flow
Bile stasis is considered one of the factors in the accumulation of estrogen metabolites and estrogen dominance, which is associated with the formation of polyps, fibroids, cysts, and endometriosis.

## Step 8 — Gut
Balanced diet + fiber (dietary fiber for the microbiome) + fermented foods (kombucha, fermented vegetables, yogurt, kefir, kimchi) + exclusion of simple sugars.

## Step 9 — Body Composition
Reducing body fat through clean eating and increasing muscle mass is linked to reducing toxic load, inflammation, estrogen levels, and increasing testosterone.

## Step 10 — Toxic Load
Remove non-eco-friendly detergents, excess plastic, alcohol, smoking. Choose eco-friendly cosmetics and household chemicals.

## Step 11 — Information Detox
Periodically take breaks from information noise — spend time with loved ones, favorite activities.
