# 80/20 Nutrition and a Comprehensive Approach to Health (chunk 25)

## Core Principle

The 80/20 rule — without extremes and stress. The foundation of health improvement is a properly composed plate.

## What is Strictly Excluded

- **Sugar** — minimize sugar-containing foods to restore basic GI tract functions.
- **Gluten** — all derivatives of wheat: pasta, couscous, bulgur, oatmeal, rye, barley, pearl barley, bakery products, sauces, mayonnaise, sausages, convenience foods.
- **Dairy products** — cheese (goat or sheep) is acceptable in limited amounts (a few times a week); everything else is prohibited.
- **Refined oil** — eliminate sunflower oil forever, especially for frying (becomes a carcinogen when heated); minimize pumpkin and sesame oil.

## What to Include in the Diet

**Fish (2–3 times/week):** cod, chum salmon, pink salmon, wild trout, halibut, dorado, sea bass, mackerel (not smoked), herring — ideal sources of omega-3.

**Lightly salted fish:** wild chum salmon, pink salmon, coho salmon — to diversify breakfast.

**Seafood (2–3 times/week, in the absence of allergies):** shrimp, squid, mussels, scallops, etc.

**Limit:** Atlantic salmon, tuna, pangasius — due to the accumulation of heavy metals.

**Meat:** turkey, duck, rabbit, lean veal, chicken (grain-fed, free-range, farm).

**Offal (2–3 times/week):** turkey or rabbit liver — a source of heme iron, vitamins A, D, E, and group B.

**Fruits (recommended):** berries (raspberries, strawberries, blueberries, cherries, blackcurrants, cranberries, lingonberries), grapefruit, green apples. Up to 1 portion per day (1 portion = 1 fruit or 1 handful of berries). Consume after the main meal as a dessert — not recommended as a separate snack.

**Nuts:** pre-soaked (especially when taking iron supplements). For those who struggle with 3 meals a day — a snack of 20–30 g of nuts.
*How to soak:* overnight in filtered water, in the morning rinse and dry at a low temperature in the oven.

**Oils/Fats:** olive, sesame, avocado, walnut, ghee, butter (high-quality, 82.5% fat), grape seed, coconut.

## Comprehensive Approach to Health Improvement (11 practices)

1. **Balanced diet** — plates with proteins, fats, carbohydrates, fiber.
2. **10–12 thousand steps daily** outdoors — glucose utilization, muscle tone, endorphins.
3. **Sleep schedule:** bedtime 22:00–23:00, wake-up 6:00–7:00.
4. **Environment** — avoid "toxic" people, keep positively minded ones close.
5. **Small goals** on the way to a big goal — dopamine reinforcement.
6. **Bodywork:** morning exercises, workouts, stretching, pilates, swimming pool, running.
7. **Relaxation practices and essential oils** for sleep and mood: meditation, aroma baths, massage.
8. **Gratitude and compliments** — endorphins and oxytocin; planning short-term events — dopamine; physical contact (hugs) — oxytocin.
9. **Bathhouse/sauna** — toxin removal and serotonin production.
10. **Positive thinking**, the ability to switch from routine to rest — prevention of burnout.
11. **Allowing yourself to dream** and set goals regarding health and appearance.
