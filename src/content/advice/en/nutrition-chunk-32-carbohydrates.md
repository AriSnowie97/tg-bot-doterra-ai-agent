# Carbohydrates: Everything You Need to Know (Chunk 32)

## The Role of Carbohydrates

Carbohydrates are the main source of energy. Even to burn fats, the body needs energy, which it gets from carbohydrates. Carbohydrates can be simple or complex.

## Simple Carbohydrates

**Sources:** all sweets and sugar, fructose, fruits, honey, bakery products, pastries.

Essentially, these are refined products — the body spends minimal energy on breaking them down, but they are neither healthy nor nutritious (they do not provide a lasting feeling of fullness).

## Complex Carbohydrates

**Sources:**
- Long-cooking grains (at least 20 minutes)
- Durum wheat pasta
- Whole grain crispbreads and bread (check ingredients — if there is refined flour, these are already simple carbohydrates)

## Fiber

**Sources:** greens, vegetables, grain husks — everything that contains fibers, for the breakdown of which the body spends time and energy.

The body "eats" fiber not for itself, but for the normobiota (gut microflora) — it is the microflora that is engaged in the fermentation and "digestion" of fibers.

## The Balance Rule

To stay healthy and not provoke insulin resistance:
- **80%** of all carbohydrates in the diet — complex carbohydrates and fiber
- **20%** — simple carbohydrates

**Estimated carbohydrate intake norm:** ≈ 1.5–2 g per 1 kg of desired body weight.

---
ℹ️ **Note:** these are general guidelines, not a personalized meal plan. Carbohydrate needs are individual and depend on activity level, health status (including insulin resistance, diabetes, kidney disease) and other factors — for exact numbers, you should consult a doctor/nutritionist rather than relying solely on this formula.
