# Cooking Oils: The Complete Guide (Chunk 30)

## 🔥 For High-Heat Frying (180–230°C)

**Avocado** — up to 270°C
- Most stable, neutral flavor
- Rich in monounsaturated fats

**Refined olive (pomace)** — up to 240°C
- Stable, versatile

**Clarified butter (ghee)** — up to 250°C
- Traditional for high temperatures
- Free of milk proteins

## 🍳 For Moderate Frying, Sautéing (160–180°C)

**Extra Virgin Olive** — up to 190°C
- Best for medium temperatures
- Rich in antioxidants

**Unrefined coconut** — up to 177°C
- Stable saturated fats
- Coconut aroma

**Grapeseed** — up to 216°C
- Neutral flavor
- Rich in vitamin E

## 🥗 Raw Only (Do Not Heat!)

**Flaxseed** ⚠️
- Omega-3, very healthy
- Oxidizes quickly
- Store in the refrigerator
- Add only to ready dishes

**Walnut**
- Delicate flavor
- Rich in omega-3
- Cold dishes only

**Pumpkin seed**
- Zinc, vitamins
- Oxidizes when heated

**Pine nut**
- Immunomodulator
- Very delicate

**Unrefined sesame** — up to 177°C
- Can be used with moderate heating, but healthier raw
- Salads, Asian cuisine

## 💡 Golden Rule

The less processing (cold-pressed, unrefined) — the more health benefits, but the lower the smoke point.

---
ℹ️ **Note:** reference culinary material on oil smoke points. Temperature values may vary depending on the manufacturer and the degree of refinement of a specific bottle — you should also refer to the manufacturer's label.
